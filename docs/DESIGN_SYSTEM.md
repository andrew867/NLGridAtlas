# Visual system

## Direction

The design takes cues from technical publications, control drawings, mid-century engineering identity systems, hydroelectric machinery, and three-phase alternating current. It does not copy a utility brand or the NASA identity.

References:

- NASA Graphics Standards Manual, 1976: disciplined grids, strong scale changes, and controlled typography
- IBM Design Language technical diagrams: legible relationships, restrained decoration, and consistent line logic
- Niagara hydroelectric machinery and the Nikola Tesla memorial context: copper, water, stone, and phase-wave details

Color is never the only state indicator.

## Typography

- Barlow Condensed: titles, section numbers, equipment labels
- Source Serif 4: explanatory prose and long reading
- IBM Plex Mono: ratings, citations, dates, controls, and identifiers

The combination separates editorial explanation from equipment data. It also avoids the common all-sans software-dashboard appearance.

## Phase frame

Primary evidence panels have two sharp corners and two clipped corners. Three sine traces mark the clipped corners. The treatment refers to three-phase alternating current and to the Tesla memorial context without using a literal statue or lightning-bolt logo.

## Motion

Use motion only to show a change in state, layer visibility, or selection. Do not animate background decoration. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
