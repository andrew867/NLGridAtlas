# Rights and source attribution

## Repository material

The source code, original atlas diagrams, original written analysis and project documentation are provided under the repository's [MIT License](LICENSE), unless a file, caption or source record states otherwise.

Technical facts are cited to the records from which they were obtained. A citation does not transfer ownership of the source document to this project.

## Third-party material

Public filings, reports, archival records, photographs, maps, newspaper pages, technical manuals, logos and trademarks remain subject to the rights and terms of their creators, publishers and holding institutions. They are not relicensed under the repository's MIT License.

The atlas normally links to the original record and identifies the exact page used. Locally preserved source files are retained as research evidence. Material with an explicit reproduction restriction is cited without copying the restricted image into the published atlas. This includes the Memorial University Faculty of Medicine Founders' Archive photographs currently used as catalogue records.

## Principal source collections

The project relies extensively on records made available by:

- The Board of Commissioners of Public Utilities of Newfoundland and Labrador.
- Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro, Nalcor Energy, Churchill Falls (Labrador) Corporation Limited and their predecessor records represented in public filings.
- Newfoundland Power and its predecessor-company records represented in public filings and annual reports.
- Fortis Inc. corporate reports that document Newfoundland Power history and investment.
- Memorial University Libraries, including the Digital Archives Initiative and the Memorial University Collections catalogue.
- The Memorial University Faculty of Medicine Founders' Archive.
- McGill University Library's digitized Canadian corporate reports.
- The Government of Newfoundland and Labrador, including GeoAtlas and departmental reports.
- Government of Canada Publications, Library and Archives Canada, Statistics Canada and the Canadian Radio-television and Telecommunications Commission.
- Newspaper, trade-journal and historical collections identified in the individual source records.
- Northern Telecom and Nortel technical publications preserved by independent technical archives.

Each technical claim uses its individual source citation. This list acknowledges the collections; it does not replace those citations.

## DMS-100.net

Joe Z owns and maintains [DMS-100.net](http://www.dms-100.net/). The site preserves Northern Telecom and Nortel documentation used to verify the atlas's DMS-10 and DMS-100 equipment descriptions, including the DMS-10 Generic 602.20 documentation set. The manuals retain their original rights.

## Geographic data

Newfoundland transmission-route geometry includes Government of Newfoundland and Labrador GeoAtlas records. Station placement also uses published OpenStreetMap objects. OpenStreetMap data is available under the [Open Data Commons Open Database License](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) and is credited to OpenStreetMap contributors.
