# 1932 System Search

## Topsail hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power places the Topsail Plant in service in 1932 with one 1.2 MW turbine-generator. The same engineering report dates the reinforced-concrete intake foundation and gate conduit, steel head gate and wooden gatehouse to the original construction. The 1983 upgrade replaced the original inlet valve, turbine-generator, switchgear and controls.

The later plant records give different ratings for the replacement unit:

- The 2010 development record gives 2,300 kW under about 111.2 m net head.
- The 2020 engineering report gives 2.6 MW at 85.5 m rated net head and approximately 13.3 GWh normal annual production.

The filings do not reconcile the capacity and net-head values. They agree that the original 1932 unit was rated 1.2 MW and was replaced in 1983.

## Other 1932 records

The depreciation schedules retain 1932 original-cost vintages in several plant accounts, but the schedules do not assign those amounts to named sites. A 1955 Bell Island cable record also states that its cable supplemented three submarine cables installed in 1932; that history is already retained in the Bell Island record.

No separate exact-year transmission-station, transformer, telecommunications, load, reliability, storm or outage event was located beyond the named Topsail and Bell Island records.
