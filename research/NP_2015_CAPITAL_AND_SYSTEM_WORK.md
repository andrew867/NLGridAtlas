14L included 1.13 km of aerial line and 1.13 km of underground cable. The aerial line was built in 1950. A 0.45 km section was rebuilt in 1993, leaving 22 original single-pole structures. The filing records a January 2014 wind failure. Source: physical PDF p. 553. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 565.

15L was 4.22 km long and used 73 mainly single-pole structures. It was built in 1958. The proposed work covered the remaining 27 original structures. Source: physical PDF p. 554. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 566.

30L was 2.9 km long and used 87 single-pole structures, all with distribution underbuild. The filing identifies Canadian Ohio Brass insulators affected by cement growth. It proposed 1.5 km in 2015 and the remaining 1.4 km in 2016, with filed costs of $590,000 and $511,000 respectively. Source: physical PDF pp. 554 to 555. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 567.

The 69L construction year is inconsistent inside the application. The summary table on physical PDF p. 552 gives 1951. The project text on physical PDF p. 555 says the line was originally constructed in 1959. Both values remain in the atlas as a source conflict. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 568.

400L was 21.9 km long with 90 H-frame structures. 404L was 1.2 km long with 14 single-pole structures. Both were built in 1967. The filing rated 400L at 50 MVA but stated that conductor and splice condition reduced its available rating below 50 MVA. Source: physical PDF pp. 556 to 560. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 569.

## Feeder additions for load growth

MOB-01 served approximately 1,500 customers from Mobile north through Witless Bay and Bay Bulls. The first 3.0 km section used 4/0 AASC with a 356 A planning rating and a filed 2015 forecast of 407 A per phase. The next 2.0 km section used #4 copper with a 153 A planning rating and a forecast of 228 A per phase. The application proposed 477 ASC and 4/0 AASC replacements for $785,000. Source: 2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 646 to 647. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 653.

RBK-01 supplied Sandy Point. The application proposed converting approximately 6.0 km from single-phase to three-phase for $386,000. Source: physical PDF pp. 648 to 649.

The same program included the SCV-01 and KEN-05 projects already recorded in the atlas. The four filed projects totalled $1.684 million. Source: physical PDF p. 649.

## Generating stations

The Seal Cove filing proposed rewinding the 16 rotor poles on G1. The Allis Chalmers unit dated from 1924, its stator had been rewound in 1988, and the rotor windings were original. The plant was listed at 3.3 MW with normal annual production of 9.4 GWh. The filed cost was $156,000. Source: 2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 30 and 342 to 343.

The Tors Cove filing proposed replacing the penstock trestle with a reinforced steel penstock and refurbishing the overhead crane, turbine runner, main inlet valve, generator rotor, and power cables. The plant was commissioned in 1941 and listed at 6.5 MW. The application summary gives 25.4 GWh of normal annual production; the engineering assessment gives 25.9 GWh after the work. The filed cost was $1.777 million. Source: physical PDF pp. 28, 356 and 361.

CA-NP-001 later reported $165,000 of actual expenditure for Seal Cove against a $156,000 budget, and $2.122 million for Tors Cove against a $1.777 million budget. Source: CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 3.

## SCADA replacement

The existing SCADA system was installed in 1999 on HP Alpha servers running Tru64 and Schneider or Telvent OASyS. It replaced an Automatec system installed in 1984. The 2015 filing describes 71 substations, 25 hydro generators, two gas turbines, 187 feeders, 78 power transformers, and approximately 40,000 points. Source: 2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 783 to 785.

The proposed replacement included a historian, upgraded security, GIS and outage-management interfaces, alarm-storm suppression, encrypted ICCP communication with Hydro, and an automated operator diary. Newfoundland Power planned parallel operation during the transition. The two-year filed cost was $5.675 million, split into $2.833 million in 2015 and $2.842 million in 2016. Source: physical PDF pp. 786 to 789.

CA-NP-001 reports $1.620 million of actual 2015 expenditure against the $2.833 million 2015 budget. The expenditure table does not establish system commissioning. Source: CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 5.

## Evidence state

The capital application establishes proposed equipment, conductor, route, and system scope. CA-NP-001 establishes later reported expenditures. Asset-level completion is established only where a separate completion or operating record says so. The 69L construction year remains unresolved because the primary filing gives two different years.
