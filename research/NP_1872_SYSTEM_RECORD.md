# 1872 System Record

## Western telegraph offices

The *Morning Chronicle* reported on November 13 that inquiries did not support the alleged abandonment of telegraph stations west of Placentia. The report identified Rose Blanche as an operating office and said an expected closure there was not part of a general plan to close other western offices.

The report said the Rose Blanche office had opened to serve a firm whose business covered the station's expenses. That firm had ceased business. Receipts were then too small to support the office. The paper said the office was expected to close in spring.

The same report said a new telegraph office was intended for Bay Roberts, Conception Bay. It does not establish that either the Rose Blanche closure or the Bay Roberts opening was completed.
