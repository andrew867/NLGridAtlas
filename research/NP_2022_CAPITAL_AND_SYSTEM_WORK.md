## Station and feeder completion

The Q4 report records the Glovertown and Humber substation construction and commissioning work complete. It also records the Springfield SPF-01, Pulpit Rock PUL-03, and Virginia Waters VIR-01 load-growth work complete, physical PDF p. 13.

At Humber, the project converted HUM-01, HUM-04, and HUM-07 from 4.16 kV to 12.5 kV. The expenditure report states that all three feeders were converted ahead of schedule. Actual expenditure was $910,000 against $1.355 million approved, with no carryover, physical PDF pp. 10 and 21.

The SPF-01 project covered 3.8 km. The filed work converted 1.2 km from two phase to three phase and 2.6 km from single phase to three phase, with both sections reconductored from #6 copper to 4/0 AASC. The measured current was approximately 145 A. The application gives a 132 A winter rating for new #6 copper, an 85 A single-phase planning maximum, and 50 A neutral current, physical PDF pp. 402 to 403 and 407.

The 2022 feeder-additions program closed at $1.773 million actual expenditure against $1.690 million approved, with no carryover, physical PDF p. 10.

## Broad Cove outage

On November 22, a fault on the radial transmission supply to Broad Cove interrupted approximately 17,300 customers in the Portugal Cove-St. Philip's and Bell Island area. Newfoundland Power reported 2,285,000 customer outage minutes, physical PDF p. 11. The report does not identify the faulted circuit number.

## Capital program closeout

The Substation Refurbishment and Modernization program closed at $9.135 million actual expenditure against $7.049 million approved. The report attributes the variance mainly to unexpected site conditions at Glovertown and Humber, physical PDF p. 18. These are aggregate program values and are not assigned to either station.

The St. John's teleprotection project recorded $89,000 of 2022 expenditure and $361,000 of carryover into 2023, physical PDF p. 17. The later 2023 completion record remains the controlling commissioning source.

The annual financial report was checked. It provides aggregate electricity-system capital values and prior-year carryover totals, but no additional station topology, equipment ratings, or commissioning dates.
