# 1965 Island System Record

The published MHI-Nalcor historical-demand table begins in 1967. No 1965 demand value was located in the reviewed archive.

## TL203

The island transmission-line register records TL203 from Western Avalon to Sunnyside:

- 44.534 km.
- 230 kV.
- H-frame wood construction.
- Built in 1965.
- Ambient MVA ratings of 261.7 at 30 C, 276.2 at 25 C, 307.8 at 15 C, 326.1 at 7 C and 347 at 0 C.

A later engineering report records 185 H-frame wood structures. It identifies 562.5 kcmil AACSR 30/19 in the ice zone and 636 kcmil ACSR 26/7 in the normal zone.

## Newfoundland Power transmission

The 2024 rebuild schedule dates 35L from Oxen Pond to Airport and 108L from Gander to Gander Bay to 1965. The 2020 schedule dates 352L from Massey Drive to Walbournes to 1965.

The 108L engineering record provides the physical detail:

- 44 km.
- 396 single wood-pole structures.
- 2/0 ACSR conductor.
- Supplied from Gander.
- Primary supply to Gander Bay and Jonathan's Pond.
- A normally open connection at Gander Bay permits emergency supply from 114L and Boyd's Cove.

## Springdale, Northwest Brook and Greenspond

The Springdale filing dates the shared Hydro and Newfoundland Power station to 1965. Hydro owns and maintains the 138 kV bus and breakers. TL222 connects to South Brook, TL223 connects to Indian River, and one 138/25 kV, 16.7 MVA transformer supplies four 25 kV feeders.

Two later records identify 1965 transformer manufacture dates:

- NWB-T1, Westinghouse, 138/25 kV, 11.2 MVA.
- GPD-T1, Canadian General Electric, 66/12.5 kV, 2.8 MVA.

The Northwest Brook and Greenspond dates are transformer manufacture records. They do not establish the station construction dates.

## Bay d'Espoir

Stage 1 construction began in spring 1965. The initial scope comprised Units 1 to 3, each rated 75 MW.

Two 25 kV three-phase distribution feeders also date to 1965:

- L1 is a radial line over 20 km long with #2 ACSR. It supplied approximately 1,100 residential and 160 commercial customers in the later filing. T11, rated 69/24 kV and 10/13/16.6 MVA, supplies the 25 kV bus. BD1-VR1 and BD1-VR2 provide line regulation; BD1-R1, BD1-R2 and BD1-R3 provide feeder protection.
- L2 is 17 km long and supplied approximately six Hydro sites and four private customers in the later filing.

## Pierre's Brook and Heart's Content penstocks

The Pierre's Brook penstock record identifies:

- A 2,470 m woodstave section constructed in 1965.

- A diameter of 1,829 mm for both sections.

## Rural distribution

Later project records identify four 1965 distribution builds:

- Rigolet L1, 4.16 kV three-phase, 5 km, approximately 150 customers in the later filing.
- The Newfoundland Power portion of SPR-03 toward Little Bay, built by Bowater as a long-span 266.6 MCM ACSR single-pole three-phase line without neutral to supply Whalesback Mine. Ownership changes to Hydro at the metering tank before Little Bay Substation.
- The L'Anse Amour section of the L'Anse au Loup system, built with precast concrete and wood poles.
- BOT-01 extensions toward Leading Tickles and Fortune Harbour. The later feeder study identifies the overall feeder as 25 kV and records approximately 1,607 customers.
