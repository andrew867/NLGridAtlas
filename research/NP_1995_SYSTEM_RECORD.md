# Newfoundland Power 1995 system record

## Source boundary

CA-213 requests Newfoundland Power annual returns from 1996 onward. The archive contains attachments for 1996 through 2001 and no standalone 1995 annual return. The 1995 record therefore uses later regulatory tables that publish 1995 actual values and later asset histories that identify completed 1995 work.

## Reliability

CA-446, first revision, physical PDF p. 1, reports 1995 SAIFI of 3.70 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 5.34 hours per customer. Both figures exclude Hydro loss-of-supply outages.

## Capital expenditure

CA-2, physical PDF p. 4, records $30.782 million actual capital expenditure against $38.023 million approved. Actual expenditure includes $1.321 million for energy supply, $1.278 million for substations, $1.790 million for transmission, $11.840 million for distribution and $0.552 million for telecommunications.

## Energy and assets

The comparative normalized return in the 1996 annual filing, physical PDF p. 53, records 423,100 MWh produced, 4,186,353 MWh purchased and 212,936 MWh lost. Losses were 4.6 percent of purchased and produced energy.

The comparative fixed-asset table, physical PDF p. 21, records 1995 net book values of $46.310 million for substations, $44.707 million for transmission and $7.693 million for telecommunications. The comparative column does not provide the corresponding 1995 cost, accumulated depreciation or construction-in-progress values.

## Dated asset work

- Stony Brook was converted from its original load-bus arrangement to a 230 kV ring bus, PUB-NLH-318 physical PDF p. 4.
- TL241 was extended to Peter's Barren and placed in 138 kV operation, TL244 was upgraded for 138 kV operation, and TL256 was built from Bear Cove to St. Anthony Airport, IC-270 physical PDF p. 35.
- Greenhill's Olympus gas generator was overhauled and a cracked power-turbine casing was replaced with a used casing from a similar unit, the 2027 thermal-generation filing physical PDF p. 92.
- Pierre's Brook received a replacement runner and station-service transformers, the 2016 capital filing physical PDF p. 235. The later record attributes 4,100 kW actual peak output to the 1995 runner and 1996 stator-winding replacements together.

## Unlocated 1995 measures

The reviewed records do not publish a 1995 service-availability percentage, operating-area reliability table, peak demand or system load factor.
