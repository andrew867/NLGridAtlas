## Oxen Pond

The filed OXP-01 project replaced 3.2 km of 1960s three-phase #4 copper conductor on Thorburn Road. The scope also replaced deteriorated poles, crossarms, insulators, transformer brackets, and overloaded mainline poles. The filing records approximately 1,290 customers on OXP-01. It identifies 32 poles, 35 crossarms, and 105 insulators for replacement on the inspected section, physical PDF pp. 41 to 46.

The Q3 report records completion of the Oxen Pond 66 kV bus upgrade and switch replacement projects, physical PDF p. 13. The capital application states that the bus project increased the capacity of both existing 66 kV buses. The switch project replaced nine 66 kV switches, physical PDF pp. 164 to 165.

The capital expenditure report records the following 2024 closeout values, physical PDF pp. 12, 14 and 23:

| Project | Approved | Actual | Carryover to 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Oxen Pond 66 kV bus upgrade | $451,000 | $364,000 | $0 |
| Oxen Pond 66 kV switch replacements | $316,000 | $281,000 | $0 |

## Commissioning and financial closeout

The Q4 report records Gambo and Old Perlican commissioned in 2024. It records the Memorial project at 80 percent complete, with its new power transformer commissioned in the fourth quarter, physical PDF p. 15.

The capital expenditure report carries $414,000 for Gambo, $1,033,000 for Memorial, and $247,000 for Old Perlican into 2025, physical PDF pp. 8 and 12. These amounts are financial closeout work. They do not reverse the equipment commissioning statements in the quarterly report.

The annual financial report repeats prior-year substation carryovers on physical PDF p. 50. It does not add station topology, equipment ratings, or commissioning dates beyond the project records above.
