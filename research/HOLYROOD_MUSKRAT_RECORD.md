# Holyrood and Muskrat Falls record

## Conversion plan history

| Date | Filed position | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2012 | Nalcor said no specific conversion schedule had been prepared. It anticipated converting Holyrood Units 1 and 2 to synchronous condensers before commissioning the Soldiers Pond converter. SNC-Lavalin was to develop the schedule and execution plan. | [MHI-Nalcor-17, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-17.pdf) |
| 2012 | Nalcor had not completed detailed Holyrood retirement studies. It estimated the conversion of the units at $7 million and said that cost was included in the Labrador Island Transmission Link island-upgrades budget. | [MHI-Nalcor-11, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-11.pdf) |
| 2020 | The filed Unit 3 procedure required five to seven days to return from synchronous-condenser service to generation. Wood proposed retaining hydrogen in the generator, which it estimated would remove four days of gas-purge work and permit mechanical recoupling in three 10-hour days. The filing presents this as a recommendation. | [Backup-facility assessment, physical PDF pp. 3 and 24](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/reports/From%20NLH%20-%20Assessment%20of%20Options%20to%20Improve%20Holyrood%20Thermal%20Generating%20Station%27s%20Suitability%20as%20a%20Backup%20Facility%20-%202020-09-30.PDF) |
| 2022 | The condition study treated Unit 3 as a reversible generator and synchronous condenser. Its capital plan allowed for generator and transformer upgrades so synchronous-condenser operation could continue beyond 2030. | [Condition assessment, physical PDF pp. 69 and 648](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/reports/From%20NLH%20-%20Assessment%20to%20Determine%20the%20Potential%20Long-Term%20Viability%20of%20the%20Holyrood%20Thermal%20Generating%20Station%20-%202022-03-31.PDF) |
| 2025 filing | Hydro's 2026 capital plan states that retaining Unit 3 as a synchronous condenser after steam operation is a planning assumption under review through the Reliability and Resource Adequacy study. | [2026 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 170, report p. 4](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NLH2026Capital/app/From%20NLH%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%20Volume%20I%20-%202025-07-15.PDF) |
| July 2026 | Unit 3 recorded 0 generating hours and 697 synchronous-condensing hours. During its annual steam-side outage, the generator was placed online in synchronous-condenser mode on May 1. It was unavailable from July 5 to July 7 after an auxiliary cooling-water pump failure. | [July 2026 energy report, physical PDF p. 10, report p. 7](http://www.pub.nl.ca/indexreports/20152019energysupply/From%20NLH%20-%20Energy%20Supply%20Monthly%20Report%20-%20July%202026%20-%202026-08-17.PDF) |

The located record does not support a completed permanent conversion of Units 1, 2, or 3. The 2012 three-unit concept, the 2020 reversible Unit 3 procedure, the 2022 life-extension plan, and the 2026 operating report are different dated states.

## Drawing gap

The 2022 condition study confirms equipment that remained in the Holyrood switchyard, but it does not publish a complete current station one-line. Its document register names internal drawings, including single-line records, without reproducing the full drawing set. The atlas therefore keeps the December 2012 electrical drawing as a dated historical arrangement.
