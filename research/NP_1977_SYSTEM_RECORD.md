# 1977 system record

## Demand

The historical demand table reports a Newfoundland Power peak of 584.2 MW and a Hydro system peak of 679 MW. The table does not print an Island transmission peak for 1977.

Source: [MHI-Nalcor-92](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf), physical PDF p. 2. SHA-256 `cb8d035644fd2909c838e17866f2f366a71d9318b60c01a804f0dab0e0e03e19`.

## Blaketown

The 2027 station study says the 138 kV steel structure was constructed in 1977, when two 138 kV lines were brought into the existing station. It identifies BLK-T3 as a Westinghouse 138/66 kV, 25/33.3/41.6 MVA transformer manufactured in 1977. The older structure register lists the 138 kV steel structure as installed in 1978. The filings do not reconcile the dates.

Sources:

- [2027 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nf.ca/applications/NP2027Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202027%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202026-05-29.PDF), physical PDF pp. 423 to 424. SHA-256 `b55142e2f0a65cce29cba35c534492b1c151bb21f9925ff1a1daa71466522063`.

## Greenhill

A 2002 engineering review says the 25 MW Greenhill gas turbine was installed near Grand Bank in 1977. The 2027 facility filing says the Curtiss Wright unit was manufactured in 1975 and the Greenhill substation was constructed in 1977. It does not state a separate turbine installation date. The atlas retains the conflict.

## Power line carrier

The Central and West inventory lists one TL232 carrier system between Stony Brook and Buchans and the TL205 carrier system between Bay d'Espoir and Stony Brook with 1977 purchase dates.

Source: [Telecommunication Plan](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd2003B/files/applic/AppSectionH.pdf), physical PDF p. 41. SHA-256 `7dc7f33d73d0f2a2175cb3ba153a0551369a931ce6232d5a83e22a1f0de54e24`.

## Bay d'Espoir Unit 7

The Unit 7 exciter project identifies the GE Silcomatic IV exciter as original equipment installed in 1977. It had accumulated 91,856 service hours when the project was filed. The filing gives 1977 as the exciter in-service date and identifies loss of Unit 7 as a loss of 150 MW.

Source: [2003 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd2003B/files/applic/AppSectionAtoGtab1.pdf), physical PDF pp. 36 to 37. SHA-256 `d32a8fee06efc9213af092ba37037c873a0a97de291d6d76441e097246a294bc`.
