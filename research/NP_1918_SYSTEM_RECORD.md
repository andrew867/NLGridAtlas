# 1918 System Search

## Heart's Content hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power dates the original Heart's Content development to 1918. A later plant record describes the site as one generating unit with 2.7 MW nameplate capacity, 46.9 m rated net head and approximately 8.3 GWh normal annual production. Those values describe the later site record. The plant commissioned in 1960 replaced the original plant, so the later machine ratings are not assigned to the 1918 equipment.

The detailed development record describes a steel-frame powerhouse on a concrete substructure, a woodstave penstock, a concrete intake, an earth-embankment power canal, and storage structures at Southern Cove Pond or Rocky Pond, Seal Cove Pond, Long Pond and Packs Pond. Much of that physical description reflects the rebuilt development and is not treated as an as-built record of the original 1918 plant.

## Heart's Content pipe or flume corridor

The utility property schedule reproduces a deed dated June 14, 1918 from Theodore George of Heart's Content, administrator of the Estate of Philip George. It describes a 14 ft wide corridor through land at Southern Cove for the Public Service Electric Company pipe or flume line, containing about 36 perches.

## Port Union woodstave penstock

The 2020 woodstave-penstock register dates the original Port Union penstock to 1918. It records replacement in 1984 and 137 m of woodstave pipe. The detailed development record dates the Port Union plant and both original turbines to 1917. The penstock construction year and plant commissioning year remain separate asset dates.

## Unidentified accounting entries

The depreciation transaction ledger records $4,850 in account 326.00, Switching, Metering and Control Equipment, and $700 in account 327.00, Miscellaneous Power Plant Equipment, with installation year 1918. Each is paired with an equal retirement entry in 1966. The ledger does not identify a plant or location, so the amounts are not assigned to named atlas assets.
