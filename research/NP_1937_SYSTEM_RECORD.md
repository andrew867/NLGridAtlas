# 1937 System Record

## Lawn Forebay Dam

Newfoundland Power describes the original Lawn forebay dam as rockfill. In 1937, the dam received a concrete base and concrete was placed along its upstream and downstream sides, encasing the rockfill compartments.

The same filing gives a 50 m crest length, 9.0 m maximum height, a concrete intake and sluice gate, and a 53.0 m shallow concrete-weir spillway. It does not assign those dimensions or components specifically to the 1937 work.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 185, SHA-256 `80f3ff5963cb77172f9b48c3a9ea97255c554c3291c0eb35d823652dbbb3190c`.

## Grand Falls Generator No. 4

A 1992 Newfoundland Supreme Court judgment filed with the North Atlantic Refinery proceeding describes Grand Falls Generator No. 4. The judgment conflicts with itself on the installation year. Physical PDF p. 132 says the unit was installed in 1938. Physical PDF p. 135 says it was installed in 1937. The atlas therefore marks the year unresolved.

The judgment identifies G4 as the largest generator at the Grand Falls mill and as a vertical-shaft, 50 Hz unit. It records turbine replacements in 1954, 1973 and 1986. After the 1986 rewind, it gives a rated capacity of 27,000 kW and 29,700 kVA, while evidence cited on the same page gives 27.6 MW. Later system and condition records give 25.7 MW and 27.5 MVA. These values describe different filed records and are not combined into one rating.

Source: [Abitibi-Price Inc. v. Voith Hydro Inc., included in NARL written submissions](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2013NARL/files/corresp/WrittenSubmissionsofNorthAtlantic.PDF), physical PDF pp. 131 to 135, SHA-256 `b7834bf0e6b77405d42ad46511a20f57b1c49b2a6955a0c4500e1276295e3831`.
