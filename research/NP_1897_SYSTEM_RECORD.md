# 1897 System Record

## Black River industrial water power

The Newfoundland Quarterly history says construction of the Black River pulp mill began in May 1897 and was completed in November. The works included a dam, a storage dam at Puzzle Point and an artificial falls. It states that the falls supplied water to mill turbines that provided 12,000 hp for machinery. The mill began making wood pulp when construction was completed.

The source describes direct mechanical water power. It does not identify a generator, electrical output, transmission line or grid connection. The 12,000 hp statement is therefore not converted to electrical MW or added to the generation fleet.

Source: [Black River Pulp Mill](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/quarterly/TheNewfoundlandQuarterlyvolume92no1Summer1998.pdf), Newfoundland Quarterly, Summer 1998, physical PDF p. 16, printed p. 14. SHA-256 `6b501adf29cbd85be795b84f686b1fdcce664af4d446d01b9da132c6c1342434`.

A 1966 federal stream survey independently says the mill was built in 1897 with a 30 foot dam at the river mouth to supply mill operations. It says the mill had a 20 ton per day operating capacity and ceased production in 1903.

Corroborating source: [A Stream Survey Report of Black and North Harbour Rivers](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/10260.pdf), physical PDF p. 12, report p. 7. SHA-256 `4a926bf30bbbdc60d70cd4217064ca61d29aa532cc051364893f9cc1754ed520`.
