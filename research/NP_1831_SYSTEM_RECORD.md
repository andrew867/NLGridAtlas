# 1831 System Record

## Harbour Grace packet communication

A packet notice dated April 15 and printed in the May 6 *Public Ledger* states that the *Express* had resumed its usual trips between Harbour Grace and Portugal Cove. The vessel left Harbour Grace at 09:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday. It left Portugal Cove at noon on the next day. The schedule was subject to weather.

The service carried passengers, parcels and letters. The published fares were 10 shillings for a cabin passenger, 5 shillings for a steerage passenger, 6 pence for a single letter and 1 shilling for a double letter. Henry Winton at St. John's and Robert Oke at Harbour Grace were the agents.

The words *usual trips* show that this was a resumed operating schedule. The notice does not establish the service's first year.

## Lighthouse and electrical search

The 44 surviving regular 1831 issues of the *Newfoundlander* were downloaded and searched. The collection lists six missing issues, dated February 17, February 24, September 29, October 13, December 1 and December 15. Physical PDF pp. 1 and 2 are missing from the January 20 issue. The September 15 supplement was not included in the regular-issue scan.

No named 1831 Newfoundland lighthouse project, telegraph instrument, electrical installation or operating circuit was located. One January article mentions a letter on electricity in foreign news. It does not describe Newfoundland equipment or activity.

Later summaries consistently place the government-financed Harbour Grace Island lighthouse project in 1835 to 1837. The search did not locate a contemporary page supporting an internet claim that Robert Oke operated a Harbour Grace Island lighthouse in March 1831. That claim is not added to the atlas.
