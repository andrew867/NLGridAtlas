# 1836 System Record

## Harbour Grace Island lighthouse

Chapter 12, dated May 6, authorized a further loan of up to £1,000 to complete the lighthouse on Harbour Grace Island. The act states that the first authorized £1,000 was insufficient. Interest on the further loan could not exceed six percent per year.

The act blocked borrowing and expenditure until a specification of the work and machinery had been laid before the Governor and Council and the related cost estimate had been approved. The statute establishes financing and an engineering-approval control. It does not establish completion in 1836.

## Cape Spear lighthouse

The *Public Ledger* published a lighthouse commissioners' tender on August 5. It requested carpenter work and materials required to complete the Cape Spear lighthouse. The workmanship and material specification was available from secretary John Shea. Tenders were due at noon on August 11.

The newspaper published a notice to mariners on September 6. The notice was dated August 25 and announced that the lighthouse would show a light from sunset to sunrise beginning on September 1. It specified an elevation of 275 feet above sea level, a powerful revolving light and a brilliant flash at one-minute intervals. It also said that the stationary Fort Amherst light would continue.

The tender documents active completion work. The notice documents the commissioners' announced operating arrangement. No post-start log was located in the four newspaper collections searched, so the September 1 start remains reported rather than verified by an operating record.

## Published telegraph context

On November 30, the *Times and General Commercial Gazette* reprinted an account based on the Liverpool Telegraph-office logbook. The account described signals relayed station to station between Liverpool and Holyhead, including a daily one o'clock time signal used by the signalmen.

The report concerns a foreign station telegraph. It does not describe an electrical circuit or a Newfoundland installation. No named 1836 Newfoundland telegraph instrument, company, station or circuit was located in the four local newspaper collections searched. The available 1836 *Royal Gazette* scan contains eight parent issues, and the scanned *Newfoundlander* collection contains no 1836 parent issue.
