# 1942 System Record

## Tors Cove date conflict

Newfoundland Power records do not give one construction date for Tors Cove. The 2013 generating-unit table dates G1 and G2 to 1940 and G3 to 1951. The 2022 capital filing says the plant entered service and TCV Substation was built in 1941. The 2010 depreciation study says the 6,500 kW development was commissioned in 1942 under 52.7 m net head. The three dates remain separate.

## Rocky Pond station and forebay line

The 2009 filing dates Rocky Pond Substation to 1942. It describes the original generation station as one transformer with a 4 MVA capacity at 6.9 kV and a connection to Mobile through the 66 kV system. The same filing dates the 12.5 kV forebay distribution line to 1942. The line supplied the forebay and carried copper communications for water-level indication, gate control and gate position.

The plant commissioning year remains disputed. The 2009 filing says 1942; later development records say 1943.

Source: [2009 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2009/NP2009Capital/files/applic/NP2009ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 167 and 281, SHA-256 `1efb024d75066cf865f57d5b67da68ee653cb76f8945efa93019ea6dd024f9ef`.

## Transmission lines 11L and 23L

Line 11L was built in 1942 as a 5.0 km, 66 kV connection between Tors Cove and Mobile. Line 23L was built in 1942 as a 5.5 km, 33 kV radial connection between Pierre's Brook and Mobile. The 2010 filing describes 23L as having non-standard 3/0 copper conductor and no directly served customers.

## West Brook development

West Brook was commissioned in 1942 with one Leffel horizontal Francis turbine and one Westinghouse generator. The development record gives 680 kW capacity under approximately 47.0 m net head.

Source: [CA-NP-088](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf), physical PDF p. 140, SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

## Fall Pond forebay dam

The Fall Pond forebay structure is a reinforced-concrete buttress Ambersen dam built in 1942. The record gives a 54.7 m crest, 10.8 m maximum height, 32.6 m spillway crest and 9.0 m maximum spillway height at the left abutment. A sluice gate is also located at the left abutment.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 188, SHA-256 `80f3ff5963cb77172f9b48c3a9ea97255c554c3291c0eb35d823652dbbb3190c`.
