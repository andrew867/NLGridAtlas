## Terminal stations

Bottom Brook Terminal Station was commissioned in 1967 with TL211 to Massey Drive. TL209 followed in 1971, TL233 in 1973, and the fourth 230 kV ring-bus breaker in 1978.

- Source: `http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-318.pdf`
- Physical PDF page: 3
- SHA-256: `341a4aa08a70909a34b79b2a1ae0fe33e365d2e173812abf16144176e9607344`

The Sunnyside records conflict. The 2013 Capital Budget Application says the terminal had been in service since 1967. PUB-NLH-318 says the original station was commissioned in 1965 to 1966 and dates the 138 kV ring bus to 1967.

The same 2013 filing says Stony Brook had been in service since 1967. The separate terminal history dates its original load-bus commissioning to 1966.

## Rural distribution and generation
