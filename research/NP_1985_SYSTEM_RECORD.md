# Newfoundland Power and Island system record, 1985

## Demand, energy, customers and capital

- Newfoundland Power peak demand was 825.6 MW. Hydro Rural peak demand was 48.4 MW. Island transmission peak demand was 1,180 MW and Hydro system peak demand was 1,009 MW. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- Newfoundland Power purchased 3,204 GWh, generated 327 GWh, supplied 3,531 GWh, sold 3,331 GWh, and reported 172,119 customers at year end. [CA-NP-076, physical PDF p. 24](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022GRA/responses/CA-NP-076.PDF)
- The 1981 supply-planning case forecast a 1,487 MW coincident peak, 1,600 MW installed capability, 1.37 loss-of-load-probability index, 7,745 GWh energy requirement, 7,490 GWh firm capability, and a 255 GWh deficit. These are planning values, not actual operating results. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)
- CA-NP-146 reports $32.990 million of 1985 capital expenditure. CA-NP-076 reports $33.117 million. Neither filing explains the $0.127 million difference. [CA-NP-146 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-146,%20Attachment%20B.pdf); [CA-NP-076, physical PDF p. 24](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022GRA/responses/CA-NP-076.PDF)
- No supported 1985 SAIFI, SAIDI, or CAIDI record was located. The comparative series located in the archive begins in 1987.

## Generation and terminal stations

- Cat Arm commissioned two 63.5 MW Pelton turbine-generator units, 127 MW total. Station service could be supplied from either unit, the Coney Arm distribution feed, or the standby diesel. [2015 Capital Budget Application Volume II, physical PDF p. 6](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NLH2015Capital/files/application/NLH-2015-Capital-Budget-Application-Volume-II-2014-08-01.PDF)
- Holyrood Unit 3 synchronous-condenser equipment was installed and commissioned in 1985. The equipment history also records Unit 3 commissioning in 1979. [2011 Capital Budget Application Volume I, physical PDF p. 814, project p. 7](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2011Capital/files/application/NLH2011ApplicationComplete-VolumeI.pdf)
- Holyrood Unit 3's stator was rewedged in 1985. Stephenville's generator exciter was repaired and modified, and the plant record says the unit returned online in August 1985 after the earlier failure and replacement. [PUB-174, physical PDF pp. 54, 79 and 256](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/PUB-174.pdf)
- Hardwoods added an emergency diesel in 1985. The Island transformer register dates Hardwoods T5, the 75 MVA gas-turbine step-up transformer, to 1985. [PUB-174, physical PDF p. 128](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/PUB-174.pdf); [PUB-NLH-161, physical PDF p. 70](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-161.pdf)
- Bay d'Espoir Surge Tank 1 received interior coating refurbishment in 1985. [2024 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 699](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2024/NLH2024Capital/app/From%20NLH%20-%202024%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202023-07-12.PDF)
- Holyrood installed one 125 V, 30 A Saft Nife battery charger. Massey Drive installed two 125 V, 20 A Cigentic chargers. [2008 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 421](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/application/NLH2008ApplicationComplete.pdf)

## Newfoundland Power plant and station work

- Tors Cove G3 received a 42-inch electrically actuated butterfly main inlet valve, replacing the original manual valve. [2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 209](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf)
- New Chelsea replaced generating-unit wicket gates and linkages. [2013 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 213](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013Capital/files/applic/NP2013ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Pierre's Brook installed intake fans, exhaust fans, and louvers. [2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 251](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016Capital/files/applic/NP-2016-Capital-Budget-Application-2015-06-23.pdf)
- At Port Union, Whirl Pond dam and its gate section were rebuilt, Long Pond dam was rebuilt, and the Wells Pond dam and timber gate were repaired. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 145](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Glenwood station was built in 1968. The asset register dates its existing 138 kV and 25 kV wood structures and buses to 1985, and lists an 8.3 MVA, 138/25 kV transformer. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 198](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)

## Transmission and telecommunications

- Hydro inspected TL201, TL203, TL218, and TL236 during summer 1985 after the 1984 Avalon sleet storm. No poles were rejected. The inspection found incipient decay and recommended treatment. [2005 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 263](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hydro2005cap/files/applic/hydro2005CapBudComplete.pdf)
- Newfoundland Power purchased Motorola MCX-100 VHF mobile units and installed the Sandy Brook VHF tower. [PUB-69NP, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/rfi/PUB-69NP.pdf)
- A UHF link between Rattling Brook and Sandy Brook provided voice, remote control, and monitoring of Sandy Brook. [2003 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 74](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/applic/np2003CapitalBudgetapp.pdf)
- The Corner Brook frequency-converter assigned-asset table dates control, metering, and relaying equipment to November 30, 1985. The table does not identify individual relay models or the associated circuit. [IC-NLH-097, physical PDF p. 19](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013GRA/files/rfi/IC-NLH-097.PDF)

## Regulatory record

- Hydro filed the Rate Stabilization Plan referral in 1985. The Board recommended the plan on November 8, 1985, Government accepted it, and the plan began January 1, 1986. It replaced the Water Equalization and Fuel Adjustment Charge and tracked Holyrood fuel cost, hydraulic production, and load differences. [Order P.U. 25(2010), physical PDF p. 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/PU/orders/2010/pu25-2010.pdf)

## Date conflict

The Tors Cove penstock year is unresolved. Later Newfoundland Power records separately state 1984, 1985, and 1986 for the same 591 m by 2,743 mm upstream section and 113 m by 2,438 mm downstream section. [CA-NP-139, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/rfis/CA-NP-139.PDF); [2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 354](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NP2015Capital/files/applic/2015CapitalBudgetApplication-2014-06-26.pdf); [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 240](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
