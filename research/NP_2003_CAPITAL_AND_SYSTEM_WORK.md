# Newfoundland Power 2003 capital and system records

## Source set

The primary source is Newfoundland Power's amended 2003 Capital Budget Application, filed October 21, 2002. Source: [application PDF](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/applic/np2003CapitalBudgetapp.pdf).

The Board decision is [Order P.U. 36 (2002-2003)](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/order/pu36-03.pdf). The order approved a $55.290 million capital budget. It rejected the $500,000 Transmission System Engineering Study because the evidence did not establish a sufficient need. Source: physical PDF pp. 26 and 28.

Projects listed below remain proposed unless a later source states that the work was completed.

## Substations

- Greenhill and Marystown: reterminate distribution feeders with aerial conductors. Filed cost was $109,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 25.
- Trepassey: upgrade station equipment. Filed cost was $96,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 25.
- Frenchman's Cove and Gillams: rebuild regulator installations to correct electrical clearances. Filed cost was $60,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 25.
- Virginia Waters: add a third 25 MVA, 66/12.5 kV transformer. The filing forecast one existing transformer above its 25 MVA nameplate during 2003. Filed cost was $1.15 million. Source: application physical PDF pp. 34 to 35. The 2005 filing later records the third transformer as added in 2003.
- Chamberlains: add a second 25 MVA, 66/25 kV transformer. The existing transformer peak had exceeded its nameplate rating. Filed cost was $1.25 million. Source: application physical PDF pp. 36 to 37.

## Equipment condition and replacement

- Hardwoods: one HWD-T2 cable failed in 2002. The cables had approximately 30 years in service. The replacement estimate was $15,000 labour and $40,000 non-labour. Sources: CA-22(a) physical PDF p. 1 and CA-22(k) physical PDF p. 1.
- Rattling Brook: the 66 kV breaker was purchased in 1958 and was 44 years old. The replacement estimate was $22,000 labour and $95,900 non-labour. Sources: CA-22(b) and CA-22(h), physical PDF p. 1 in each file.
- St. George's: the 66 kV breaker was purchased in 1960 and was 42 years old. The replacement estimate was $32,000 labour and $124,600 non-labour. Sources: CA-22(b) and CA-22(h), physical PDF p. 1 in each file.
- Pasadena: two reclosers were purchased in 1981 and were 21 years old. The replacement estimate was $11,500 labour and $67,000 non-labour. Sources: CA-22(b) and CA-22(h), physical PDF p. 1 in each file.
- Big Pond: replace two oil-filled reclosers, one station-service transformer and one metering tank. Filed cost was $84,000. Source: CA-22(l), physical PDF p. 1.
- Fall Pond and West Brook: replace three 6.9/12.5 kV step-up transformers at each plant. Filed cost was $47,600 for each plant. Source: CA-22(j), physical PDF p. 1.
- Gander: replace three grounding transformers. Filed cost was $53,800. Source: CA-22(j), physical PDF p. 1.

## Protection, control and feeder work

- Colliers: add a feeder recloser. Source: application physical PDF p. 29.
- Holyrood to Bay Roberts 39L: add motor operators to line switches. Source: application physical PDF p. 29.
- Glenwood: add a high-voltage switch. Source: application physical PDF p. 29.
- Greenhill, Laurentian and Salt Pond: replace protection on 302L and 305L. Source: application physical PDF pp. 30 to 31.
- Bay Roberts, Walbournes and Blaketown: replace tap-changer controls. Source: application physical PDF pp. 30 to 31.
- Feeder remote control: install approximately 30 relays and 10 reclosers. Source: application physical PDF pp. 32 to 33.
- SPR-03: extend the feeder to Hydro's St. Patrick's system. The filing states that Hydro would convert its 4.16 kV system to 25 kV and retire the Little Bay supply. Source: application physical PDF pp. 55 to 56.
- GLV-02: extend the feeder to Charlottetown. Source: application physical PDF p. 55.
- 2003 reliability selection: GLV-02 served 1,195 customers with SAIFI 3.7 and SAIDI 8.2; LGL-02 served 688 customers with SAIFI 3.7 and SAIDI 5.5; MIL-02 served 1,293 customers with SAIFI 2.9 and SAIDI 2.2. Source: application physical PDF p. 60.

## Transmission

- 24L, Goulds to Mobile: rebuild 11 km and decommission the poorer-condition 17L section. Source: application physical PDF pp. 39 to 40.
- 301L: continue work on the approximately 38 km line. Source: application physical PDF pp. 39 to 40.
- 124L: complete clearance and design upgrades. Source: application physical PDF pp. 39 to 40.
- 123L: replace defective bolts and insulators. Source: application physical PDF pp. 39 to 40.
- 357L: complete work between Bayview and Massey Drive. Source: application physical PDF pp. 39 to 40.

The proposed $500,000 radial transmission study covered the Old Perlican to New Chelsea and Port aux Basques to Rose Blanche systems. The Board rejected that study. Sources: application physical PDF p. 41; Order P.U. 36 physical PDF p. 26.

## Telecommunications

The filing proposed replacement of the 1985 Rattling Brook to Sandy Brook UHF link for $155,000. It records availability below the remote-control requirement for approximately 9 percent of the 12 months ending July 2002. Source: application physical PDF pp. 74 to 75.

Order P.U. 36 approved the $383,000 telecommunications category, including 20 VHF mobile radios for $25,600 and 10 VHF portable radios for $10,000. Source: physical PDF p. 21.

Teleline-isolation surveys covered Pierre's Brook, Bay Roberts, Carbonear, Riverhead and Clarenville. Filed isolation work covered Bay Roberts, Carbonear, Blaketown, Bishop's Falls and Mobile. Source: application physical PDF pp. 76 to 77.

## 2002 project status

- Rattling Brook: a 45-year-old generator winding failed. The generator was fully rewound for $266,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 112.
- Seal Cove: failure of the 79-year-old generator damaged its bearings. The reported refurbishment cost was $350,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 112.
- Gander: the 12.5 kV structure was relocated and a 30-year-old cable was replaced. Source: application physical PDF p. 113.
- Grand Bay: T1 was built in 1966 and failed in December 2001. The reported repair cost was $200,000. Source: application physical PDF p. 113.
- Salt Pond: transformer delivery moved into early 2003. The proposed Salt Pond gas-turbine move to Wesleyville was delayed after Salt Pond equipment failures in April and May 2002. Sources: application physical PDF pp. 112 to 113.
- Telecommunications: planned relocation of repeater circuits at two sites was cancelled after analog-circuit rate reductions. Source: application physical PDF p. 120.
