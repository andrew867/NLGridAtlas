# NL Hydro 2024 terminal-station work

This record reconciles the 2024 quarterly work summaries with the 2024 Capital Budget Application asset registers and the 2024 capital-expenditure closeout. A completed quarterly entry establishes the station and activity stated in that report. The capital-budget register supplies an equipment identifier only where the quarterly report identifies the same asset or the mapping is unique.

## Hardwoods B2L42 disposition

The 2024 application planned replacement of the three B2L42 current transformers. The 2027 Capital Budget Application states that construction did not proceed in 2024 because the equipment arrived late after Measurement Canada approval delays. Construction also did not proceed in 2025 because an outage was unavailable. The work transferred to the 2025 to 2026 instrument-transformer program.

Source: 2024 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 505; 2027 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 236 and 342.

## Incomplete 2024 work

- The Sunnyside breaker-failure protection project carried into 2025. The closeout says its scope expanded to Oxen Pond and Massey Drive and remained incomplete at the end of 2024, physical PDF p. 59.
- The station-lighting program also carried into 2025. Sunnyside lighting was not complete in 2024, physical PDF p. 72.
