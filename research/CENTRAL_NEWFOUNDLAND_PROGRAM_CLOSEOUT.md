# Central Newfoundland 138 kV conversion program closeout

## Result

Newfoundland Power reports that the projects in the selected Central Newfoundland System Planning Study alternative were complete. CA-NP-138 lists a $13.641 million approved budget and $13.881 million actual expenditure, a $240,000 variance reported as approximately 2 percent.

The selected alternative converted Lewisporte and Rattling Brook to the 138 kV system and removed the intervening 66 kV system. Current filings show Lewisporte supplied by 137L and 147L, Rattling Brook supplied by 136L and 137L, and Roycefield Tap supplied by the retained 102L section from Gander.

The closeout is a program-level record. It does not give a dismantling date for Notre Dame Junction or identify the date on which each breaker and switch was removed.
