# Avalon 230 kV line engineering records

Updated: 2026-11-13

## 1996 construction record

Hydro's 1996 reliability study records the original and modified construction of the east Avalon lines. The report was filed as Muskrat Falls Exhibit 85.

| Circuit | Filed construction record | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TL201 | Commissioned in 1966; approximately 81 km from Western Avalon to Hardwoods; H-frame wood-pole construction; primarily 636 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR. Selected sections used 795 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR from 1988 and 1192 kcmil, 54/19 ACSR after the 1994 failure. | [Exhibit 85, physical PDF pp. 48 and 53](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf#page=48) |
| TL217 | Commissioned in 1970; approximately 76 km from Western Avalon to Holyrood; steel-tower construction; 795 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR. | [Exhibit 85, physical PDF p. 54](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf#page=54) |
| TL218 | Commissioned in 1968; approximately 38 km from Holyrood through Hardwoods to Oxen Pond; steel towers to Hardwoods, then double-circuit wood-pole construction with TL236; 795 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR. | [Exhibit 85, physical PDF pp. 54 to 55](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf#page=54) |
| TL236 | Commissioned in 1966 as the second circuit on the approximately 10 km Hardwoods to Oxen Pond double-circuit wood-pole line; 795 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR. | [Exhibit 85, physical PDF p. 53](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf#page=53) |

These routes predate the Soldiers Pond line splits. The interactive network keeps its 2018 endpoints and displays these records with their 1996 date.

The same study identifies the following conductor records:

- TL201 between Western Avalon and Soldiers Pond: 636 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR Grosbeak.
- TL217 between Western Avalon and Soldiers Pond: a combination of 804 kcmil AACSR/TW and 795 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR Drake.
- TL242: 804 kcmil, 23/19 AACSR/TW.
- TL266: 636 kcmil, 26/7 ACSR Grosbeak.

Sources: [physical PDF pp. 44 to 45](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2014Capital/NLHCBSUPP2014/TransmissionLine/Application/Application-2014-04-30.pdf#page=44).

## Later operating change

Hydro installed the TL201 dynamic line rating system in March 2024. Source: [2024 Reliability and Resource Adequacy Study, physical PDF p. 339](https://nlhydro.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/2024-08-26_NLH_RRA-Study_2024-RAP_Rev-2.pdf#page=339).
