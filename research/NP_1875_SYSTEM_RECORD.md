# 1875 System Record

## Direct Cable offer and Faraday failure

Council minutes dated January 16 record a Direct United States Cable Company proposal to pay the Newfoundland Government £30,000. The payment was conditional on the government giving the Anglo-American Telegraph Company notice that it would exercise its right of pre-emption. The government was favourable only if Anglo-American would repurchase the telegraph property or the colony was otherwise protected from financial liability.

Minutes dated February 16 state that Laurence Oliphant withdrew the offer because the Faraday had failed to lay the cable. The record establishes a withdrawn proposal and failed cable-laying attempt. It does not identify the cable's planned landing point, route, conductor construction or completed works.

## House monopoly motion

On April 17, a House motion proposed protecting the colony's purchase rights under the 1854 charter of the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company. It also proposed immediate government action to abolish the existing telegraph monopoly.

Debate began, but the Governor summoned the House and prorogued the session before the journal recorded a vote. The motion remains proposed.

## Anglo-American tariff notice

The North Star published an Anglo-American Telegraph Company tariff notice on November 6. The notice lists St. John's, Holyrood, Salmonier, Placentia, Brigus, Bay Roberts, Harbor Grace, Carbonear and Heart's Content. The newspaper's accompanying editorial says the cable tariff from Newfoundland to Great Britain and France had been reduced from 75 cents to 50 cents per word.

The notice establishes the named operating network and published tariff change. It does not identify line lengths, conductor construction, office equipment or traffic volume.
