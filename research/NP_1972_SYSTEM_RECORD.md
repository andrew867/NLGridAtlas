# 1972 system record

## Demand

Newfoundland Power peak demand was 316.1 MW. The table does not print Hydro system peak demand or Island transmission peak demand for 1972.

Source: [MHI-Nalcor-92](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf), physical PDF p. 2. SHA-256 `cb8d035644fd2909c838e17866f2f366a71d9318b60c01a804f0dab0e0e03e19`.

## Hardwoods Terminal Station

The original Hardwoods Terminal Station was built in 1972. The transformer table identifies T1 as a 230/66 kV, 40/53.3/66.6 MVA unit placed in service that year.

Source: [2013 Oxen Pond application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLHCBSUPP2013/oxenpond/application/2013-03-20AppOxenPond.PDF), physical PDF p. 17. SHA-256 `d1dec55ac47d4af754d9d7d01579504933cd77e7db8da4c5e289f3a3e8587116`.

## 114L

The 66 kV 114L was built in 1972. It extends north from Clarke's Head through Boyd's Cove to Summerford. The later operating record identifies the Gander Bay end as normally open.

Source: [2025 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF), physical PDF pp. 100 and 492. SHA-256 `4d7af5a441cbb7cc82e98766e482afcf59b0ef7cc03497ffab852a5a90c71dd3`.

## Dated station structures

- Molloy's Lane has a steel 12.47 kV structure dated 1972. Its wood 66 kV structure is dated 1967, and the later station history says the station was built in 1960.
- Clarkes Pond has steel 66 kV and 12.47 kV structures dated 1972. The register prints 20 MVA of transformer capacity.
- Bayview has steel 66 kV and 12.47 kV structures dated 1972. A later station history says Bayview was built in 1971, so the structure dates are not used as the station construction date.
- Islington has a wood 12.47 kV structure dated 1972. Its 66 kV structure is dated 1974.

Source: [CA-NP-088](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf), physical PDF pp. 192, 194 and 200. SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

## Catalina transformer CAT-T1

The 2017 filing identifies CAT-T1 as a 138/66 kV, 16.6 MVA transformer installed in 1972. The same filing says Catalina Substation was built in 1976. The transformer installation date is therefore retained separately from the station construction date.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 234. SHA-256 `80f3ff5963cb77172f9b48c3a9ea97255c554c3291c0eb35d823652dbbb3190c`.

## Port Union diesel

The depreciation schedule records 1972 as the Port Union diesel facility's year in service, 2005 as its probable retirement year, and 33 years as its life span. This is a depreciation record, not a commissioning report.

Source: [2003 General Rate Application, Complete Application, first revision](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/Application/CompleteApplicationRev1.pdf), physical PDF p. 810. SHA-256 `f927aa2c20da8ba4275708b1c0afeec77b14b2d747917d4f09e15400783c4a52`.

## Come By Chance date conflict

The 2018 review says Come By Chance Terminal Station was constructed in 1972 to supply the refinery. IC-NLH-097 estimates the terminal in service in 1973 and dates the listed transformers and terminal assets to January 1, 1974. The filings do not reconcile the three dates.
