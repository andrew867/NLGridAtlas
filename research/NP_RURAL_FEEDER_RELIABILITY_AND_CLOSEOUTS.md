# Rural feeder reliability and capital closeouts

Retrieved: 2026-11-13

## Reliability record

CA-NP-034 reports feeder-caused unscheduled outage results for 2016 to 2020. Newfoundland Power reported a system average System Average Interruption Duration Index of 1.82 hours and a system average System Average Interruption Frequency Index of 1.33 interruptions per year.

| Feeder | Customers | Average SAIDI | Average SAIFI |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MIL-02 | 1,493 | 2.98 h | 1.55 interruptions/year |
| PBD-01 | 672 | 2.20 h | 1.47 interruptions/year |

Source: [CA-NP-034](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/rfis/CA-NP-034.PDF), physical PDF pp. 1 to 3.

## 2018 substation program

The 2018 filing included feeder automation at New Harbour and Port Blandford. It assigned two feeders to New Harbour and one feeder to Port Blandford. The equipment would permit remote opening, closing, and operational setting changes.

The 2018 Capital Expenditure Report records the complete Substations Refurbishment and Modernization program at $8.001 million approved and $7.917 million actual. The program was $84,000 under budget and had no carryover to 2019.

The expenditure report closes the combined program. It does not identify the commissioning date or final state of NHR-01, NHR-02, or PBD-01.

## 2021 feeder load-growth program

The 2021 filing assigned $600,000 of the $2.655 million Feeder Additions for Load Growth program to PBD-01. The filed work included 700 m of new three-phase line and conversion of 2.3 km from single-phase to three-phase.

The 2022 Capital Expenditure Report records $1.899 million spent in 2021 and $773,000 spent in 2022. The program total was $2.672 million, $17,000 over budget, with no carryover to 2023.

The expenditure report closes the combined program. It does not identify the commissioning date or final state of the PBD-01 work.
