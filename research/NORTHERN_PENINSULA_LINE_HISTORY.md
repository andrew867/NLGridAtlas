# Great Northern Peninsula transmission line history

## Scope

This record attaches Hydro's dated construction and voltage history to TL221, TL241, TL244, TL256, TL261, and TL257. The later 2018 route and voltage inventory remains the current topology source for the island schematic.

## Filed history

| Circuit | Filed history | Source page |
|---|---|---|
| TL221 | Built in 1970 as a 66 kV line from Daniel's Harbour to Hawke's Bay. In 1990 it was terminated at the new Peter's Barren Terminal Station; the Daniel's Harbour to Peter's Barren section became TL262. | Physical PDF pp. 34 to 35 |
| TL241 | Built in 1983 from Hawke's Bay to Plum Point to 138 kV standard, initially operated at 66 kV. Extended to Peter's Barren and placed in 138 kV operation in 1995; the former Hawke's Bay 66 kV termination was removed. | Physical PDF p. 35 |
| TL244 | Built in 1983 from Plum Point to Bear Cove to 66 kV standards; upgraded for 138 kV operation in 1995. | Physical PDF p. 35 |
| TL256 | Built in 1995 as a 138 kV line from Bear Cove to St. Anthony Airport. | Physical PDF p. 35 |
| TL261 | Built in 1996 as a 69 kV line from St. Anthony Airport to St. Anthony Diesel Plant. | Physical PDF p. 35 |
| TL257 | Built in 1989 as a 69 kV line from Roddickton Woodchip to St. Anthony Airport. | Physical PDF p. 35 |
