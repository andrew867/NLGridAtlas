# 1882 System Record

## Coon River to Harbor Breton telegraph branch

An 1885 review of the colony's telegraph expansion reports that a branch line was built in 1882 from Coon River, on the Cape Ray line, to Harbor Breton. The article describes the branch as completed work. It does not give the route length, conductor, pole construction, office equipment or opening date.

## Twillingate and Fogo extension demand

On July 14, 1882, the Twillingate Sun called for an extension of the telegraph line to Twillingate, Fogo and nearby settlements. The editorial said the existing network reached the colony's northern and western extremes but left this side of Notre Dame Bay without service. It linked the missing connection to commercial disadvantage, reported losses and delayed communication with law-enforcement authorities.

The editorial records a local demand for service. It does not establish that a Twillingate or Fogo line was authorized or built in 1882.
