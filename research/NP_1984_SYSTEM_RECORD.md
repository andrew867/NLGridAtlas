# Newfoundland Power and Island system record, 1984

## Demand, planning and capital

- Newfoundland Power peak demand was 833.8 MW. Hydro Rural peak demand was 45.6 MW. Island transmission peak demand was 1,186 MW and Hydro system peak demand was 1,013 MW. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- Newfoundland Power capital expenditure was $30.615 million. [CA-NP-146 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-146,%20Attachment%20B.pdf)
- The 1981 supply-planning case forecast a 1,423 MW coincident peak, 1,600 MW installed capability, 0.45 loss-of-load-probability index, 7,391 GWh energy requirement, 7,490 GWh firm capability, and a 99 GWh surplus. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)

## April sleet storm

| Failure date | Circuit | Route | Conductor affected | Structures failed | Return to service |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| April 9 | TL237 | Come By Chance to Western Avalon | 5 conductor miles | 7 | 35 days |
| April 13 | TL201 | Western Avalon to Hardwoods | 5 conductor miles | 38 | 29 days |
| April 14 | TL217 | Western Avalon to Holyrood | 7.5 conductor miles | 12 | 14 days |
| April 14 | TL218 | Holyrood to Oxen Pond | 5 conductor miles | 12 | 9 days |
| April 14 | TL236 | Hardwoods to Oxen Pond | 5 conductor miles | 12 | 14 days |

Source: [PUB-NLH-299, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-299.pdf)

TL218 restored the first Holyrood to St. John's path after nine days. TL236 and TL217 restored the broader connection among hydro generation, Holyrood generation, and the Avalon load after 14 days. TL201 and TL237 remained out for 29 and 35 days. [PUB-NLH-299, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-299.pdf)

Newfoundland Power rotated Avalon feeders for seven days while local generation was limited. SCADA had not been implemented, so technicians and engineers performed the feeder rotation manually from substations. [PUB-NP-250, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-250.pdf)

The failure review records three distinct mechanisms. A guying arrangement moved at a large TL201 angle structure. A self-supported C-type TL217 angle structure collapsed and initiated a cascade. TL237 conductor broke in tension under 1.60 inches radial ice. More than 2.0 inches radial ice was observed. [Muskrat Falls Project Exhibit 85, physical PDF p. 56](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf)

Order P.U. 37(1984) authorized recovery of extraordinary storm operating costs over 1984 to 1987. A later filing identifies $650,000 of operating costs associated with the April storm. [2016 Deferred Cost Recovery Application, physical PDF p. 12](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016DeferredCostRecovery/files/applic/Application-2016-Deferred-Cost-Recovery-2015-04-15.PDF); [PUB-5, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/rfi/PUB-5.pdf)

## Transmission and stations

- Hydro upgraded 22 km of TL203 with high-strength 562.5 kcmil conductor in a two-inch radial-ice design zone. [2012 Capital Budget Application Volume II Report 10, physical PDF p. 12](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2012Capital/files/reports/NLH2012Application-VolumeII-Report10.pdf)
- TL247 between Cat Arm and Deer Lake was built in 1984. With TL239 and TL248, it formed the Deer Lake 138 kV ring-bus arrangement built in 1983 and 1984. [PUB-NLH-318, physical PDF p. 4](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-318.pdf)
- Bay Roberts received a 20 MVA, 138/12.47 kV distribution transformer. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 194](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- Oxen Pond installed a station battery charger. [2008 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 416](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/application/NLH2008ApplicationComplete.pdf)

## Generation and plant work

- Lookout Brook G1 and G2 were replaced by G4, producing the current G3 and G4 arrangement. The 2010 plant history dates G4 and its governor to 1984. A separate equipment history dates the Barber turbine and Ideal Electric generator to 1983, so the component commissioning year remains unresolved. [2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 183 to 184](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf); [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 113](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Petty Harbour replaced the Unit 2 turbine and generator and upgraded the Unit 3 turbine. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 207](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Port Union installed a 1,370 mm diameter, 137 m woodstave penstock. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 144](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Lookout Brook rebuilt the 76 m Cross Pond spillway as an earthfill and rockfill structure with a galvanized-steel core. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 115](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Cat Arm Unit 1 received its exciter. Cat Arm spherical-valve maintenance seals were commissioned in November. [NP 98, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/NP%20%2098.pdf); [PUB-NLH-1, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-1.pdf)
- The Hardwoods gas turbine experienced an exciter failure and had alternator end windings replaced. [IC-NLH-150 Attachment 1, physical PDF p. 69](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NLH2017GRA/rfi/IC-NLH-150.PDF)
- Greenhill was derated from 25 MW to 20 MW on the manufacturer's recommendation after deformation and cracking of the power-turbine casing. [2027 Thermal Generation Supplemental Application, physical PDF p. 40](http://www.pub.nf.ca/applications/NP2027ThermalGeneration/app/From%20NP%20-%202027%20Wesleyville%20and%20Greenhill%20Thermal%20Generation%20Supplemental%20Application.PDF)

## Unresolved dates

Later Newfoundland Power records date the Tors Cove penstock to 1984, 1985, or 1986. They describe the same 591 m by 2,743 mm upstream section and 113 m by 2,438 mm downstream section. [CA-NP-139, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/rfis/CA-NP-139.PDF); [2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 354](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NP2015Capital/files/applic/2015CapitalBudgetApplication-2014-06-26.pdf); [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 240](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
