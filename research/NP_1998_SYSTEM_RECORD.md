## Reliability

CA-446Rev gives actual system SAIFI of 3.21 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 4.91 hours per customer, excluding Hydro loss-of-supply outages. The annual report attributes the year's increase in outages to major sleet and lightning storms and interruptions originating with the major electrical supplier. It does not publish a 1998 service-availability percentage.

## Communications and power quality

Telephony Video Data entered service in 1998. It combined computers, telecommunications and the existing SCADA system and could provide outage cause, location and estimated-duration information to as many as 2,000 simultaneous callers.

Approximately 60 remote monitors were installed at customer premises and company facilities for the power-quality pilot. The annual report states that a replacement SCADA system would be installed in 1999. That future state is kept separate from the TVD and monitoring equipment operating in 1998.

## Capital and system work

Newfoundland Power invested $45 million in 1998 capital programs. The report describes equipment replacement, structural improvements against extreme weather, vegetation management across 10,000 km of transmission and distribution lines, and a four-year, $2.6 million program to replace distribution transformers at risk of spills. It does not identify specific line or station designations for the general upgrade work.

The Rose Blanche hydro plant was energized on December 22, 1998. The annual report gives a 6.1 MW rating and identifies it as Newfoundland Power's first new generating facility in 14 years. Its official opening followed in August 1999.

## Operating and financial record

The ten-year summary reports 429 million kWh generated, 4,259 million kWh purchased and a 957 MW peak one-hour demand in 1998.

At December 31, 1998, the fixed-asset table reports transmission and substations at $167.328 million cost, $73.332 million accumulated depreciation and $93.996 million net book value.
