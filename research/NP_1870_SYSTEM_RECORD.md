# 1870 System Record

## Coastal fishery cable proposal

The *Express* of August 27 discussed an earlier *Newfoundlander* proposal for a submarine cable along the coasts of Newfoundland and Labrador. The proposed system would report fishery conditions to coastal stations. The article cited a cable along the Norwegian coast as its example.

The *Express* said a Labrador trial could lead the Dominion Government and Parliament to extend similar service to fisheries around the Gulf of St. Lawrence. This was newspaper advocacy. The report does not identify an approved project, survey, route, appropriation, construction contract or completed cable in Newfoundland or Labrador.
