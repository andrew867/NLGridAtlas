## Corner Brook fibre

The 2019 application filed one fibre link between Humber and Bayview for SCADA, corporate data, and digital protection relay communications on the Corner Brook 66 kV loop, physical PDF p. 92. No completion record was located in this tranche, so both station histories label the link as filed work.

## Financial filing review

The expenditure report supplies project totals and construction explanations that are absent from the engineering schedules. It establishes actual 2019 spending for Long Pond, the Oxen Pond termination, and 36L, but it does not establish the commissioning state of BLK-02, DUN-01, SJM-06, or SCV-01.
