# 1891 System Record

## Search result

The July 14, 1891 Evening Herald printed the St. John's Municipal Council by-laws. Clause 8 required every electric light company established or doing business in St. John's to pay an annual licence fee of $200 from July 1, 1891. This is evidence of municipal regulation, not a change to the electrical system.
