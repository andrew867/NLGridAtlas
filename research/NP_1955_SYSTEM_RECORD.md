# 1955 System Record

## Lockston to Port Union transmission

Newfoundland Power's 2025 filing says a 46 kV transmission line was built in 1955 from the future Lockston plant site to the Port Union electricity system. The line preceded the 1956 Lockston hydro development. The filing dates the increase to 66 kV and the links to Port Blandford and Clarenville to 1965.

Source: [2025 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF), physical PDF p. 413. SHA-256 `4d7af5a441cbb7cc82e98766e482afcf59b0ef7cc03497ffab852a5a90c71dd3`.

## Bell Island submarine cable 28L

A three-phase submarine cable was installed in 1955 to supplement three cables installed in 1932. The technical specification identifies the 1955 cable as 28L. It was still operating when the replacement cables were installed in 1988, then was abandoned in 1990 after a cable fault.

SNC-Lavalin inspected the route on 2013-04-03. The abandoned cable was exposed on the Bell Island beach in the tidal zone. Its cast-iron protective necklaces had deteriorated but were described as relatively good for their age.

The report's 12.47 kV voltage and load values describe the later BCV-02 system.

Source: [Bell Island Submarine Cable System Replacement](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/NPCBSUPP2014/BellIslandSubCable/application/Application-ApprovaltoReplacetheBellIslandSubmarineCable-2013-12-09.PDF), physical PDF pp. 15, 79 to 80 and 314. SHA-256 `3d4088af8ea327741a80fa3b72925fe8e252fc869699ed41a41e56c28e293eb6`.

## Venam's Bight penstock

Hydro's 2009 capital plan dates the original wood-stave penstock to 1955. The penstock supplied water to the Venam's Bight generating station and had deteriorated to the point that replacement was required.

The 1955 penstock date precedes the plant dates in other surviving records. The 2024 asset assessment says the plant was constructed in 1956 and commissioned in 1957. Hydro's 2007 response instead lists it in service in 1956. Keep the penstock installation separate from the plant construction and commissioning dates.

Source: [2009 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2009/NLH2009Capital/files/application/NLH2009ApplicationComplete-VolumeI.pdf), physical PDF p. 34. SHA-256 `2c5db8dfca8bbb6fbbb5d093ba9ab9d3bdf78ccdd0a4282c6e23f61993e93ca4`.

## March ice event

The weather chronology dates a St. John's area freezing-rain event from 1955-03-13 through 1955-03-22. It reports that United Towns Electric lost three poles from heavy ice, one area served by Newfoundland Power was dark, and wires were torn from eight houses. Canadian National Telegraphs had little trouble because there was no wind.

Source: [Muskrat Falls Project Exhibit 96](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit96.pdf), physical PDF p. 54. SHA-256 `c00996592fa0c9bead4c414dcf8750a534c55acd73a955fb6e39502ca26f1266`.
