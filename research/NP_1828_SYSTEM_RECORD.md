# 1828 System Record

## Conception Bay packet communication

The January 9 *Newfoundlander* reprints an *Express* packet notice dated July 25, 1827, on physical PDF p. 1. The notice states that the boat had undergone alterations for passenger comfort and continued between Harbour Grace and Portugal Cove. It left Harbour Grace at 09:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and left Portugal Cove at noon on the next day. A carrier left St. John's at 08:00, except on Sundays and in bad weather. The service carried passengers, parcels and letters. James Clift at St. John's and P. Rogerson at Harbour Grace were the agents. The publication confirms continuity but does not establish a new 1828 alteration.

The March 5 *Newfoundlander* prints the winter arrangement on physical PDF p. 1. The *Express* was laid up for the winter. A suitable boat with an experienced crew would run between Harbour Grace and Portugal Cove when favourable opportunities occurred. If water communication stopped, a land letter carrier would travel weekly from Harbour Grace to St. John's, weather permitting. T. Ridley at Harbour Grace and James Clift at St. John's were the agents.

The December 19 *Public Ledger* prints a Union packet notice dated May 2 on physical PDF p. 1. Matthew Guswell stated that he had launched a commodious packet boat that was operating between Portugal Cove and the principal ports of Conception Bay. The published schedule named Carbonear, Brigus and Port de Grave, and Harbour Grace. Henry Winton's shop in St. John's received letters and parcels, closed the mails on the departure mornings and dispatched the messenger at 08:00.

The June 19 *Newfoundlander* prints an *Express* repaired-service notice dated May 7 on physical PDF p. 1. It states that the boat had undergone a thorough repair and would continue between Harbour Grace and Portugal Cove. The notice retained the three weekly Harbour Grace departures, next-day Portugal Cove departures and St. John's carrier connection.

The December 25 *Newfoundlander* prints the *Express* and *Dart* notices on physical PDF p. 1. The *Express* notice repeats the repaired service. James Doyle's *Dart* notice records continued operation between Carbonear and Portugal Cove during the remainder of the season and states that Doyle would receive and deliver letters and parcels personally. The wording documents an operating route. It does not identify the route's first service date.

## Western Ward watch and fire communication

The April 22 *Royal Gazette* prints resolutions adopted at a Western Ward meeting on April 8, 1828, on physical PDF p. 3. The inhabitants formed the Western Association for an annual night watch, an efficient fire company and protection of movable property during a fire.

The rules specify two watchmen and scheduled overnight rounds. They establish a fire company after fifty volunteers enrolled. Each warden was to hold a key to the engine house. On an alarm, each warden was to report with a lantern, speaking trumpet and staff, select a position for the engine and direct the company. The captain was also assigned a lantern, speaking trumpet and engine-house key. Lieutenants were to report to the engine house with lanterns at night, and other members were to report there to help move the engine and assist under the wardens.

This is a documented local alarm-response and command arrangement. The source does not describe an electrical alarm, telegraph or installed electrical apparatus.

## Newspaper search

The review covered 188 surviving 1828 newspaper files: 47 issues of *The Newfoundlander*, 48 issues of *The Royal Gazette* and 93 issues of the *Public Ledger*. The January 9 reprint remains a continuity record. Other repeated packet advertisements were not counted as separate operating events.

No named 1828 Newfoundland lighthouse project, electrical telegraph, installed electrical apparatus or operating electrical circuit was located in the surviving files. Foreign scientific reports, foreign navigation reports and sale advertisements were not treated as Newfoundland equipment records.
