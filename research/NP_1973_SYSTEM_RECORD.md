## Gallant Street

Gallant Street was built in 1973 as a 66/12 kV distribution substation. The 2010 filing records two transformers with 26.7 MVA combined capacity, four 12 kV feeders, 401L to Stephenville, 402L to Berry Head, and 406L to Harmon.

## Lewisporte radial supply

The 14 km 103L was constructed in 1973 as a 66 kV radial line from Notre Dame Junction to Lewisporte. Before 1973, Lewisporte was supplied by a distribution feeder from Rattling Brook.

Source: [2019 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2019/NP2019Capital/applications/NP%20-%202019%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202018-07-13.PDF), physical PDF p. 174. SHA-256 `c733684ac89212fe9fd1a2ac5f2198b6840692dd8f80260b5d08ae98471c5d5d`.

## Buchans and TL233

Buchans Terminal Station was commissioned in 1973. Hydro's system history says the 230 kV ring bus was established that year. Before the terminal opened, Bowater supplied Buchans at 50 Hz from Deer Lake. The later record says TL232 and TL233 terminations were swapped in the late 1980s.

TL233 was added to Bottom Brook in 1973. The line is 230 kV, 135 km, and built on wood H-frames.

The Buchans compressed-air piping was installed in 1973 for four air-blast circuit breakers. The 2012 filing proposed replacement piping, a backup air dryer, and a SCADA-connected dew-point monitor.
