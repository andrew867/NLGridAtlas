# 1935 System Search

## Unassigned civil-property vintage

Newfoundland Power's depreciation records retain $2,300 of original cost from 1935 in account 321.00, Roads, Trails, and Bridges. The 2010 depreciation schedule applies the Iowa 55-R3 survivor curve to the vintage. Neither filing identifies a plant, road, trail or bridge.

## Archive result

Exact-year searches covered generation, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, hydro civil works, property records, transmission lines, circuits, feeders, telecommunications, system demand, reliability, storms and outages.

The other returned records were depreciation references to Iowa Engineering Experiment Station Bulletin 125, United States utility legislation, aggregate accounting tables, hydrology series, modern operating times, engineering calculations containing the number 1935, and external utility records. No named Newfoundland or Labrador grid event can be assigned specifically to 1935.

The atlas therefore has no 1935 public card or timeline entry.

## Search rule

The account vintage cannot be attached to a site without a property register, work order or other source that identifies the asset. A later primary record can reopen this queue if it resolves the location.
