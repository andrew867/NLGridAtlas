# 1950 System Record

## Mobile powerhouse date conflict

A 2013 Newfoundland Power plant history says the Mobile powerhouse was commissioned in 1950. Later capital records date the plant and its original generating equipment to 1951. The 2013 description gives a 12,000 kW plant under 114.6 m net head with one vertical Francis turbine and a Westinghouse generator. The later equipment history gives an 11.0 MVA generator, a 13,000 hp turbine and the original powerhouse, crane and switchgear in 1951.

The turbine descriptions are not equivalent manufacturer records. The 2013 history attributes the turbine to Voith Hydro. The later detailed history says Dominion Engineering Works supplied the original turbine and Voith supplied a replacement runner and wicket gates in 1990.

## Transmission lines 12L and 14L

Newfoundland Power dates the original aerial sections later recorded as 12L and 14L to 1950. The filings describe the later endpoints as King's Bridge to Memorial for 12L and Stamp's Lane to Memorial for 14L. Memorial Substation was built in 1966, so the sources do not establish those endpoints for 1950.

The later 12L record gives 2.2 km of aerial line, 1.0 km of underground cable, 59 single-pole aerial structures with distribution underbuild and 1/0 copper conductor before the 2013 to 2014 rebuild. The rebuild replaced the aerial conductor with 477 ASC.

Source: [2014 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/applic/NP2014ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 69 and 309. SHA-256 `da133a107fc706c45e36d4e0f30b2156855a7b247f468aec02a23d1fad19566c`.

The later 14L record gives 1.13 km of aerial line and 1.13 km of underground cable. A 0.45 km aerial section was rebuilt in 1993. The 2015 filing recorded 22 original single-pole structures along University Avenue, all with distribution underbuild, and proposed replacing 477 ASC with 715.5 ASC.

Source: [2015 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NP2015Capital/files/applic/2015CapitalBudgetApplication-2014-06-26.pdf), physical PDF pp. 70 and 552 to 553. SHA-256 `fc573a340c7c2139ea63740568785ed30100b3766e2965a54349b75f517b2908`.

## Transmission line 16L

16L was constructed in 1950. The later record describes a 1.98 km, 66 kV line between King's Bridge and Pepperrell, using single-pole construction and 1/0 copper conductor. The line had 50 structures; 43 carried distribution underbuild and many supported street lighting along the Boulevard.

Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 74 and 373. SHA-256 `7494209347c908d48dc7e2b4a458a46ce39d5395ef43b5737ed93119a9785061`.

## Tors Cove transformer TCV-T1

Canadian General Electric manufactured TCV-T1 in 1950. The condition report identifies asset P-298, serial 256858, a 7.5 MVA generator step-up transformer with a 6.9 kV to 33/66 kV ratio. The 2022 project replaced it after corrosion and condition concerns.

Source: [2022 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202022%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202021-05-18.PDF), physical PDF pp. 329 and 339. SHA-256 `00288c716dd9b077f00a6d5b30d2e876f4dcd0f6988aaafe656f6b0bc0da3f71`.

## Bay Roberts construction-year conflict

A 2004 response gives 1950 as the Bay Roberts Substation construction year. The 2014 station report says Bay Roberts was built as a distribution substation in 1967 and expanded for transmission and distribution in 1978. Neither date has been substituted for the other.

Sources: [NLH-30 NP](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/rfi/NLH-30NP.pdf), physical PDF p. 1, SHA-256 `fb0be1d468a43429eecdbb343ffa0a06a456179b3c0b01e665bd40b395e81bc8`; [2014 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/applic/NP2014ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 243, SHA-256 `da133a107fc706c45e36d4e0f30b2156855a7b247f468aec02a23d1fad19566c`.

## Mobile land
