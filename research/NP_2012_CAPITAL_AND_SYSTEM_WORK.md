## Filed work and later outcome

### St. John's fibre routes

The application filed fibre routes from the System Control Centre to Molloy's Lane, Molloy's Lane to Stamp's Lane, and Molloy's Lane to St. John's Main. CA-NP-016 states that a five-year agreement for six leased circuits was completed in June 2012 and the construction work was suspended.

### Lockston plant

The filing specified plant-control, governor-control, protection, cable, station-service, panel, switchgear, generator, exciter, runner, wicket-gate, main-valve, and protection-panel work. The Q2 report states that the building extension was substantially complete and an outage was scheduled for mid-July. The annual review records a $387,000 favourable variance caused by a competitive construction bid. These records do not provide a single explicit final completion statement.

### PUL-02

The filing proposed replacing 4/0 AASC on 6.8 km from Pulpit Rock toward Windgap Road with 477 ASC. It gives a 356 A planning rating for the existing conductor, a 374 A per-phase forecast, and a 590 A planning rating for the proposed conductor. A completion record was not identified in this queue.
