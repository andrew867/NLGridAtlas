# 1956 System Record

## Transmission

### 43L

43L was built in 1956 as a 25.1 km, 66 kV radial line from Heart's Content Substation to New Chelsea Substation. The filing describes H-frame wood-pole construction, more than 2,500 customers in the New Chelsea to Old Perlican area, and the New Chelsea hydro plant's connection to the main grid.

Source: [2007 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/applic/NP2007ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 178. SHA-256 `6cfd75d76b0f7cdb103216420b29a1130a57dc22999f47621951c8322b8c3402`.

### 111L

111L was built in 1956 as a 31 km, 66 kV line from Lockston Substation to Catalina Substation through Port Union Substation. The filing describes single wood-pole construction, approximately 1,058 customers, and the Port Union hydro and diesel plants' connection to the main grid.

Source: [2008 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008Capital/files/applic/NP2008ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 191. SHA-256 `0982f4e3c88c5967ccb014c2299758e56b2029abe316da0313098de946820bf2`.

### 407L

407L was built in 1956 as a 27.9 km, 66 kV line between Stephenville and St. George's through Stephenville Crossing. It combined H-frame and single wood-pole construction. The 2005 filing identified 7.6 km of original 1956 poles and said the 13 km Stephenville Crossing to St. George's section contained later 1978 and 1992 rebuilds. The line served more than 3,000 customers and was the only grid connection for Lookout Brook generation.

Source: [2006 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2006cap/files/applic/NP2006ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 312 to 313. SHA-256 `7ce9f6671c827eab339c0e6c47caae3a807d99fa2458410c370d1ba7d3f8d485`.

## Lockston development

Lockston Substation was constructed in 1956 as part of the hydroelectric development. The 2024 filing dates the legacy 46 kV and 6.9 kV wood-pole structures to 1956. The 46 kV line to Port Union had been built in 1955 in anticipation of the plant; the later 66 kV connections are not assigned to the 1956 construction record.

The plant entered service in 1956 with one 1.5 MW unit under approximately 80 m net head. The unit used a horizontal 2,000 hp Gilkes Francis turbine and Canadian General Electric generator. The first unit was labelled G2. A second identical unit was installed in 1962 and labelled G1. The 2011 assessment says G2's stator and rotor windings remained original to the 1956 installation.

## Heart's Content station

Heart's Content Substation was built in 1956 as a generation station. Distribution equipment was added later. The 2011 arrangement contained a 66 kV bus with 41L to Carbonear, 43L to New Chelsea and 80L to Islington; a 3 MVA generation transformer; and a 2.3 MVA distribution transformer. Those later equipment ratings are not assigned to the original 1956 station.

Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 287. SHA-256 `7494209347c908d48dc7e2b4a458a46ce39d5395ef43b5737ed93119a9785061`.

## New Chelsea civil and electrical works

The steel penstock was constructed in 1956. The 2004 assessment dates the control panels, switchgear, protection systems and DC distribution panel to the original 1956 installation. It also identifies an original 1956 grounding transformer used for generator high-impedance grounding.

The plant service date remains unresolved. PUB-126 NP and the 2004 application say January 1957. Later engineering reports say 1956.

## Thomas Pond spillway

The Thomas Pond Spillway was constructed in 1956 as part of the reservoir system feeding Topsail Plant. The structure is a 46 m reinforced-concrete spillway with wooden stoplogs and reinforced-concrete abutments.

Source: [2019 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2019/NP2019Capital/applications/NP%20-%202019%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202018-07-13.PDF), physical PDF p. 195. SHA-256 `c733684ac89212fe9fd1a2ac5f2198b6840692dd8f80260b5d08ae98471c5d5d`.

## Snook's Arm and Venams Bight

The 2024 asset assessment says both mine-supply plants were constructed in 1956 and commissioned in 1957 for First Maritime Mining Corporation. Snook's Arm had one 0.56 MW horizontal Francis unit. Venams Bight had one 0.36 MW horizontal Francis unit. Hydro's 2007 response instead lists both in service in 1956 and explicitly says the published 1957 Snook's Arm date was wrong. The commissioning dates remain unresolved.

The original Snook's Arm woodstave penstock was built in 1956 by Pacific Coast Pipe Company. It was 30 inches in diameter and 3,050 feet long, with Douglas fir staves and approximately 12,000 steel bands.
