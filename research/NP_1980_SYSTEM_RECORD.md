## Rural-system ownership

The Power Distribution District of Newfoundland and Labrador fixed assets transferred to Hydro in April for one dollar. Current assets and liabilities transferred at cost. The same record says Hydro would fund and construct new assets, with the district paying the costs of those assets, including interest and depreciation. The rural electrification program moved approximately 690 households from diesel generation to the provincial grid during the reporting period.

Source: [1981 Board report](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/Consents/81PAPRIL.pdf), physical PDF p. 20.
