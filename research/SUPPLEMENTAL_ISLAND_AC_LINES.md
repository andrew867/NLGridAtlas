# Supplemental island AC circuit record

The PUB-NP-084 assessment asset list is not a complete inventory of every Hydro circuit found in other filings. TL225 and TL257 are absent from that list but have explicit voltage and endpoint records in separate Hydro responses.

## TL225

PUB-NLH-309 identifies TL225 as a 66 kV transmission line between Deer Lake Power and Deer Lake Terminal Station. It also identifies TL225 as the exception to the normally radial operation of Hydro's 66/69 kV circuits.

PUB-NLH-324 gives a 53.8 MVA rating at 0 degrees Celsius, a 23.0 MVA anticipated 2014/2015 winter peak, and a 20.1 MVA anticipated 2015 average load. Hydro states that the line connects Deer Lake Power generation and forms part of the delivery path to Corner Brook Pulp and Paper through the 230 kV connection to Massey Drive.

## TL257

Hydro's 2001 construction-history response identifies TL257 as a 69 kV line between Roddickton Woodchip and St. Anthony Airport Terminal Station. It records 1989 as the construction year.

PUB-NLH-324 gives a 56.2 MVA rating at 0 degrees Celsius, a 3.5 MVA anticipated 2014/2015 winter peak, and a 2.0 MVA anticipated 2015 average load.
