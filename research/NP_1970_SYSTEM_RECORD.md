## Holyrood generating equipment

Unit 1 was commissioned in 1970 at its original 150 MW design output. Unit 2 was commissioned in 1971. The 2022 condition study records both GE generators as installed in 1970 and rates each at 175 MVA, 16 kV. The installation year and commissioning year therefore describe different milestones for Unit 2.

## Hawke's Bay Terminal Station

Hawke's Bay Terminal Station was built in 1970. The filed original arrangement had 69/25-12.5 kV transformers rated 5/6 MVA and 2.5/3.3 MVA. Breaker B1L21, disconnect switches, the ground switch and the wood-pole support structure were installed with the station. The 2011 filing says the station then supplied approximately 1,300 customers in Hawke's Bay, Port Saunders, Port au Choix, Eddie's Cove West and River of Ponds.

Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2011Capital/files/application/NLH2011ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf), physical PDF pp. 570 to 571. SHA-256 `270c2e5c5cf5c09fec614b366a0a7c6c531113bda0f22685e59f5b75fdcccd5f`.

## Transmission construction

### TL220

TL220 was commissioned in 1970 as a 69 kV wood H-frame line from Bay d'Espoir to Barachoix through English Harbour. The retrospective engineering report gives an approximate original length of 48 km and identifies 266 kcmil 26/7 ACSR conductor rated at 11,000 lb tensile strength.

Source: [Exhibit 85, Reliability Study of Transmission Lines on Avalon and Connaigre Peninsulas](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf), physical PDF p. 55. SHA-256 `3bac7e9b0a9ccf3bce4d60ec1aa7e5fc3cb574eebe118989ebc57ff3fd903e96`.

### TL221

TL221 was built in 1970 as a 66 kV line between Daniel's Harbour and Hawke's Bay. Peter's Barren Terminal Station divided the original corridor in 1990, after which TL221 terminated at Peter's Barren.

Source: [IC-270, Northern Peninsula Transmission Line Construction History](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/filings/Sept10/Hydro/pkg19.pdf), physical PDF p. 34. SHA-256 `fc2cbb674eb94af1d3d031fffe3f1fe3d8efb48e38a55807b71e2c0f08351e19`.

### Other 1970 wood-pole lines

The 2014 inspection table dates TL225, TL226 and TL227 to 1970 and lists each at 69 kV. The same table dates TL221 to 1970. It reports the inspection populations for that program, not line lengths.

## Bay d'Espoir Stage II hydraulic equipment

Surge Tank 3 and its riser were installed in 1970 during Stage II powerhouse construction. The filing gives the surge tank height as 112 m and identifies its role in limiting penstock pressure changes and water hammer.

Source: [2015 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NLH2015Capital/files/application/NLH-2015-Capital-Budget-Application-Volume-I-2014-08-01.PDF), physical PDF p. 100. SHA-256 `c360d51f5cdaa875d20d22bdedc0e46315f2445f516cb723f1917a658933f401`.

## Holyrood emergency communications

Holyrood's plant paging system was installed in 1970 and served as the primary communication system for emergency protocols. By 2005, Hydro reported poor coverage and sound quality and proposed replacing it with coverage using the Hydro VHF mobile radio system.

Source: [Order P.U. 31(2005)](http://www.pub.nl.ca/PU/orders/2005/pu31-2005.pdf), physical PDF pp. 13 to 14. SHA-256 `1ee0a81a7943e74a431059a660278151aa939cf286dcf9c2d88fe214c6d76dd9`.
