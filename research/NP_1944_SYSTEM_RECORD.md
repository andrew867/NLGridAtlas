# 1944 System Record

## Salmon River hydrometric record

Hydrometric station 02ZE001 at Salmon River near Long Pond began recording in 1944. Hydro's 2004 inflow study says the station had 21 years of record from 1944 to 1964. The study used the pre-project record to develop separate Upper and Lower Salmon inflow sequences for the Bay d'Espoir system by prorating the observations by drainage area.

The 2024 Island Hydroelectric Supply Refresh Study lists the same station at latitude 47.944, longitude -55.914, with a 2,640 square kilometre gross drainage area. Its table gives a 1944 to 1965 period and a 22-year record. The two filed ranges are retained separately.

Sources: [IC-128 Attachment 1, Adjustment of Bay d'Espoir Reference Inflow Sequences](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hydro2006gra/files/rfi/IC-128.pdf), physical PDF pp. 33 and 43, SHA-256 `31e455e79a32b466d0e50f566486ee9489692a9d5c74b03976839ec4bf6b0b0b`; [Island Hydroelectric Supply Refresh Study](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/study/From%20NLH%20-%20Island%20Hydroelectric%20Supply%20Refresh%20Study%20-%202024-10-01.PDF), physical PDF p. 340, SHA-256 `a800a6c9dea43c783ae1d494bab8b4d939db8dc64e9c6e1610e9b335edf61b90`.

## Utility property records

The Newfoundland Power property schedule records three 1944 deeds:

- United Towns Electric acquired West Brook property from Walter E. Seibert in November 1944, Volume 177, Folio 510.
- West Coast Power acquired St. George's property from Leo Gaultois on November 2, 1944.
- West Coast Power acquired St. George's property from Mary O'Reilly on November 2, 1944.

The schedule prints no registration volume or folio for either St. George's row.

Source: [CA-NP-016 Attachment A](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-016,%20Attachment%20A.pdf), physical PDF pp. 152 and 155. SHA-256 `84f2db8eec714793a206a1f12175403aa9390f5f9527eae35a8396e9b745cd60`.
