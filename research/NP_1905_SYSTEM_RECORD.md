# 1905 System Record

## Exploits generation equipment

The 2017 Exploits Generation Condition Assessment dates two groups of surviving plant equipment to 1905.

At the Grand Falls power canal entrance, eight surface forebay gates were installed in 1905. The assessment says the gates no longer performed their original control function, were in poor condition, and could only serve as stoplogs for canal dewatering if the structure was refurbished. Those statements describe the 2017 inspection, not the 1905 installation condition.

At Bishop's Falls, Units 8 and 9 were horizontal pit turbines manufactured by S. Morgan Smith and originally installed in 1905. The equipment table rates each unit at 1,000 kW. The report says they were rehabilitated in 1983, their speed was increased by 20 percent for 60 Hz generation, and they were not in service during the 2017 inspection. The generator table elsewhere in the assessment lists the associated 600 V machines at 1.6 MVA and 1.9 MVA.

Source: [PUB-NLH-005, Exploits Generation Condition Assessment](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/rfis/PUB-NLH-005.PDF), physical PDF pp. 651, 671 and 690, report pp. 101, 121 and 140. SHA-256 `7295b73cf565c32382d53881a6e3e6e655d42a1c7c7d9d74665fc8d096097f02`.

## Independent watershed record

Hydro's 2018 long-term resource plan says the Exploits watershed was developed in 1905 to support the Grand Falls and Bishop's Falls generating stations. It describes the later system as run-of-river and gives the then-current station capacities. Those later capacity values are not 1905 ratings.

Source: [Reliability and Resource Adequacy Study, Volume III, Long-Term Resource Plan](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/2018ratemitigation/responses/PUB-Nalcor-122.PDF), physical PDF pp. 22 to 23, report pp. 17 to 18.
