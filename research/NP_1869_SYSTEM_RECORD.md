# 1869 System Record

## Almanac record

The title page identifies the source as the *Newfoundland Almanac for the Year 1869*. It was printed in 1868. The company entry on printed page 73 lists three related telegraph companies:

- the Atlantic Telegraph Company;
- the Anglo-American Telegraph Company;
- the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company.

The almanac identifies Brooking & Co. as the Anglo-American Telegraph Company agent in St. John's. It identifies Alexander M. McKay as general superintendent and local manager of the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company in St. John's.

The Anglo-American entry reports two annual financial entitlements under company arrangements:

- £125,000 from earnings of the Atlantic Telegraph Company's lines;
- £25,000 from the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company for through messages.

These are reported company terms. The entry does not state the Newfoundland land-line route, list operating stations or document construction in 1869.

## Reports presented to the House

The April 24 *Morning Chronicle* reports that the Colonial Secretary presented the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company's reports for the years ending July 1, 1867 and July 1, 1868. The newspaper does not reproduce the reports. This notice establishes their presentation, not their contents.
