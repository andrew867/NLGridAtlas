# Newfoundland Power transmission asset register

Source date: historical costs at December 31, 2022  
Filing: 2025/2026 General Rate Application, CA-NP-168  
Source pages: physical PDF pp. 1 and 3 to 4

## Result

Attachment A contains 109 unique circuit records. Their historical costs total $186,897,000. The table also records $54,000 as undistributed, for the published total of $186,951,000.

The register gives a circuit acronym, description, allocation class, and historical cost. It does not state circuit voltage, conductor, length, breaker position, or energization status. The atlas therefore uses it as a dated transmission asset layer, not as an as-built operating one-line.

The June 2005 Electrical System Handbook remains the source for the historical network map, station register, and feeder register. The two line registers are available separately in the interface.
