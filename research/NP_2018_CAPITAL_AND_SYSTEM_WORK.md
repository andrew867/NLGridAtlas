## 302L rebuild

The 2018 filing describes 302L as a 66 kV Salt Pond to Laurentian line. Most of the line was built in 1959. A 2.4 km section entering Laurentian was built in 1974. The original line was approximately 26.6 km, used 315 single-pole structures and carried 4/0 ACSR. The filed rebuild replaced the non-standard conductor with 477 ASC, with 11 km planned in 2018 and the remaining 16 km in 2019. Source: 2018 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 244.

## Automation programs

### Distribution feeder automation

Physical PDF p. 68 lists downline automated reclosers for CHA-01, CHA-02, CHA-03, HWD-07, HWD-09, BRB-01, MIL-02, LEW-01 and DLK-03. The LEW-01-R2 unit is identified as single-phase; the other listed units are three-phase.

Physical PDF p. 131 describes the program as eight additional automated reclosers, while the feeder table on physical PDF p. 68 lists nine feeders. The filing does not reconcile the difference. The atlas preserves the feeder table and does not convert either count into a commissioning claim.

### Substation feeder automation

Physical PDF p. 235 identifies a separate station program. Harbour Grace modernization covered three feeder positions. The additional work covered two feeders at Dunville, two at New Chelsea, two at New Harbour, one at Port Blandford and two at Upper Island Cove. The New Chelsea entry does not identify which two of NCH-01, NCH-02 and NCH-03 were included.

## Other closeout records

The St. John's Main Waterford River ductbank project closed at $4.512 million, consisting of $2.965 million before 2018 and $1.547 million in 2018. The distribution reliability initiative closed at $2.605 million, consisting of $0.218 million before 2018 and $2.387 million in 2018. Source: 2018 Capital Expenditure Report, physical PDF p. 17.

The report records $227,000 actual expenditure for fibre-optic network additions against $99,000 approved. The added Corner Brook link used a longer route to avoid distribution clearance upgrades. Source: 2018 Capital Expenditure Report, physical PDF pp. 13 and 26.
