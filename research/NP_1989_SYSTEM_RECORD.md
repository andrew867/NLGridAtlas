# Newfoundland Power and island system record, 1989

## Newfoundland Power reliability, capital and operations

The comparative reliability table reports 1989 SAIFI of 3.80 interruptions per customer, SAIDI of 5.20 hours per customer, and CAIDI of 1.37 hours per interruption, physical PDF p. 11.

CA-NP-146 Attachment B reports actual capital expenditure of $56.304 million, physical PDF p. 9.

The 1998 annual report's ten-year summary records 3,772 GWh purchased, 415 GWh generated, 4,187 GWh total supply, 3,960 GWh in sales, a 1,106 MW annual peak one-hour demand, and 188,575 customer accounts, physical PDF p. 29.

## Island transmission and generation

- TL257 entered service between St. Anthony Airport and Roddickton Woodchip. The transmission register lists the 69 kV H-frame wood line at 63.395 km, physical PDF p. 5. IC-270 separately dates the connection to 1989, physical PDF p. 4.
- TL258 entered service between Monkstown and Paradise River. The transmission register lists the 25 kV single-wood-pole line at 17.2 km, physical PDF p. 5.
- Paradise River entered service on February 26 with one 8 MW unit, physical PDF p. 76. Its T1 generator step-up transformer is rated 6/8/10 MVA at 25/4.16 kV, was constructed in 1988, and entered service in 1989. The 2014 filing reports 35 GWh net annual energy, physical PDF p. 222.

## Holyrood

- Unit 2 maximum continuous rating increased from 150 MW to 175 MW, physical PDF p. 232.
- Federal Pioneer T2 entered service at 115/152/190 MVA, 230/16 kV, ONAN/ONAF/ONAF, vector group Yd1, with Z1 of 13.7% and Z0 of 11.75%, physical PDF p. 229.
- Federal Pioneer T8 entered service at 75/100/125 MVA, 230/138 kV, ONAN/ONAF/ONAF, physical PDF p. 229. This is the unit later moved to the Sunnyside T1 position, not the replacement Holyrood T8 installed in 2014.

## Telecommunications

Hydro's ATI VHF system entered service with one central switch and 29 repeaters. Twenty-six repeater sites were Aliant sites and three were Hydro sites. The single-channel system operated at 150 MHz with 25 kHz channels and provided public switched telephone network access and ECC paging, physical PDF p. 17.

## Newfoundland Power asset work

- The concentrated transmission-insulator replacement program began in 1989. The later program summary reports $6.911 million in formal transmission-insulator work from 1989 through 2000, physical PDF p. 1.
- New Chelsea replaced the power cables between its switchgear and substation, physical PDF p. 212.
- Heart's Content upgraded the controls on its main-inlet and bypass valves, physical PDF p. 171.
- Pierre's Brook received a battery bank, physical PDF p. 235.

## 410L date conflict

Two responses in the same 2024 capital proceeding disagree on the date of the customer-funded 8.9 km 410L extension from Abrahams Cove to Lower Cove Mine. CA-NP-156 states 1988, physical PDF p. 1. CA-NP-160 states 1989, physical PDF p. 1. The station record retains the directly stated 1988 customer-contribution date from CA-NP-156. The 1989 timeline does not claim the extension as a 1989 completion.
