# Newfoundland Power 1988 system record

## Verified record

### Reliability and capital

- The comparative reliability table reports 1988 SAIFI of 5.78 interruptions per customer, SAIDI of 9.59 hours per customer, and CAIDI of 1.66 hours per interruption. [Information-17, physical PDF p. 11](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008GRA/files/information/Information-17.pdf)
- The historical capital table reports actual 1988 capital expenditure of $46,598,000. [CA-NP-146 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-146,%20Attachment%20B.pdf)
- The accessible 1998 ten-year operating summary begins with 1989. It does not establish 1988 generation, purchases, sales, peak demand, or customer totals.

### Boyd's Cove and TL254

- Boyd's Cove was built in 1988 as a 66 kV transmission station. It connected 114L toward Summerford and Gander Bay with TL254 toward Farewell Head, and served no customers directly. [2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 288](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- The Hydro register identifies TL254 as an 18.868 km, 66 kV H-frame wood line between Boyd's Cove and Farewell Head, built in 1988. Its listed seasonal ratings are 44.5 MVA at 30 C, 50.1 MVA at 25 C, 65.4 MVA at 15 C, 72.6 MVA at 7 C, and 80.8 MVA at 0 C. [PUB-NLH-316, physical PDF p. 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-316.pdf)

### Submarine cable systems

- The Farewell Head system entered service in 1988. Segment I runs 5.1 km from Farewell Head to Change Islands; Segment II runs 5.3 km from Change Islands to Fogo Island. Each segment uses four single-conductor 25 kV cables. The 2019 filing reports approximately 240 customers on Segment I and 1,600 on Segment II, with no underwater span failures but several termination failures. [2019 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 311 to 313](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2019/NLH2019Capital/applications/2019%20CBA%20-%20Volume%201.PDF)
- Two Bell Island operating cables entered service in 1988. A third 1988 cable was never used because of a factory-splice problem and was abandoned. The 12.47 kV BCV-02 crossing is approximately 5.2 km. Two additional operating cables entered service in 1990. [Bell Island Submarine Cable System Replacement, physical PDF p. 314](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/NPCBSUPP2014/BellIslandSubCable/application/Application-ApprovaltoReplacetheBellIslandSubmarineCable-2013-12-09.PDF)

### Holyrood and gas turbines

- Holyrood Unit 1 was uprated from 150 MW to 175 MW in 1988. [2013 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013Capital/files/application/NLH2013ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf)
- Hydro moved the original Holyrood T2 to the T4 spare position in 1988. The Canadian General Electric transformer is listed at 115/152/190 MVA, 230/16 kV, delta-wye, oil cooled. [PUB-NLH-277, physical PDF p. 229](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/rfis/PUB-NLH-277.PDF)
- A Fern Engineering modification at Hardwoods and Stephenville primarily replaced four cracked Curtiss-Wright power-turbine casings and several smaller components. The later history reports an approximate cost of $4 million. [2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 166](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NLH2010Capital/files/application/NLH2010ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf)

### Hydroelectric plant work

- Frozen Ocean Lake dam in the Rattling Brook system was rebuilt in 1988. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 269](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/applic/NP2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Seal Cove G1, an Allis-Chalmers unit, received a stator rewind in 1988. [2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 30](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NP2015Capital/files/applic/2015CapitalBudgetApplication-2014-06-26.pdf)

## Unresolved record

CA-NP-156 dates the customer-funded 8.9 km 410L extension to 1988. CA-NP-160 dates it to 1989. The atlas retains the conflict and does not assign a definitive completion year.
