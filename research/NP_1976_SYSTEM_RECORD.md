# 1976 system record

## Demand

The historical demand table reports a Newfoundland Power peak of 585.4 MW and a Hydro system peak of 681 MW. The table does not print an Island transmission peak for 1976.

Source: [MHI-Nalcor-92](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf), physical PDF p. 2. SHA-256 `cb8d035644fd2909c838e17866f2f366a71d9318b60c01a804f0dab0e0e03e19`.

## Hardwoods Gas Turbine Facility

The 2014 criticality assessment says the Hardwoods Gas Turbine Facility entered service in 1976. It describes two Rolls-Royce Olympus C gas generators, each with a nominal 25 MW rating, driving a Curtiss-Wright power turbine through an SSS clutch. The 13.8 kV alternator connects to the 66 kV system through a 45/60/75 MVA transformer. The unit provides peaking and emergency generation, but the assessment says synchronous-condenser service is its dominant operating role.

The 2010 Capital Plan age table prints 1977 as the Hardwoods commissioning year. The filings do not reconcile the dates.

## Transmission lines

### TL231

Order P.U. 31(2005) identifies TL231 as a 105.3 km, 230 kV steel-tower line between Bay d'Espoir and Stony Brook, constructed in 1976.

Source: [Order P.U. 31(2005)](http://www.pub.nl.ca/PU/orders/2005/pu31-2005.pdf), physical PDF p. 16. SHA-256 `1ee0a81a7943e74a431059a660278151aa939cf286dcf9c2d88fe214c6d76dd9`.

### TL229

The 2026 Capital Budget Application dates the 13 km TL229 route to 1976. The atlas topology identifies its endpoints as Wiltondale and Glenburnie.

Source: [2026 Capital Budget Application, Volume II](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NLH2026Capital/app/From%20NLH%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%20Volume%20II%20-%20Redacted%20-%202025-07-15.PDF), physical PDF p. 331. SHA-256 `59d3ac5507b9e9e707b7566ae3db91181c65db663bd1a267d644ad60cddb40d7`.

### 123L

The 2006 Capital Budget Application records 123L as a 94 km, 138 kV line between Clarenville and Catalina. It uses wood H-frame construction and was built in 1976.

Source: [2006 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2006cap/files/applic/NP2006ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 332 to 338.

## Stations

Later station histories date five Newfoundland Power stations to 1976.

| Station | 1976 record |
| --- | --- |
| Marystown | Transmission and distribution station built |
| Glovertown | Transmission and distribution station built |
| Springfield | 138/12 kV station built with a 20 MVA bank, two 138 kV lines and three 12 kV feeders |
| Fermeuse | 66/12.5 kV radial station built with a 4 MVA transformer and one feeder |
| New Grand Falls | 138/66/25 kV transmission and distribution station built |

The Grand Falls filing identifies GFS-T1 as a 138/66 kV, 30 MVA transformer; circuits 130L to Stony Brook, 132L to Bishop's Falls and 101L to Rattling Brook; a 66 kV tie to Grand Falls; two 138/25 kV, 20 MVA distribution banks; and five 25 kV feeders serving approximately 6,000 customers. These are the values printed in the 2011 filing, not a claim that every item had the same rating in 1976.
