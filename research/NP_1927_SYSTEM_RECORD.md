# 1927 System Search

## Seal Cove unit-year conflict

A Statistics Canada generating-station table filed by Hydro lists Newfoundland Light & Power's Seal Cove plant with two units, years 1922 to 1927 and 3,150 kW plant capacity.

Newfoundland Power's plant records do not support those unit years. The detailed hydro-development record says Seal Cove was commissioned in 1924 with two generating units. Its 2013 equipment response lists both SCV-G1 and SCV-G2 as 1924 units. A 2010 capital filing also says the original G1 runner operated from 1924 until 1964.

The evidence therefore does not establish that a Seal Cove unit entered service in 1927. The atlas retains 1924 for the development and marks the 1922 to 1927 Statistics Canada range as a source conflict.

## Petty Harbour range

CA-NP-088 describes Petty Harbour's three turbine-generator sets as commissioned between 1907 and 1927. The detailed plant record in CA-NP-036 gives the three commissioning years as 1900, 1908 and 1926. The broad range does not identify a 1927 unit.
