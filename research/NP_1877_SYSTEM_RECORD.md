# 1877 System Record

## Telegraph line completion

An Evening Mercury review published in 1885 reports that two groups of telegraph lines began in 1876 and were completed in 1877.

The first group served Trinity, Catalina, Bonavista and King's Cove. The second group served St. George's Bay, Bay of Islands, Bonne Bay, Batt's Cove, Tilt Cove and Little Bay. The place-name spelling `Batt's Cove` is retained from the article.

The review does not give line lengths, conductor sizes, pole construction, office equipment or individual opening dates. The completion year remains reported because the account was published eight years later.

## Telegraph property protection

An act passed on April 26, 1877 made malicious damage to telegraph property an offence. Its equipment list names batteries, machinery, wire-cable and posts used in an electric or magnetic telegraph. It also covered obstruction of the sending, conveyance or delivery of a communication.

The act describes protected equipment categories. It is not an inventory of the equipment installed on a particular line.
