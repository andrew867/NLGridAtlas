# 1877 System Record

## Eastern telegraph connections

A Governor's speech published in the North Star on February 9, 1878 reports that Trinity, Catalina and Bonavista had been connected with the capital since October 1877.

The speech treats the western line from Garia through St. George's Bay and Bay of Islands to Betts Cove and Tilt Cove as future work planned for completion in autumn 1878.

An Evening Mercury review published in 1885 later says two line groups began in 1876 and were completed in 1877. It adds King's Cove to the eastern group and St. George's Bay, Bay of Islands, Bonne Bay, Batt's Cove, Tilt Cove and Little Bay to the western group. Contemporary 1878 reports show western construction beginning and King's Cove still pending during 1878, so the later review is not used as completion evidence for those places.

## Telegraph property protection

An act passed on April 26, 1877 made malicious damage to telegraph property an offence. Its equipment list names batteries, machinery, wire-cable and posts used in an electric or magnetic telegraph. It also covered obstruction of the sending, conveyance or delivery of a communication.

The act describes protected equipment categories. It is not an inventory of the equipment installed on a particular line.
