# 1843 System Record

## Electrical experiments in St. John's

On December 8, the *Public Ledger* printed a letter dated December 5 at St. John's. The writer described a society formed in the town more than a year earlier. It had about forty members. Patrick Kough was president, John Withers was vice-president, R. M'Lea, Jr. was secretary and N. W. M'Donald was treasurer.

The society held semi-monthly meetings with essays and debates. Members had assembled scientific books and provided an electrical machine. The letter says that the machine was used for experiments for members and visitors.

The writer proposed that the society petition the legislature for money to buy philosophical apparatus and books. The letter does not report that a grant was approved.

The letter does not print the society's name. Kough's office in another St. John's organization is not enough to identify this society. The atlas therefore leaves the organization unnamed.

## Optical-telegraph terminology

On January 27, the *Public Ledger* described a proposed British Admiralty telegraph from London to Land's End, the existing Portsmouth line and relay systems in continental Europe. The article describes hilltop stations, signals and repeated observations. It is an optical semaphore network, not an electric telegraph.

No named 1843 Newfoundland electric-telegraph line, company, station, construction project or operating circuit was located. The December record is evidence of local electrical apparatus and experimentation only.
