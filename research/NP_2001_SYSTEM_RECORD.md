## System work reported for 2001

- Sections of 20 distribution feeders were rebuilt for $5 million.
- Laurentian-02 was rebuilt for 40 km between Lawn and Point May.
- The Salmonier Line was upgraded for 23 km.
- Transmission inspection work replaced poles, crossarms and insulators, and corrected conductor sag and clearances.
- A new substation was constructed at Lawn.
- Substations were rebuilt at Blaketown and Lewisporte.
- Approximately $4.5 million was invested in Burin Peninsula transmission, distribution, substation and generation work.
- The Cape Broyle hydro plant penstock was replaced.
- Sandy Brook received a new runner, instrumentation and programmable-logic controls.
- Greenhill received updated start-up and control technology.

The Greenhill relocation was approved for a planned 2002 project. CA-213F does not report it as completed in 2001.

## Control and outage communications

CA-213F reports expanded SCADA and remote-control units, Human Machine Interface protection technology in urban substations, and a Telephony Video Data outage system able to accept 2,000 simultaneous calls across eight service areas.

## Reliability

Newfoundland Power reported 99.96 percent service availability for 2001. CA-446Rev gives actual system SAIFI of 2.99 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 3.43 hours per customer, excluding Hydro loss-of-supply outages.

## Asset-base record

At December 31, 2001, the fixed-asset table reports transmission and substations at $181.928 million cost, $82.892 million accumulated depreciation and $99.036 million net book value.

## Other dated record

Newfoundland Power acquired approximately 70,000 joint-use poles from Aliant in September 2001 for $40 million. The transfer was to be completed over five years.
