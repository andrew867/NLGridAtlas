# 1863 System Record

## Operating company

The 1863 *Newfoundland Almanac* lists the New York, Newfoundland, and London Telegraph Company. Peter Cooper was president, Cyrus W. Field was vice-president, Moses Taylor was treasurer and David D. Field was counsel. Alexander M. McKay was local manager at St. John's and superintendent.

## Cape Race wreck traffic

The April 28 *Day-Book* states that St. John's received telegraphic intelligence from Cape Race at 4:30 p.m. on April 27. The message reported that the *Anglo-Saxon* had wrecked at Clam Cove, about four miles east of Cape Race. The same issue prints another Cape Race message dated 9 p.m. The article does not identify the transmission route or equipment.

## Atlantic cable tenders

The August 21 *Public Ledger* reports that the Atlantic Telegraph Company directors received several tenders on August 5 for manufacturing and laying an Atlantic cable. The directors referred the tenders to a committee that included William Fairbairn, Joseph Whitworth and Professors Wheatstone and Thomson. The report records tender review, not a contract award.
