# 1962 Island System Record

The published MHI-Nalcor historical-demand table begins in 1967. No 1962 demand, energy, customer, or annual reliability total was located in the reviewed archive.

## Transmission

### 13L, St. John's Main to Stamp's Lane

The 2014 filing describes 13L as a 66 kV circuit with 0.42 km of underground cable from St. John's Main to the Southside Road termination tower and 2.64 km of aerial line to Stamp's Lane. The aerial section was originally constructed in 1962 with 81 single-pole structures, most carrying distribution underbuild. At filing, 1.84 km and 56 original structures remained.

Source: [2014 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 310](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/applic/NP2014ApplicationComplete.pdf#page=310), SHA-256 `da133a107fc706c45e36d4e0f30b2156855a7b247f468aec02a23d1fad19566c`.

### TL2303, Churchill Falls to Wabush

Hydro's 2018 transmission summary dates TL2303 to 1962. It records a 215 km, 230 kV line on steel structures with Grosbeak conductor.

Source: [PUB-NLH-008 Attachment 3, physical PDF p. 16](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/NLH2018CostofService/rfis/PUB-NLH-008%20-%20Attachment%203.XLSX), SHA-256 `0bd1162e5bf97d6767368da48642ee29a300950f7a9e7c1ade1b3bedbc2b6913`.

### TL224, Howley to Indian River, unresolved date

The 2018 transmission summary identifies TL224 as a 57.51 km, 138 kV wood-pole line with Owl conductor and gives 1962 as its year built. A 2027 inspection table also gives 1962 and states that 225 poles were inspected.

Other Hydro tables give 1968. The 2025 wood-pole inspection plan lists 1968, as does the 2027 replacement-plan table. The 2027 application therefore contains both dates in separate tables. The atlas does not select one date.

## Station equipment

### King's Bridge

The station asset register gives 45 MVA total transformer capacity and dates one 66 kV high-voltage steel structure and bus to 1962. It separately dates another 66 kV high-voltage steel structure and 12.47 kV low-voltage switchgear to 1977, and 4.16 kV low-voltage switchgear to 1959. The 1962 entry is an equipment date, not a station construction date.

Source: [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 191](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf#page=191), SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

## Generation

### Lockston G1

Lockston was commissioned in 1956 with one 1.5 MW unit. A second identical unit was added in 1962, increasing the plant to 3.0 MW. The filing designates the 1962 unit G1 and describes its horizontal 2,000 hp Gilkes Francis turbine, Canadian General Electric generator, and original General Electric exciter. The Brown Boveri AB2/1 voltage regulators, GE AKF-1 field breakers, generator breakers, station-service breaker, forebay-line breaker, and instrument transformers are identified as original to the 1956 and 1962 installations.

Source: [2012 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 278 and 281](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2012/NP2012Capital/files/applic/NP2012ApplicationComplete.pdf#page=278), SHA-256 `80599e8c2fc0e5804fc9137916f6673f27f126621425c7829c3427f2773647f1`.

### Port Union diesel, unresolved date

The 2013 plant record says the Port Union diesel unit was commissioned in 1962. It records a 500 kW Caterpillar engine, a 625 kVA Canadian General Electric generator, and a 9,100 litre No. 2 fuel tank. The unit was inside the hydro powerhouse and shared common substation equipment with the hydro plant. A 2010 study reported it no longer operational and under retirement planning.

The 2003 depreciation schedule instead gives 1972 as the diesel facility's year in service, 2005 as its probable retirement year, and 33 years as its recorded life span. The atlas retains both dates.

## Distribution

### NWB-01, Goobies to North Harbour

The 2003 response dates the original line to 1962. It reports poor reliability before one section was rebuilt in 2000. Aliant rebuilt a second section, and the 2003 project attached Newfoundland Power's feeder to the newer section and removed the old duplicate line.

### Happy Valley L8

The 2011 project record describes L8 as a 25 kV, three-phase, 11 km line constructed in 1992 and serving approximately 500 customers. L8 incorporated a section of the older L1 built in 1962; the filed work applied to that older section.

## February ice storms

Exhibit 96 records a freezing-rain storm from February 7 to 12 affecting the Avalon Peninsula and St. Lawrence. It reports thousands of power lines down, 500 poles down, a broken transmission line, widespread communications failures, and ice accumulations up to two inches. Atlantic Transmission Company and Newfoundland Power and Light supplied equipment and personnel to United Towns Electric.

A second storm from February 16 to 19 affected the Gander area. The chronology records winds above 60 mph, a three-inch glaze, power outages lasting hours, and failure of one mile of transmission line.

Source: [Exhibit 96, physical PDF p. 54](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit96.pdf#page=54), SHA-256 `c00996592fa0c9bead4c414dcf8750a534c55acd73a955fb6e39502ca26f1266`.
