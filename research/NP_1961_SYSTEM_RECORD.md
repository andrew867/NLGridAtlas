# 1961 System Record

## Transmission

### 38L, Seal Cove to Duff's

The line was built in 1961. The 2004 filing identifies 38L as a tie between the Holyrood generating plant and the Seal Cove, Kelligrews and Chamberlains substations. Its filed rebuild covered a 2.8 km section with deteriorated poles, crossarms, insulators and hardware. The conductor on that section had limited transfers between Duff's and Hardwoods.

Source: [2004 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 389](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicComplete.pdf#page=389), SHA-256 `96f51d230a792c294b759fcc0dc6a52a5755f1ece0351be661444d16041f21cd`.

### 53L, Golden Eagle Refinery

53L was a 5.9 km, 66 kV single-pole line built in 1961 to serve the Golden Eagle Refinery in Holyrood. Newfoundland Power retired it in 2007 after the refinery load had declined enough to be supplied by the local distribution system.

Source: [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 277](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf#page=277), SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

## Hydroelectric works

### Rattling Brook storage

The Rattling Brook development was commissioned in 1958. Additional storage was added in 1961. The plant used two vertical Allis-Chalmers Francis turbines and General Electric generators.

Source: [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 141](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf#page=141), SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

### Venams Bight dams

The 0.36 MW Venams Bight generating station was constructed in 1956 and commissioned in 1957. Its 1961 work comprised the SV-5 concrete-faced rockfill dam, intake and concrete spillway, plus the SV-6 and SV-7 timber crib systems. SV-6 comprises three structures and SV-7 comprises two. The timber crib dams were rebuilt in 2000; the SV-5 spillway was rebuilt in 1994.

The same assessment records a 681 m wood-stave penstock from the original plant construction. The plant had been out of service since 2011 because of the deteriorated generating unit and penstock.

Source: [Mini-Hydro Economic and Technical Assessment, physical PDF pp. 30 to 35](http://www.pub.nl.ca/indexreports/other/From%20NLH%20-%20Mini-Hydro%20-%20Economic%20and%20Technical%20Assessment%20-%202024-12-20.PDF#page=30), SHA-256 `ccc3f058bbe4c9bd121c0fce847e6b4d0bbf1d8f0dccf4720cb0fc06efc82304`.

## Station equipment

### Wabush Terminal Station breaker 46-33

Breaker 46-33 and its internal 16 MVA metering current transformers date to 1961. The station record says Wabush Terminal Station had operated since 1963. Its listed equipment included eight 230:46 kV transformers, three 230 kV air-blast breakers, twenty-two 46 kV oil breakers, five 46 kV gas breakers, two 46 kV capacitor banks and two synchronous condensers.

The 2017 operating record states that breakers 46-32 and 46-33 normally shared a 50 MVA distribution load. With both in service, the 16 MVA metering current transformer rating was exceeded by approximately 15 percent. Loss of 46-32 at peak load would significantly overload 46-33.

Source: [Various Replacements at Wabush Terminal Station, revised application, physical PDF pp. 10 to 11](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NLH2017Capital/NLH2017Capital_SUPP_VariousWabush/applications/From%20NLH%20-%20Application%20-%20Various%20Replacements%20at%20the%20Wabush%20Terminal%20Station%20-%20Revision%20-%202017-04-10.PDF#page=10), SHA-256 `89cf5ebafa5e7f68dec2658d6c4f4b46310a4994453608bb819664df864ec230`.

## Happy Valley diesel plant building

The 460 m2 Hunt Street building was constructed in 1961 and housed diesel generating units until 1977. In the 2007 facilities study, 290 m2 was warehouse space and 170 m2 was a line depot.

Source: [2008 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 462 to 463](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/application/NLH2008ApplicationComplete.pdf#page=462), SHA-256 `ce5a753ba7d51e46bdc4d0ce88056f16d862201d05f3f0400e84c3191dcb8daf`.
