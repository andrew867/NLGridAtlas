# 1846 System Record

## Submarine-telegraph report

On September 9, the *Times and General Commercial Gazette* reprinted a report of Admiralty submarine-telegraph experiments in Portsmouth Harbour. The test used a cable section intended for the harbour. Boats carried it from HMS *Blake* in a bight and lowered it to the bottom.

The report said electric current traversed the cable and the receiving index showed the signals while the cable lay under salt water. It presented the test as evidence that telegraph communication could cross between headlands and discussed a future connection between Great Britain and France.

The test occurred in Portsmouth, England. No named 1846 Newfoundland telegraph line, company, station, instrument, construction project or operating circuit was located. No event is added to the atlas timeline for 1846.
