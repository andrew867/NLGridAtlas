## King's Bridge conversion and 30L rebuild

King's Bridge was built in 1948. The 2016 filing identifies three 66 kV connections, 12L to Memorial, 16L to Pepperrell, and 30L to Ridge Road. KBR-T1 and KBR-T2 were 66/4.16 kV, 10 MVA units supplying eight 4.16 kV feeders, KBR-01 through KBR-08, and approximately 2,266 customers. KBR-T3 was a 66/12.5 kV, 25 MVA unit supplying KBR-09 through KBR-12 and approximately 2,849 customers. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 263 to 264.

The filed work converted KBR-01 to 12.5 kV. A new KBR-12 trunk took the existing KBR-12, KBR-01 and KBR-07 load, eliminating KBR-01 and KBR-07. The station scope added KBR-13 and KBR-14 breaker and cable positions. The filing planned conversion of KBR-03 and KBR-06 in 2017, which would eliminate the remaining 4.16 kV system. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 487. The reconfigured feeder drawing is on physical PDF p. 492.

30L connected Ridge Road and King's Bridge. The line was 2.9 km long, built in 1959, and carried distribution underbuild on all 87 single-pole structures. The filing divided the rebuild into 1.5 km in 2015 and 1.4 km in 2016, with $507,000 filed for the 2016 work. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 316. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 322.

## St. John's fibre network

Newfoundland Power installed its first fibre-optic circuits in 1990. The 2016 filing reports 36 circuits, 30 owned and 6 leased. The St. John's network connected 15 substations and four office buildings in logically ringed networks. The filed work added Hardwoods to Kenmount and Kenmount to Duffy Place links for route diversity and capacity. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 556 to 558. The network drawing is on physical PDF p. 558.

The 2017 Capital Expenditure Report gives a $409,000 budget and $252,000 actual expenditure for the 2016 route-diversity project. It states that the final route was shorter than estimated. CA-NP-001 gives the same $409,000 budget and a $229,000 actual expenditure. Neither record states the exact installed endpoints. Sources: 2017 Capital Expenditure Report, physical PDF p. 167; CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 8.

## Corner Brook fibre network

The Corner Brook stations formerly used analog telephone service and leased four-wire SCADA circuits with 1980s modems. The 2016 filing proposed a staged fibre ring:

| Year | Filed link | Filed cost |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| 2016 | Massey Drive to Maple Valley to Bayview | $125,000 |
| 2017 | Bayview to Humber | $82,000 |
| 2019 | Humber to Walbournes to Massey Drive | $289,000 |
| Total | Completed ring | $496,000 |

The design gave the transmission-line protection relays redundant optical paths. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 558 to 560. The network drawing is on physical PDF p. 560.

## Greenhill gas turbine

The Greenhill unit used a Rolls Royce OLYMPUS aero-derivative gas generator last overhauled in 1995. The unit was rated at 20 MW after derating from 25 MW because of long-term power-turbine casing cracks. A March 2014 borescope inspection found coating loss and corrosion in the high-pressure compressor and stators, with carbon buildup and corrosion in the combustion chambers. The filed overhaul cost was $1.5 million. Source: 2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 32.

CA-NP-001 reports $1.091 million of actual expenditure against the $1.500 million budget. The expenditure table does not state the asset's commissioning date. Source: CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 6.

## Evidence state

The application establishes the filed station, line, generation and communications scope. CA-NP-001 establishes pooled actual expenditure. The two located closeout records disagree on the fibre project's actual expenditure, $252,000 in the 2017 Capital Expenditure Report and $229,000 in CA-NP-001. No record located in this tranche identifies the exact installed St. John's fibre endpoints or gives an asset-level completion statement for the staged Corner Brook fibre ring.
