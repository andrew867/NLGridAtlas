# 1873 System Record

## Telegraph-company consolidation

The Newfoundland Law Reports reproduce the 1875 judgment in *Anglo-American Telegraph Company v. Direct United States Cable Company*. The judgment identifies two separate agreements. The Anglo-American and French companies signed an agreement on June 25, 1873. The Anglo-American and Newfoundland companies signed a consolidation agreement on June 26.

The court held that execution of the June 26 agreement consolidated the Anglo-American Telegraph Company and the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company. The Newfoundland company merged into the consolidated company. The consolidated company acquired the Newfoundland company's exclusive privileges. The court rejected the argument that the transaction was a tripartite consolidation with the French company.

## Company submarine-cable inventory

An Anglo-American Telegraph Company notice dated July 18 lists fourteen owned submarine cables:

- three between Valentia and Heart's Content;
- one between Falmouth and Brest;
- one between Brest and St. Pierre;
- one between St. Pierre and Duxbury, Massachusetts;
- two between Placentia and Sydney;
- two between Placentia and St. Pierre;
- two between St. Pierre and Sydney;
- one between Port Basque and Aspoe Bay;
- one between Prince Edward Island and New Brunswick.

The route counts sum to fourteen cables.
