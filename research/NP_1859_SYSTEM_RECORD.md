# 1859 System Record

## St. John's message service

A January 4 *Morning Post* advertisement states that the Telegraphic News Agency transmitted messages between Europe and America over the line between St. John's and New York. Messages carried by European steamers touching at St. John's were forwarded by telegraph. The agency reported a vessel stationed off Cape Race to intercept steamers, with intercepted messages telegraphed from Newfoundland.

## City of Washington interception

The June 7 *Public Ledger* reports that the agency boat intercepted a dispatch from the *City of Washington* off Cape Race on the night of June 2. The ship had Liverpool news dated May 25 and Cork news dated May 26. The article states that the dispatch was published in New York on the following day.

## Atlantic Telegraph Company property sale

The November 9 *Telegraph* advertises an auction of articles belonging to the Atlantic Telegraph Company. The list includes new sashes and frames, nails, cross-cut saws, spunyarn, cordage, a warp, iron bedsteads, mushroom anchors, grapnels, bar steel, carpenters' tools, iron fittings and bolts. The notice does not state why the property was sold.

## St. John's electric telegraph office

The November 30 *Telegraph* asks correspondents to address newspaper submissions to the editor because material addressed only to the Telegraph Office had gone to the Electric Telegraph Company's office. The notice confirms a company office in St. John's but does not print its address.
