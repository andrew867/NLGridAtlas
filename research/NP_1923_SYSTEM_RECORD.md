# 1923 System Search

## Seal Cove date conflict

CA-NP-263 includes a Newfoundland Power description-of-operations table that lists Seal Cove as commissioned in 1923 with two units and 3.18 MW capacity. Detailed development records in CA-NP-036 and CA-NP-088 instead state that the two-unit development was commissioned in 1924. The 2015 Capital Budget Application also states 1924 and identifies G1 as manufactured in that year.

The lone 1923 table does not displace the repeated detailed 1924 records. The atlas retains it as a visible source conflict and does not create a 1923 commissioning event.
