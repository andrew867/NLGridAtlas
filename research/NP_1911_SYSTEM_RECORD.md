# 1911 System Record

## Goodyear's Dam

The Exploits system condition assessment dates the original construction of Goodyear's Dam to 1911. The dam is approximately 4 km upstream of the Grand Falls Generating Station. It regulates upstream water levels and helps a stable ice cover form during winter operation of the station.

The same assessment records later reconstruction phases. Its dam table says the right closure dam was raised in 1969. These later changes do not alter the filed 1911 original-construction date.

## Grand Falls G5 to G8 predecessors

The 2016 turbine assessment says the original turbines for Grand Falls units G5 to G8 were built in 1911 to drive pulp grinders. The turbines were replaced by SMS units in 1950 while the existing penstocks and concrete draft tubes were retained. In 1976 the grinding mills were dismantled, motors were rewound to 60 Hz, rotors were replaced, and the units were converted to electric generation.

The 1911 machines are therefore recorded as hydraulic equipment that preceded the generating units. They are not described as 1911 electric generators.
