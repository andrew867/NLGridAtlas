## Reliability and control systems

Newfoundland Power reported 99.89 percent service availability for 1999. CA-446Rev gives actual system SAIFI of 5.28 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 9.36 hours per customer, excluding Hydro loss-of-supply outages. The annual report identifies the April 5 to 6 snowfall, equipment failure, Hydro supply losses and wind storms among the year's outage causes.

The SCADA installation cost $2.6 million. The report states that it connected directly to the Telephony Video Data outage-communications system.

## Distribution-system work

The annual report records $2.8 million allocated to distribution feeders with poor performance records. It names Burnt Islands, Riverhead, Dunville and Botwood as program areas. The report does not identify feeder designations or state that a specific feeder project was completed, so the atlas attaches area records only where a station profile exists.

The report also records thermoscanning and vegetation management across 10,000 km of transmission and distribution lines. It states that thermoscanning prevented more than 250 outages.

## Generation and waterways

The Rose Blanche hydro plant was officially opened in August 1999. The report gives a facility cost of $13.8 million, including more than $1 million for fish ladders and a habitat-compensation channel, and estimates annual greenhouse-gas displacement at 19,000 metric tonnes.

Newfoundland Power replaced the Petty Harbour penstock and the surge tanks at Mobile and Horse Chops for a combined $3.5 million. The ten-year summary reports 450 million kWh generated and 4,292 million kWh purchased in 1999.

## Financial record

The annual report gives total 1999 capital programs of $42 million. At December 31, 1999, the fixed-asset table reports transmission and substations at $171.604 million cost, $76.225 million accumulated depreciation and $95.379 million net book value.
