# 1889 System Record

## Flavin Lane incandescent plant

The *Evening Mercury* reported on April 27 that the St. John's Electric Light Company had completed an incandescent-lighting plant in addition to the arc-lighting plant already operating at its Flavin's Lane central station. The completed orders supplied Government House, the Council Chamber, the House of Assembly, the Army and Navy depot, and the Bavarian Beer depot.

The generating plant had two horizontal steam engines driving one countershaft. Either engine, or both engines, could drive the shaft. The countershaft drove two dynamos:

- the larger machine supplied 300 A at 125 V at 650 r/min;
- the smaller machine supplied 30 A at 158 V at 1,050 r/min and charged accumulators at Government House.

The stated current and voltage pairs give 37.5 kW and 4.74 kW by `P = V x I`. These values are calculations from the newspaper data, not separately reported ratings.

Approximately 2,800 yd of overhead copper cable connected the station and the supplied buildings. The report states that conductor sizes allowed twice the operating current. It also describes safety fuses, room switches, main switches and a central station switch. The installed lamps were rated at 16 candlepower. Government House had about 165 lamps and the House of Assembly had about 130 lamps.

The Manchester Edison Swan Company Limited supplied the equipment and plant and carried out the work.
