# Newfoundland Power 1993 system record

## Reliability

CA-446, first revision, physical PDF p. 1, reports 1993 SAIFI of 3.68 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 4.64 hours per customer. Both figures exclude Hydro loss-of-supply outages.

## Capital expenditure

CA-2, physical PDF p. 2, records $33.862 million actual capital expenditure against $35.180 million approved. Actual expenditure includes $2.115 million for energy supply, $3.104 million for substations, $2.140 million for transmission, $21.710 million for distribution and $0.618 million for telecommunications.

## Energy and customers

The 2002 annual report's ten-year summary, physical PDF p. 51, records 4,093 GWh purchased, 420 GWh generated, 4,513 GWh total supply and 4,286 GWh in sales for 1993. The year-end customer count was 201,628.

No standalone 1993 annual return was located in the public mirror. The annual system totals above survive in later regulatory schedules.

## Transmission and station work

- PUB-NP-147, physical PDF p. 1, records that a 1991 study recommended a second 66 kV circuit between Oxen Pond and Stamp's Lane because of the forecast overload on 31L. It states that 70L was completed in 1993 and was Newfoundland Power's last newly constructed transmission line.
- CA-NP-062, physical PDF p. 10, records a 1993 right-of-way amount for 70L and identifies its endpoints as Stamp's Lane and Oxen Pond.
- The 2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 553, records that 0.45 km of 14L along Stamp's Lane was rebuilt in 1993. The circuit connects Stamp's Lane and Memorial University.
- The Bay d'Espoir to Western Avalon corridor history, physical PDF p. 12, records replacement of Canadian Ohio Brass insulators on TL202 from 1993 to 1996.
- The 2025 Northwest Brook modernization report, physical PDF p. 406, identifies the 138 kV bus and NWB-T1 protection relays as outdoor, pole-mounted electromechanical equipment installed in 1993.

## Generation and transmission research

- The Hardwoods life-extension report, physical PDF pp. 8 and 33, records the 1993 overhaul of the End B gas-turbine engine, serial 202223.
- The Hardwoods plant condition assessment, physical PDF p. 135, records replacement of both air-start compressors, the air lines and valves, and the air-start control panel in 1993.
- The Salt Pond gas-turbine record, physical PDF p. 335, records construction of the replacement powerhouse in 1993. The building replaced the deteriorated original prefabricated enclosures around the 14.7 MW plant.
- The 2006 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 69, records that the Hawke Hill test site was commissioned in 1993. Hydro used its icing-storm data to develop and validate transmission-line design criteria, evaluate line upgrades and validate long-term ice models.
