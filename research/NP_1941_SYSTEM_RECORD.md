# 1941 System Record

## Tors Cove service and original equipment

Several Newfoundland Power capital filings date Tors Cove plant service and substation construction to 1941. The 2015 filing describes the original plant as two 2,350 kVA English Electric generators, G1 and G2, each rated at 85 percent power factor and a 2,000 kW load, with two 2,850 hp Francis turbines under 52.7 m rated net head. The three-unit plant nameplate after the 1951 G3 addition was 6.5 MW.

The 1941 date is not consistent across the utility record. A 2013 generating-unit table dates G1 and G2 to 1940, while the 2010 depreciation study dates plant commissioning to 1942.

Sources: [2015 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NP2015Capital/files/applic/2015CapitalBudgetApplication-2014-06-26.pdf), physical PDF p. 353, SHA-256 `fc573a340c7c2139ea63740568785ed30100b3766e2965a54349b75f517b2908`; [2022 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202022%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202021-05-18.PDF), physical PDF pp. 289 and 329, SHA-256 `00288c716dd9b077f00a6d5b30d2e876f4dcd0f6988aaafe656f6b0bc0da3f71`.

## Tors Cove equipment and civil works

The filings identify the following 1941 equipment and structures:

- G2 turbine runner installed by English Electric.
- G2 generator manufactured by English Electric, with original rotor windings still installed in the 2014 filing.
- Original 15-ton Vaughan Crane Company double-girder overhead crane.
- Penstock trestle supporting a 17 m section of 2,438 mm woodstave penstock.
- Concrete abutments for two site bridges.

The G3 record requires a separate date distinction. The plant history says the G3 unit was added in 1951, but the 2017 filing says the G3 runner was installed by English Electric in 1941, the generator was manufactured in 1941, and the power cables were original to a 1941 installation. Those statements are retained without changing the 1951 unit-addition date.

## Rocky Pond generator

The Rocky Pond generator was manufactured by Westinghouse in 1941 and operates at 6.9 kV. The 2009 filing described its stator windings as extremely brittle and identified its rotor and stator as the oldest windings then in service in a Newfoundland Power generating plant. The plant service date remains disputed between 1942 and 1943.

Source: [2009 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2009/NP2009Capital/files/applic/NP2009ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 167, SHA-256 `1efb024d75066cf865f57d5b67da68ee653cb76f8945efa93019ea6dd024f9ef`.
