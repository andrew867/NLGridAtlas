# 1910 System Record

## Petty Harbour Unit 1 records

The filed records do not provide one consistent commissioning date for Petty Harbour Unit 1.

- The selected generating-unit table lists PHR-G1 at 1.6 MW with a 1910 installation date.
- The 2006 plant-refurbishment report says Unit 1 retained its original governor installed in 1910.
- The 2010 plant-refurbishment report identifies the Unit 1 main inlet valve as a 48-inch water-actuated butterfly valve manufactured by Voith in 1910.
- The detailed development record says the plant's three turbine-generator sets were commissioned in 1900, 1908 and 1926, but it does not map those dates to the present unit identifiers.

The atlas therefore records the 1910 governor and valve dates and preserves the PHR-G1 table entry as a reported value. It does not replace the conflicting plant-level commissioning record.

## Unidentified account 325 placement

The account 325 transaction ledger records an $89,482 placement with a 1910 installation year. It records retirements of $5,600 in 1967, $17,400 in 1970 and $5,000 in 2010. The 2010 survivor table retains $61,482, which equals the original placement less those three retirements. The depreciation records do not identify a plant, unit or location, so the amount is not assigned to Petty Harbour.
