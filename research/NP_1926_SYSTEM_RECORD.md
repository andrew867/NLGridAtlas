# 1926 System Search

## Petty Harbour generating unit and powerhouse

Newfoundland Power's detailed Petty Harbour development record says the original plant was built in 1900 and its three horizontal Francis turbine-generator sets were commissioned in 1900, 1908 and 1926. A separate 2003 capital-budget response says the 1900 powerhouse was upgraded and extended in 1926.

The 2013 selected-generating-unit table does not preserve a present unit with a 1926 installation date. It lists PHR-G1 as 1910, PHR-G2 as 1986 and PHR-G3 as 1924. The 1926 machine is therefore recorded as a historical plant addition without assigning it to a current unit identifier.

## Seal Cove G2 date conflict

The 2009 Capital Budget Application calls Seal Cove G1 a 1924 unit rated 1.1 MW and G2 a 1926 unit rated 2.4 MW. CA-NP-021 later lists SCV-G1 and SCV-G2 with 1924 installation dates and capacities of 0.9 MW and 2.6 MW. Other plant descriptions date the two-unit development as a whole to 1924.

The filings do not reconcile the G2 year or the two capacity pairs. The atlas presents the 2009 G2 record as reported and keeps the conflict with the 2013 table visible.

## Bay Bulls Big Pond property

Newfoundland Power's property schedule records a Bay Bulls Big Pond parcel acquired from Mary Frizzelle, administratrix, by deed dated November 10, 1926. The deed was registered in Volume 97, Folios 555 to 556.
