## Holyrood Stage I

Holyrood Stage I was completed in 1971 and brought Units 1 and 2 on line at 150 MW each.

Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2011Capital/files/application/NLH2011ApplicationComplete-VolumeI.pdf), physical PDF p. 910. SHA-256 `d02cac731457ffa45ea1cf8f93af64626f5323fc23f2430e4d5904fb7292647a`.

## Bayview Substation

Bayview was built in 1971 as a transmission and distribution substation. The 2017 filing records three 66 kV transmission lines, two 66/12.5 kV transformers rated 20 MVA and 15 MVA, and five 12.5 kV feeders. The later structure register dates the steel 66 kV and 12.47 kV structures to 1972, so those structure dates remain separate from station construction.

Source: [2018 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/NP2018Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202018%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202017-07-07.PDF), physical PDF p. 231. SHA-256 `d5fe2f1847725348d395edbdd0d34d649d6749c4a00eb4babb15fd1924da59ed`.

## Transmission construction

### 55L

The 66 kV 55L runs from Blaketown to Clarkes Pond and is the sole transmission supply for Placentia Junction, Dunville, Clarkes Pond, and Quartz in the 2022 filing. The line was originally built in 1971 except for a 1.0 km section built in 1968. The filing identifies 43.3 km of original construction.

Source: [2023 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2023/NP2023Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202023%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202022-06-29.PDF), physical PDF p. 440. SHA-256 `be0143cf9814e8573dcb5cdcdfa7fcd213190a5274902d0c5d60becbbf65dd73`.

### TL209

TL209 is a 21 km, 230 kV wood H-frame line between Stephenville and Bottom Brook constructed in 1971. A later system-history filing says it was the second 230 kV line added at Bottom Brook.

## Salt Pond transformer date conflict

The 2002 Burin Peninsula engineering review describes the sole Salt Pond 138/66 kV system transformer as a 1971-vintage, 41.6 MVA unit and says it was installed in 1971. The 2017 station filing says SPO-T4 was installed in 1973. The filings do not establish whether the records describe different equipment states or whether one date is incorrect.

Source: [PUB 10-2A, 2002 Burin Peninsula Engineering Review](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/rfi/PUB%20%2010-2A.pdf), physical PDF pp. 6 and 9. SHA-256 `ad75cb82626e9a1f33b48a5d828d9d83d8ef64dc457638b91ff2b092257647ba`.
