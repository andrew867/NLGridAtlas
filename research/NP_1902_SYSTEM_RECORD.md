# 1902 System Record

## United Towns Electrical Company

The 1937 *Who's Who in and from Newfoundland* says United Towns Electric Company was incorporated in 1902 and began operations in 1904. It identifies Victoria as the company's first hydroelectric plant and says it originally supplied Harbour Grace, Carbonear and Heart's Content.

Source: [*Who's Who in and from Newfoundland 1937*, United Towns Electric Company, Ltd.](https://dai.mun.ca/PDFs/cns_tools/WhosWho1937.pdf), physical PDF p. 304, printed p. 296. SHA-256 `d25fbdaecfdf9f053238d01d9d8f4adac29deb8a1d49143caa69d743f2aeea42`.

Newfoundland Power's current corporate history also dates the company's establishment to 1902. The Canadian Register of Historic Places identifies the 1902 special-act incorporation and dates Victoria station construction and first generation to 1904, with first generation on November 7. The 1902 event is therefore recorded as company formation, not plant commissioning.

Corroborating sources: [Newfoundland Power, *Our Roots Run Deep*](https://www.newfoundlandpower.com/en/About/Who-We-Are/Our-Roots), section "1902: Power expands across the Avalon"; [Canadian Register of Historic Places, Victoria Hydro-Electric Station](https://www.historicplaces.ca/en/rep-reg/place-lieu.aspx?id=4327&pid=0), section "Heritage Value." Both retrieved 2026-11-15.
