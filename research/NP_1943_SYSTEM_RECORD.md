# 1943 System Record

## Rocky Pond Hydro Plant

The 2013 development record dates the Rocky Pond powerhouse and its vertical Francis turbine to 1943. The turbine was manufactured by Dominion Turbine and the generator by Westinghouse. The development record gives 3,200 kW installed capacity under 32.6 m net head and describes a 756 m long, 2,274 mm diameter woodstave penstock.

The 2009 capital filing instead says the plant entered service in 1942. That filing gives 3.25 MW capacity under the same 32.6 m net head, 14.1 GWh normal annual production, a Westinghouse generator manufactured in 1941, and a 12.5 kV forebay distribution and communications line built in 1942. The commissioning year remains unresolved.

## La Manche Canal and Cape Pond Road

The 2014 capital filing dates the La Manche Canal and Rocky Pond Plant to 1943. Water from the canal passes through both Rocky Pond and Tors Cove generating stations.

Cape Pond Road was constructed in 1943 as a 12.6 km gravel route from Route 10 to Cape Pond. The 2020 filing records access to Cape Pond dam, spillway, outlet structure and high-speed canal; Cluney's dam, spillway and control structure; and the La Manche Canal embankment and six spillways.
