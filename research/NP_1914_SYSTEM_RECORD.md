# 1914 System Record

## Victoria hydroelectric generating unit

Newfoundland Power's detailed development record identifies one 450 kW Voith turbine installed at Victoria in 1914. It identifies the generator manufacturer as Westinghouse. The same record dates the Victoria hydroelectric development itself to 1904, so 1914 is recorded as an equipment installation rather than the development's commissioning date.

A separate selected-unit table lists VIC-G at 0.4 MW and gives 1914 as its installation date. The 0.4 MW value is retained as the table's rounded unit rating; it does not replace the detailed record's 450 kW turbine rating.
