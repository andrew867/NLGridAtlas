## Transmission

The line-rebuild report records the following, physical PDF pp. 190 to 197.

- 20L: 66 kV, 20.1 km, Mobile to Cape Broyle, H-frame wood construction, built in 1951. A 7.5 km section was approved for 2007. The filing proposed rebuilding the remaining approximately 13.5 km in 2008 for $1.563 million.
- 111L: 66 kV, 31 km, Lockston to Catalina through Port Union, single wood-pole construction, built in 1956. The filing proposed rebuilding the most deteriorated 13 km in 2008 for $1.482 million.
- The Q4 2008 regulatory report identifies the 20L rebuild as completed, CA-NP-217 physical PDF p. 261.
- The later 2009 filing identifies the 2008 111L section and proposes the remaining 17.7 km. The 2009 third-quarter report identifies the final section as complete.

The filed 20L route sheet is preserved at `public/reference-maps/20l-2008-route.png` from physical PDF p. 196.

## Hydro generation and civil work

The facility-rehabilitation report gives the following filed 2008 work, physical PDF pp. 149 to 159.

- Port Union, Whirl Pond: replace the timber crib dam and outlet structure for $275,000 combined.
- Fall Pond: rehabilitate the main dam for $110,000.
- Seal Cove, Soldiers Pond: replace the timber crib dam and overflow spillway for $250,000.
- Horse Chops and Cape Broyle, Mount Carmel Pond: embankment, vegetation, gabion and outlet-headwall work for $200,000.
- Petty Harbour, Cochrane Pond: riprap and spillway-channel work for $50,000.
- Cape Broyle: modernize governor, generator protection, voltage regulation, switchgear, plant control and SCADA equipment for $750,000.
- Heart's Content: replace the damaged mild-steel runner and wicket gates with stainless-steel equipment and self-lubricating bushings for $700,000.
- Pierre's Brook: replace governor controls and the leaking power piston for $225,000.
- Mobile: replace worn wicket-gate bushings for $200,000. The filing records the 12 MW unit as derated to 10.5 MW by the mechanical limitation.
- Rocky Pond: complete $75,000 of engineering for the later penstock and plant project.

## Fibre network

The fibre report records the operating communications network and the proposed replacement program, physical PDF pp. 228 to 234.

- Newfoundland Power built its first fibre circuits in 1990.
- The 2008 filing lists 32 circuits, comprising 16 company-built, 15 leased and one rented circuit.
- The circuits carried corporate data, substation data, voice, SCADA, protective relay communications and control-centre data exchanged with Newfoundland and Labrador Hydro.
- The filing proposed two 2008 builds, System Control Centre to Hydro Place and Virginia Waters to Ridge Road, followed by fourteen circuits from 2009 through 2011.
- A later reconciliation states that two selected leased circuits were replaced, two leases were abandoned without replacement and one route could not be secured. Those later outcomes remain separate from the 2008 proposal.

## Completion boundary

The 2008 filing establishes proposed work. Later sources are used only where they identify an asset or project outcome. Pooled expenditure totals are not treated as station-level commissioning records.
