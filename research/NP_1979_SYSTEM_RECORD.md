## Telecommunications

Hydro installed the original West Coast Microwave Radio System as ten microwave hops for the Hinds Lake and Upper Salmon developments. It carried operational voice and data to the Provincial Control Centre at Bay d'Espoir. Transmission-line teleprotection remained on power-line carrier because microwave teleprotection was not then considered proven.

Source: [Telecommunication Plan](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd2003B/files/applic/AppSectionH.pdf), physical PDF pp. 10 and 151. SHA-256 `7dc7f33d73d0f2a2175cb3ba153a0551369a931ce6232d5a83e22a1f0de54e24`.

## Holyrood Unit 3

The electrical records establish 1979 equipment milestones:

- The Stage 2 relay panels were installed in 1978 and commissioned in spring 1979.
- The Unit 3 generator bus is a Westinghouse three-phase isolated-phase bus installed in 1979.
- The generator, T3, ST3/ST4 and auxiliary protection and control equipment was installed in 1979.
- The 258 VDC distribution panel, original dry excitation transformer, RT3 rectifier transformer and field breaker were installed in 1979.
- The transformer now designated T1 was first installed at the T3 position in 1979. It is rated 105/140/180 MVA, 230/16 kV, star-delta, ONAN/ONAF/OFAF, with positive-sequence impedance of 13.6% and zero-sequence impedance of 11.7%. It moved to T1 in 1988. The present T3 was first installed at T1 in 1970 and moved to T3 in 1978.

Sources: [2011 Capital Budget Application, Volume I](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2011Capital/files/application/NLH2011ApplicationComplete-VolumeI.pdf), physical PDF p. 918, SHA-256 `d02cac731457ffa45ea1cf8f93af64626f5323fc23f2430e4d5904fb7292647a`; [Holyrood Condition Assessment and Life Extension Study](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2012Capital/files/reports/AMEC-Report-Jan28-11.pdf), physical PDF pp. 379, 405 to 406 and 547, SHA-256 `2d1e6ed46e36cd95a97855a6fbb50704d89dacda044d7460d209ab407a04899d`.

The broader Unit 3 commissioning year remains unresolved. The relay-panel filing says Stage 2 came online in spring 1979. A separate overhaul filing says the turbine-generator was installed in 1979 and commissioned in 1980. Later Hydro summaries commonly use 1979.
