## Line and station chronology

Hydro's system-design response gives the following connection dates:

- Bottom Brook entered service in 1967 with TL211 as its first 230 kV connection.
- TL209 was added from Bottom Brook to Stephenville in 1971.
- TL233 was added from Bottom Brook to Buchans in 1973.
- A fourth 230 kV breaker completed the Bottom Brook ring bus in 1978.
- Buchans entered service in 1973. The TL232 and TL233 terminations were swapped in the late 1980s.
- Stony Brook entered service in 1966 as a load-bus station and was converted to a 230 kV ring bus in 1995.

Source: [PUB-NLH-318](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-318.pdf), physical PDF pp. 3 to 4.

## TL228 storm and strengthening record

The 1996 reliability study records TL228 as a 230 kV steel line commissioned in 1967 with a filed length of 84 km.

- December 1979: structure 73 was replaced.
- April 1983: structures 90 and 91 were replaced, new conductor was installed, and the conductor was re-sagged.
- December 1987: structures 68 to 75 received CAC towers and new conductor. Follow-up work used shorter spans, additional dead-end structures, and new conductor.
- 1990: mid-span structures were added between structures 50 and 111.

Source: [Reliability Study of Transmission Lines on Avalon and Connaigre Peninsulas](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit85.pdf), physical PDF pp. 158 to 160.

Hydro's separate storm response records a 1980 structure failure repaired in ten days, conductor contact with ground in 1981, and seven failed structures with broken conductor in 1987. The 1987 restoration took 63 days, with service maintained through TL233 and TL211.

Source: [PUB-NLH-299](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-299.pdf), physical PDF pp. 1 to 3.

## TL228 study uprating

The 2016 Maritime Link runback study permits TL228 to operate at a 55 °C hot-conductor temperature. The study states that Hydro's transmission and distribution analysis found conductor sag limits would not be violated at 55 °C because of high conductor stringing tension. This is a study operating assumption.

Under the study assumptions, TL228 limited all-lines-in-service summer Maritime Link export to 470 MW. The study also identifies TL228 as the limiting line after loss of TL233 in the spring and fall case.

Source: [PUB-NLH-565](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-565.pdf), physical PDF pp. 124 to 126.
