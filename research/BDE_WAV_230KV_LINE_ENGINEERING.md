# Bay d'Espoir to Western Avalon 230 kV circuit record

## Current circuit topology

The 2018 filed line table places TL202 and TL206 between Bay d'Espoir and Sunnyside, TL203 between Sunnyside and Western Avalon, TL207 between Sunnyside and Come By Chance, TL237 between Come By Chance and Western Avalon, and TL267 directly between Bay d'Espoir and Western Avalon.

Source: PUB-NP-084 Attachment A, physical PDF p. 26, `http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/2018ratemitigation/responses/PUB-NP-084.PDF`.

A later filing gives 1968 as the in-service year of each current circuit and records both as rebuilt in 2002 for a three-inch ice load. | 1996 reliability study, physical PDF pp. 53 to 54; 2012 corridor application, physical PDF pp. 11 to 12 |

## Recorded peak-load series

The 2012 filing gives the following corridor loads at the Island system peak, derived from hourly Energy Management System data.

| Year | Bay d'Espoir east | Bay d'Espoir west |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2006 | 836 MW | 431 MW |
| 2007 | 848 MW | 450 MW |
| 2008 | 827 MW | 436 MW |
| 2009 | 858 MW | 428 MW |
| 2010 | 833 MW | 378 MW |

The filing defines the east value as TL202 and TL206 flow plus Paradise River, Holyrood, and Hardwoods generation. It defines the west value as TL204 and TL231 flow plus Cat Arm, Hinds Lake, Stephenville, Hawke's Bay, St. Anthony Diesel, and non-utility generation purchases. Connaigre Peninsula loads are excluded.

Source: 2012 corridor application, physical PDF p. 4.

## 2026 proposed work

The 2026 preliminary engineering report recommends dynamic line rating for TL201, TL202, TL203, and TL206 with a third Western Avalon to Soldiers Pond circuit as the least-cost option evaluated for a Labrador-Island Link bipole outage. The statement is a recommendation, not an installed-equipment record.

Source: Western Avalon Transmission Expansion, physical PDF p. 27, `http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/reports/From%20NLH%20-%20Transmission%20Feasibility%20Study%20-%202026-04-17.PDF`.
