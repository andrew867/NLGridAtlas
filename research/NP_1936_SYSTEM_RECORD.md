# 1936 System Search

## Unassigned circuit breaker record

Newfoundland Power's equipment ledger lists company number 210043 as a circuit breaker purchased in 1936. The row gives serial number LSORT24, 33,000 V and manufacturer Reyrolle. The filed table does not identify the station, circuit or operating role.

Archive searches for company number 210043 and serial number LSORT24 return only this ledger row. The record is preserved here, but it is not assigned to a public station history or timeline event.

Source: [CA-NP-088](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf), physical PDF p. 214, SHA-256 `12ea7123d3f8b84f30e0f0cf445f5631fdb412b907e6d33588d2765807280b0d`.

## Archive result

Exact-year searches covered generation, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, hydro civil works, property deeds and grants, transmission lines, circuits, feeders, telecommunications, system demand, reliability, storms and outages.

The other returned records were aggregate accounting transactions, depreciation study bands, market and interest-rate series beginning in 1936, legal citations, modern operating times, engineering calculations containing the number 1936, and external utility records. A 2015 penstock report states that untreated woodstave penstocks were constructed before 1936, but it does not identify a 1936 installation.

No named Newfoundland or Labrador grid event can be assigned specifically to 1936. The atlas therefore has no 1936 public card or timeline entry.

## Search rule

The breaker cannot be attached to a station without a station assignment. A later primary record can reopen this queue if it identifies the location or role of 210043 or LSORT24.
