# 1966 Island System Record

No published demand series located in this archive extends to 1966. MHI-Nalcor-92 begins in 1967, so this record does not state a 1966 peak.

## Hydro transmission

PUB-NLH-316 dates six circuits to 1966:

- TL201, Western Avalon to Hardwoods, 80.678 km, 230 kV, H-frame wood.
- TL202, Bay d'Espoir to Sunnyside, 141.758 km, 230 kV, guyed V steel.
- TL204, Bay d'Espoir to Stony Brook, 105.021 km, 230 kV, guyed V steel.
- TL212, Sunnyside to Monkstown, 57.7059 km; Monkstown to Bay L'Argent, 48.0727 km; Bay L'Argent to Linton Lake, 32.877 km. All three sections are 138 kV, guyed Y or H-frame wood.
- TL223, Springdale to Indian River, 29.664 km, 138 kV, H-frame wood.
- TL235, Stony Brook to Grand Falls Frequency Converter, 0.62 km, 230 kV, steel tower.

## Terminal stations

PUB-NLH-318 records:

- Bay d'Espoir's original 230 kV main-and-transfer bus was commissioned in 1965 to 1966. It was converted into ring buses in 1993.
- Stony Brook was commissioned in 1966 as a 230 kV load bus. A separate 2013 filing says it had been in service since 1967.
- Sunnyside was originally commissioned in 1965 to 1966. Its 138 kV ring bus dates to 1967. A separate 2013 filing says the station had been in service since 1967.
- Western Avalon was commissioned in 1966 as a 230 kV load bus. It was converted into a ring bus in 1993 to 1994.

The Stony Brook and Sunnyside dates remain unresolved because the filed records conflict.

## Newfoundland Power stations

Later station filings date these facilities to 1966:

- Gambo, built as a transmission and distribution substation. The filed arrangement has 121L from Glovertown, 146L from Gander, GAM-T1 supplying two 25 kV feeders, and GAM-T2 supplying the 66 kV radial system toward Hare Bay, Trinity, Greenspond and Wesleyville.
- Memorial, originally constructed in 1966 with jointly owned equipment. MUN-T1 is a Ferranti-Packard 66/12.5 kV unit manufactured in 1966 and rated 11.125/14.83 MVA in the 2023 filing.
- Harbour Grace, built as a transmission and distribution substation. The filed arrangement has 57L to Upper Island Cove, 68L to Carbonear, two 66/12.5 kV transformers and three feeders.
- Victoria, built as a generation and distribution substation. VIC-T1 supplies the 12.5 kV distribution bus and VIC-T2 connects the 2.4 kV hydro generator.
- Walbournes, built as a transmission and distribution substation. The filed arrangement has 351L and 352L from Massey Drive, 353L to Frenchman's Cove, two 66/12.5 kV transformers and six feeders.
- Lookout Brook, built as a generation substation. The filed arrangement has generators G3 and G4, LBK-T1 and 403L to Robinsons.

## Hardwoods and Holyrood gas turbine

Hydro purchased the Rolls-Royce Avon gas-turbine engine, AEI power turbine and AEI generator in 1966. It was first installed at Hardwoods for emergency and peak-load service, then moved to Holyrood during the initial plant construction for black-start duty. This machine is separate from the later Olympus and Curtiss-Wright Hardwoods gas-turbine installation.

## Farewell Head and Fogo distribution

The filing dates the Farewell Head distribution system to 1966:

- L4, 12.5 kV three-phase to Fogo, 11.9 km.
- L5, 12.5 kV three-phase to Shoal Cove, Barr'd Island and Joe Batt's Arm, 12.4 km; a 4.4 km single-phase branch supplied Sandy Cove and Tilting.
- L6, 12.5 kV three-phase to Seldom, Little Seldom and Stag Harbour, 19.4 km; a 4.5 km two-phase branch supplied Deep Bay, with 1.9 km and 7.4 km single-phase branches.

The filing records about 1,500 customers at the time of the later project.
