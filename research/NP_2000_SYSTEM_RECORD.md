## System work reported for 2000

- A new St. Catherines substation was constructed for $0.5 million.
- Gander Bay substation was rebuilt for approximately $0.4 million.
- A 12 km distribution line was completed in the Bay de Verde area.
- A new 20 km distribution line was constructed at Blaketown.
- A 25 km distribution-line section near Robinsons was upgraded.
- A 12 km distribution-line section at Doyles was relocated.
- The feeder-rebuild program installed 1,055 poles and rebuilt approximately 550 structures for $2.7 million.
- Four-year insulator and transformer programs finished after replacing approximately 50,000 insulators and 3,000 transformers.
- The Tors Cove surge tank and Horse Chops steel penstock were replaced for a combined $3 million.

The report describes the Bay de Verde work by area and does not identify a station or feeder designation. The atlas therefore records it in the dated system timeline rather than attaching it to a station.

## Generation

Newfoundland Power produced 415 GWh from its hydro plants in 2000 at 88 percent plant availability. A Hydro Plant Production Study recommended changing the normal-inflow production estimate from 450 GWh to 423 GWh.

Rose Blanche produced 28 GWh against a 23 GWh target and supplied the isolated Port aux Basques load on several occasions.

## Control and metering systems

The annual report records 56 of 59 scheduled remote terminal units at substations and hydro plants connected to SCADA. A separate 200-customer automated-meter-reading pilot used radio-frequency meter reading. New electronic meters were also installed at several hydro plants.

## Reliability

## Asset-base record

At December 31, 2000, the fixed-asset table reports transmission and substations at $176.144 million cost, $78.844 million accumulated depreciation and $97.300 million net book value.
