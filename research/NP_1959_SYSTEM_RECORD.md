# 1959 System Record

## Substations

### Gander

Gander Substation was built in 1959 as a distribution station. It later developed into a major transmission and distribution station. The 2024 filing records 144L from Cobb's Pond and 146L from Gambo at 138 kV, GAN-T1 at 20 MVA supplying four 12.5 kV feeders, and GAN-T2 at 26.67 MVA supplying the 66 kV system toward Gander Bay, Jonathan's Pond and Roycefield.

Source: [2025 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 477 to 480](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF#page=477), SHA-256 `4d7af5a441cbb7cc82e98766e482afcf59b0ef7cc03497ffab852a5a90c71dd3`.

### Grand Falls

Grand Falls Substation was built in 1959 as a 66/4.16 kV distribution station. The 2010 filing records its later arrangement as a 138 kV and 66 kV transmission station with 25 kV and 4 kV distribution, three transformers totalling 48.4 MVA, and two yards separated by the Trans-Canada Highway.

Source: [2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 294](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf#page=294), SHA-256 `e3e004d1aadd10be1624cc33b402203eb60c744f24e4736d1219b94e41579cb2`.

### Rattling Brook

Rattling Brook Substation was built in 1959 as a transmission, distribution and generation station. The 2027 filing records two generators, 8.25 MVA and 7.5 MVA, connected to the 66 kV bus through RBK-T1 at 15/20 MVA and 66/6.9 kV. RBK-T2 was 5 MVA at 66/12.5 kV and supplied RBK-01. RBK-T3, installed in 2021, was 15/20/25 MVA at 138/66 kV.

The hydroelectric development date is unresolved. The 2013 plant history says the development was commissioned in 1958, and the 2005 plant assessment gives December 16, 1958. The 2021 capital filing says the development was constructed in 1959. The 2013 and 2021 records also publish different plant capacities, 11.5 MW and 14.8 MW respectively.

## Transmission and distribution

### St. John's 66 kV lines

30L connected Ridge Road and King's Bridge. The 2.9 km line used 87 single-pole structures, all with distribution underbuild. The 2016 filing identifies Canadian Ohio Brass insulators affected by cement growth.

The surviving original 35L section between Oxen Pond and Kenmount comprised 3.39 km and 39 single-pole wood structures. Three poles failed during Tropical Storm Leslie in 2012.

The 69L construction year is unresolved. The 2015 filing summary gives 1951, while its project text says the Kenmount to Stamp's Lane line was originally constructed in 1959. The rebuild scope covered 2.74 km and 48 single-pole structures, about one third with distribution underbuild.

### Burin Peninsula

Sections of 301L were built in 1959 and 1966. The filing does not divide the two construction vintages by route segment. The 2003 rebuild covered approximately 38 km and addressed deteriorated poles and conductor damaged by salt, wind and ice exposure.

302L connected Salt Pond and Laurentian at 66 kV. The original 1959 line included approximately 26.6 km and 315 single-pole structures with 4/0 ACSR. A separate 2.4 km section entering Laurentian was built in 1974.

### BOT-01

BOT-01 was built as a 25 kV feeder in the Botwood area. Extensions to Leading Tickles and Fortune Harbour followed in 1965. The 2006 filing records approximately 199 km and 1,607 customers. The three-phase main ran from Botwood to Northern Arm, then divided toward Point of Bay and Point Leamington, with single-phase extensions to Fortune Harbour and Leading Tickles.

Source: [2006 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 387 and 393 to 401](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2006cap/files/applic/NP2006ApplicationComplete.pdf#page=393), SHA-256 `7ce9f6671c827eab339c0e6c47caae3a807d99fa2458410c370d1ba7d3f8d485`.

## Hydroelectric generation and civil works

### Pitmans Pond

Pitmans Pond was commissioned in 1959 at 625 kW under approximately 21.3 m net head. The 2013 application identifies a horizontal 1,200 hp Gilkes Francis turbine and a Westinghouse 1,000 kVA induction generator rated for an 850 kW load at 85 percent power factor. The development record lists the turbine at 895 kW. The plant connected to New Chelsea through the 12.5 kV NCH-03 feeder.

The 385 m woodstave penstock was 1,372 mm in diameter. The steel head gate was raised and lowered by two steel cables connected to a manual drum-style lifting mechanism.

### Heart's Content and Victoria penstocks

Heart's Content received a 21 m, 1,829 mm steel penstock section between the 558 m woodstave section and the powerhouse. At Victoria, 238 m of the 412 m woodstave penstock was replaced in 1959. The remaining 174 m was replaced in 1963; a separate 82 m steel section was installed in 1986.

## Gallant Street Building

The Gallant Street Building was constructed in 1959 and expanded in 1970. It housed protection and control equipment for the adjacent Gallant Street Substation and stored regional operating equipment and materials.

Source: [2020 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 509](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2020/NP2020Capital/applications/NP%20-%202020%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202019-07-05.PDF#page=509), SHA-256 `eb12a773e3d81aa1cfc7b88aae339cfdb009a91fedd54088d9252d1f56366b47`.

## Historic dry sequence

Hydro defines April 1959 through December 1962 as its historic dry sequence, the longest sustained low-inflow period in its hydrological record. The response states that individual months or quarters elsewhere had lower inflows.

Source: [IC-NLH-039, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/IC-NLH-039.pdf#page=1), SHA-256 `a34b3734060b4dfc4b6098916f42ade63e8358adf18d2ace3c5e1441c24c36c3`.
