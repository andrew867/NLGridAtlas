# 1934 System Search

## Unassigned property vintages

Newfoundland Power's depreciation records contain two exact-year property vintages from 1934:

- Account 323.00, Canals, Penstocks, Surge Tanks, and Tailraces, retains $13,454 of original cost.
- The combined wire, duplex, and services account records a $10,168 addition, increasing its balance from $6,049 to $16,217.

The filed schedules do not identify a generating plant, water-control structure, circuit, community or work order for either vintage.

## Archive result

Exact-year searches covered generation, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, hydro civil works, property records, transmission lines, circuits, feeders, telecommunications, system demand, reliability, storms and outages.

The other returned records were United States securities-law references, market series beginning in 1934, aggregate accounting rows, hydrology series, modern operating times, engineering calculations containing the number 1934, and external utility records. No named Newfoundland or Labrador grid event can be assigned specifically to 1934.

The atlas therefore has no 1934 public card or timeline entry.

## Search rule

Neither vintage can be attached to an asset without a plant ledger, work order or other source that identifies its location. A later primary record can reopen this queue if it supplies that link.
