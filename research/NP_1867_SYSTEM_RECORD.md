# 1867 System Record

## Proposed duplicate route and cable

On February 26, 1867, the House received a petition from the directors of the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company. The petition proposed:

- a second overland telegraph line from St. John's to Port Hood;
- a submarine cable from Placentia to the continent of America;
- an overland connection from Heart's Content to Placentia.

The petition stated that the company had expended its authorized capital of $3 million and sought authority for more capital. These routes were proposed. The petition does not establish construction or completion.

## St. John's to Trepassey line removed

The House committee report dated March 15, 1867 records Alexander M. McKay's evidence that the telegraph line between St. John's and Trepassey had been taken down at the company's instance. The committee stated that the charter required this line to be maintained and worked, and that its removal violated that condition without warrant.

The report establishes the condition reported to the House by March 15. It does not give the removal date.

## Capital authorization

Chapter 17, passed April 26, 1867, increased the company's authorized capital from $3 million to $6 million. Its preamble states that the additional capital was sought to construct new land telegraph lines and a submarine cable between Placentia, or another part of Newfoundland, and the continent of America. The operative section changes the authorized capital. It does not identify a construction contract, route survey, start date or completion date.

## Pole position near Salmonier

A Board of Works report dated December 31, 1867 describes telegraph poles between Half-way House and Salmonier Bridge. It states that some poles were no more than five feet from the centre of the road. This is a field condition reported for 1867, not a pole-installation date.
