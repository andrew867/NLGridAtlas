## St. John's Main underground distribution

The 2009 arrangement had 12 distribution feeders leaving St. John's Main by underground cable. Nine passed through the Waterford River duct bank. SJM-03, SJM-07 and SJM-08 formed the Water Street underground trunk. The filing records that an N-1 loss of SJM-03 or SJM-08 under high load would prevent full service to the downtown core.

The filed project relocated SJM-03 between Hutchings Street and Beck's Cove into the south-side Water Street and Harbour Drive duct banks. It specified three 1,100 m single-phase 500 MCM XLPE cables and a five-compartment 12.5 kV padmount switch interconnecting SJM-03, SJM-07 and SJM-08. The filed cost was $550,000. Source: 2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 364 to 374. The later completion record concerns SJM-08, so this SJM-03 scope remains filed rather than installed.

## Lookout Brook electrical refurbishment

The 2009 plant record identifies LBK-G3 as a 1958 unit and LBK-G4 as a 1984 unit. It gives 6.2 MW plant capacity at 154.6 m net head and 30.1 GWh normal annual production. The connection to the Island system used 403L and 407L to Stephenville Gas Turbine Substation.

The 2010 electrical scope included high-impedance generator neutral grounding, arc-flash-rated switchgear, digital generator relays, bus differential and arc-flash protection, ControlLogix plant control, gateway communications, digital voltage regulation, emergency station service, larger AC and DC distribution, and a gel-cell battery bank. Source: 2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 183 to 193.

The 2024 filing lists the 2010 control-room extension, governor upgrades, plant-control upgrades, switchgear replacement, and AC, DC and battery-bank upgrades as completed. Source: [2024 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2024/NP2024Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202024%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202023-06-22.PDF), physical PDF pp. 439 to 440.

## Fibre circuit program

The 2010 filing recorded 32 fibre optic systems made up of owned, leased and rented facilities and a $244,000 replacement program. Source: 2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 99.

The later reconciliation states that five leased circuits were selected for 2010. Two were replaced, two leases were abandoned without replacement, and one cable was not built because a satisfactory route between substations was not secured. Six of the original 16 leased circuits remained at the end of 2010. Source: [2012 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2012/NP2012Capital/files/applic/NP2012ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 93 and 169.

## Substation records

The filed 2010 substation program totalled $4.043 million. Its station table allocated $837,000 to Botwood, $67,000 to Boyd's Cove, $228,000 to Clarkes Pond, $703,000 to Gallant Street, $138,000 to Gillams, $199,000 to Glovertown, $797,000 to Grand Falls, $138,000 to Springfield, $53,000 to Wesleyville, $540,000 to portable substation P3, $193,000 to smaller work, and $150,000 to monitoring and operations. Source: 2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 285 to 302.

The Boyd's Cove work added station-service transformer lightning arresters and extended the grounding system. The Springfield work replaced deteriorated transformer radiators and approximately 35-year-old compound-leaking 12 kV cables. Later project-history records identify the completed work at Clarkes Pond, Glovertown and Grand Falls.

P3 was purchased in 1977 and could back up 68% of the approximately 190 Newfoundland Power transformers then in service. The 2010 application proposed its first comprehensive refurbishment. The next filing records that P3 had to remain available for service, so engineering continued in 2010 and the refurbishment moved to the winter of 2010/2011 with a $440,000 budget. Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 174, 287 and 291.

## Sandy Lake storage project

The Sandy Lake project raised the spillway by 1.0 m. The filing estimated 0.86 GWh of additional annual energy production at Sandy Brook and a cost of $612,000. Source: 2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 30 and 254 to 264. A later RFI states that environmental approvals delayed the project and that it was completed in the 2011 capital program. Source: [CA-NP-011](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/rfi/CA-NP-011.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.
