## Reliability and system work

CA-446Rev reports 1997 SAIFI of 2.72 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 3.73 hours per customer, excluding Hydro loss-of-supply outages. The annual report says customer interruption frequency and duration increased slightly, primarily because of supply interruptions late in the year.

The annual report records the continuing 12-year insulator replacement program. It states that more than 70,000 defective insulators were scheduled for replacement by 2001 at a cost of $10 million. Newfoundland Power also increased infrared thermoscanning of its transmission and distribution systems and used power-monitoring equipment to identify customer power-quality problems.

The report does not publish a 1997 service-availability percentage, operating-area SAIFI and SAIDI table, or system load factor. The SCADA reference on physical PDF p. 13 describes a planned 1998 employee placement in SCADA design, development and implementation. It does not establish an installed 1997 SCADA replacement.

## Rose Blanche construction

More than $13 million had been allocated to the Rose Blanche Brook hydro plant. The filed rating was 6.1 MW. The report describes the project as Newfoundland Power's first new generating facility in 14 years and states that it was intended to improve supply reliability in southwestern Newfoundland. The plant remained under construction in this record and was energized on December 22, 1998.

## Operating and financial record

The ten-year summary reports 424 million kWh generated, 4,244 million kWh purchased and a 1,065 MW peak one-hour demand in 1997. The normalized regulatory return reports 424.2 GWh produced, 4,243.5 GWh purchased, 4,450.2 GWh sold and used, and 217.5 GWh of losses, equal to 4.7 percent.

The capital-expenditure return reports $30.965 million of actual 1997 expenditure. The technical categories include $2.102 million for substations, $1.855 million for transmission, $11.580 million for distribution and $0.562 million for telecommunications.

At December 31, 1997, transmission and substations carried $162.864 million cost, $70.837 million accumulated depreciation, $0.170 million construction in progress and $92.197 million net book value.
