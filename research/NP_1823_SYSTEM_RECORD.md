# 1823 System Record

## Hibernia packet communication

The January 29, 1824 *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* prints a notice dated September 18, 1823 on physical PDF p. 3. James Dooley, packet master, advertised *Hibernia* plying between Portugal Cove and Harbour Grace. The boat was ready when required to carry packages or passengers to Harbour Grace.

Letters and packages for Harbour Grace could be left at the *Mercantile Journal* office or James Holly's Sign of the Keg in St. John's. The packet master called for them and undertook their delivery.

## Dated boundaries

The *Lively* packet notice repeated in the surviving 1823 issues is dated November 13, 1822. It belongs to the 1822 record.

The April 17, 1823 issue prints a London court report about an ordnance-yard fire and military response in St. John's in 1819. The report belongs with the 1819 chronology. The surviving report does not give the fire's date.

## Newspaper search

The review covered all 14 surviving 1823 files listed in the MUN DAI *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* collection, comprising 54 physical PDF pages. The files run from January 2 to April 17. Issue numbers 465 and 466 are absent from the online collection, and the collection lists no later 1823 issue.

The MUN DAI *Royal Gazette* collection has no digitized 1823 issue. Its online run ends in April 1818 and resumes in January 1828. The MUN DAI main newspaper list and limited-run newspaper list identify no other digitized collection covering 1823. These online collection gaps do not establish that no other 1823 newspaper existed or survives elsewhere.
