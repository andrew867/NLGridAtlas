## Mobile gas turbine

The filed record says the Mobile gas turbine entered service in 1974. It describes a 6,750 kW combustion turbine assembled on two trailers. One trailer carried the Orenda OT-390 Model 2G gas generator, Orenda OT-3 Model 6 power turbine, gearbox and AC generator. The other carried switchgear, controls, batteries and chargers, a transformer, recloser and auxiliary power unit.

The unit later became permanently stationed at Grand Bay because its trailer chassis had deteriorated. Other filings print 6 MW, 7.2 MW or 7.3 MW ratings. The atlas keeps those dated published values separate.

Source: [NLH-NP-079](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022GRA/responses/NLH-NP-079.PDF), physical PDF pp. 1 and 13. SHA-256 `3bb4d79554f88270920bbc754c8137a9309287641e76148c21ee2be1b0c638aa`.

## Come By Chance terminal assets

The specifically assigned asset register estimates Come By Chance Terminal Station in service in 1973. It separately dates a large group of individual assets to January 1, 1974. These include two 230 kV power transformers, bus work, four named disconnects, the control building, compressed-air equipment, post and suspension insulators, power and control cable, and steel support structure.

The asset dates do not establish a 1974 construction date for the whole terminal.

Source: [IC-NLH-097](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013GRA/files/rfi/IC-NLH-097.PDF), physical PDF p. 19. SHA-256 `0bc2fb7c21c8486638a2e822c71c21313fd6dabd62d8a6e3a9a24006c05b1d5c`.

## Exclusions

- A later filing says L'Anse au Loup and St. Anthony were constructed in 1974 and 1970, respectively. The 1974 date belongs to the Labrador plant, not St. Anthony, and is outside this Island record.
- A Humber transformer manufactured in 1974 was installed at Humber in 1984. Manufacture date alone is not treated as a 1974 system addition.
