# 1832 System Record

## Chronology boundary

Parks Canada Manuscript Report 386 dates the grant of representative government to 1832. It then dates the Governor's technical case for better navigation aids to January 3, 1833, and the lighthouse enabling act to June 12, 1834. Physical PDF pp. 43 to 44 do not identify an 1832 lighthouse authorization, construction project or operating event.

The report's general statement that the legislature acted after representative government does not move the later dated actions into 1832. The atlas therefore adds no 1832 technical timeline event.

## Newspaper search

Sixteen available November and December issues of the *Newfoundlander* and the *Times and General Commercial Gazette* were downloaded and searched. The scan covered eight issues from each title. It was not a complete review of every 1832 issue. OCR was checked for lighthouse, Cape Spear, Fort Amherst, mooring, telegraph, electricity, galvanism and related terms. No named 1832 Newfoundland telegraph instrument, electrical installation, lighthouse project or operating circuit was located.
