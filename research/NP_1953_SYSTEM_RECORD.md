# 1953 System Record

## St. John's Diesel and Steam plants

The St. John's Diesel Plant and St. John's Steam Plant were built in 1953 on the south side of St. John's Harbour. The diesel plant supplied station services for starting the steam plant.

The diesel plant contained one 2,500 kW Nordberg engine and General Electric generator. The data sheet lists 3,125 kVA, 6,600 V, 274 A, 0.80 power factor, 60 Hz, 3,580 bhp, and 225 rpm. The plant report describes three 1,500 kVA transformers stepping the generator supply from 6.9 kV to 4.16 kV. A distribution line connected the plant directly to St. John's Main; no transmission line was associated with it.

The plant report says the steam plant was decommissioned in the late 1990s, but another section says it shut down around 1988. Those statements are retained as separate later status records rather than used as a single retirement date.

## Cape Broyle and Horse Chops

The commissioning year is unresolved. The 2013 development record dates the Cape Broyle and Horse Chops development and both turbines to 1953. The 2025 capital filing dates both plants to 1954. The conflict is documented in the 1954 system record and remains unresolved.

## Blackwoods Pond Reservoir

Blackwoods Pond Reservoir was constructed in 1953 as part of the Cape Broyle and Horse Chops development. The 2003 undertaking proposed $200,000 of rehabilitation and estimated that work would extend the dam and structure service life by approximately 15 years.

Source: [U-2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/undertaking/U-2.pdf), physical PDF p. 1. SHA-256 `2a898d56928ba539ce6397b779f74eb157d27bed277426f9b0a0eea6f431a3cc`.

## Horse Chops spillway

The Horse Chops spillway was constructed in 1953 as part of the original hydro development. The 2015 filing describes one large stop-log bay and 11 smaller bays. The smaller bays used 150 mm stop logs removed manually with hooks; the large bay used 300 mm stop logs and a mechanical lift.
