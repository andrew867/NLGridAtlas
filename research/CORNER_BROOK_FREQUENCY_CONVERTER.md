# Corner Brook frequency converter record

Work item: `CONV-001`  
Evidence date: 2026-11-11

## Scope

This record supports the historical converter diagram in the atlas. The drawing is the operating arrangement filed with the 2006 Capital Budget Application. Later records establish equipment condition, planned work, and the 2018 sale and abandonment approval. No located source establishes the current post-sale arrangement.

## Historical operating arrangement

| Item | Filed record | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Converter | Westinghouse rotating 50 Hz to 60 Hz motor-generator set, rated 25 MVA. | Condition assessment, physical PDF pp. 4 to 6 and 34. |
| 50 Hz interface | Deer Lake Power's 66 kV, 50 Hz system connects through T1 to the 6.6 kV, 50 Hz side of the rotating set. | Condition assessment, physical PDF p. 34. |
| 60 Hz interface | The 6.6 kV, 60 Hz side connects through T2 to the 66 kV, 60 Hz bus and L17 toward Massey Drive. | Condition assessment, physical PDF p. 34. |
| Main transformers | T1 and T2 are each rated 21/28 MVA, 66/6.6 kV. | Condition assessment, physical PDF p. 34. |
| Starting equipment | The filed diagram shows a starting motor and phase reversing switch associated with the rotating set. | Condition assessment, physical PDF p. 34. |
| System function | Loss of the converter reduced the ability to use Deer Lake Power's 50 Hz generation and required more 60 Hz supply from Hydro. | PUB-63, physical PDF p. 1. |

The atlas omits breaker, disconnect, grounding-transformer, surge-protection, and station-service detail from the simplified diagram. Those devices remain visible in the source drawing.

## Dated equipment record

| Date | Record | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2002-02-20 | T2 suffered an internal fault. It was removed, rewound, and returned to service. The filing says the rewound unit could be used on the 50 Hz side if T1 failed. | Condition assessment, physical PDF p. 22. |
| 2005 filing | The assessment identified original rotor insulation concerns, T1 condition, and problems with the auto-synchronizer, controls, voltage regulation, and starting system. | Condition assessment, physical PDF pp. 4 to 6 and 9. |
| 2014 | A loose rotor wedge was found. | 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 183. |
| 2015 assessment | Siemens recommended limiting operation to 19 MVA until both synchronous machines were upgraded. The filing gives 25 MW as maximum output. | 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 183. |
| 2017 to 2018 project | Proposed work included rotor wedging, stator rewinding, cleaning, exciter refurbishment, bellows replacement, and transformer-vault ventilation. The filed amounts are $194,600 for 2017 and $2,749,200 for 2018, totaling $2,943,800. | 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 182 to 183. |
| 2018-08-09 | The Board approved sale to Corner Brook Pulp and Paper for $1 and approved Hydro's abandonment of the asset. | Order P.U. 26(2018), physical PDF pp. 1 to 3. |

The 2018 order states that the mill advised Hydro in November 2017 that it was converting its remaining 50 Hz generation to 60 Hz and would need the converter only as backup. The order does not establish completion of that conversion or provide a post-sale one-line.
