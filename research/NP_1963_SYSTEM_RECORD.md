# 1963 Island System Record

The published MHI-Nalcor historical-demand table begins in 1967. No 1963 demand value was located in the reviewed archive.

## 363L

The line was constructed in 1963 between Baie Verte Junction and Seal Cove Road. The 2018 filing records:

- 138 kV H-frame construction.
- Approximately 62 km of original construction.
- 478 two-pole and three-pole H-frame structures.
- Non-standard 266.8 ACSR conductor.
- Radial operation as the only supply to Newfoundland Power and Hydro customers on the Baie Verte Peninsula.

## 32L

The 2017 filing records a 3.2 km, 66 kV single-pole line between Ridge Road and Oxen Pond. It was constructed in 1963. At the time of filing, 1.4 km and 35 original single-pole structures remained. Some structures carried distribution underbuild.

## Ridge Road date conflict

The 2007 filing states that Ridge Road was built in 1963 as a 66 kV switching station and 12.5 kV distribution substation. It records 30L to King's Bridge and 32L and 67L to Oxen Pond. It also records the later retirement of the 4.16 kV T1 bank and eight 12.5 kV feeders serving approximately 4,200 customers.

A 2027 filing instead states that Ridge Road was constructed in 1960. Neither filing explains the difference. The atlas retains both dates as unresolved.

## Stamp's Lane

Stamp's Lane was built in 1963 as a 66 kV switching station and 4.16/12.5 kV distribution substation. The 2007 filing records six 66 kV circuits, five 4.16 kV feeders, six 12.5 kV feeders and approximately 9,300 customers.

## Sandy Brook

The plant entered service in 1963 with one vertical 6.31 MW generating unit at 33.5 m rated net head. Its Canadian Westinghouse generator was manufactured in 1963 and remained original when the 2023 capital filing was prepared.

The original woodstave penstock was 336 m long and 2.6 m in diameter. It comprised a 251 m upstream section and an 85 m downstream section separated by a surge tank.

The 2011 refurbishment filing identifies original electrical equipment installed in 1963:

- Westinghouse exciter.
- Brown Boveri Model AB2/1 mechanical voltage regulator.
- Westinghouse Type DH generator breaker.
- Potential transformers and current transformers.
- Two single-phase 120/240 V emergency station-service transformers connected open delta.
