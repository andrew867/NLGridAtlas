## Avalon transformer loading

The Conception Bay South study records 2009 peaks of 19.9 MVA on each Chamberlains transformer, 23.9 MVA on HWD-T3, and 14.5 MVA on KEL-T1. The study selected replacement of the 15 MVA Kelligrews transformer with a 25 MVA unit. The feeder table records 2,266 customers on CHA-01, 1,745 on CHA-02, 2,113 on CHA-03, 2,508 on HWD-07, and 1,685 on HWD-08. Source: 2011 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 309 to 316.

The Northeast St. John's study records 2009 peaks of 25.6 MVA on 25 MVA BCV-T1, 22.7 MVA on 25 MVA PUL-T1, and 23.9 MVA on 25 MVA HWD-T3. The selected project added a 25 MVA transformer at Pulpit Rock and PUL-04 to transfer load from Broad Cove. The filed cost was $2.705 million for transformer and substation work and $638,000 for PUL-04. Source: 2011 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 333 to 340.

The third-quarter report records the Pulpit Rock transformer project complete and energized. Source: [CA-NP-217](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2012/NP2012CostOfCapital/files/rfi/CA-NP-217.pdf), physical PDF p. 1120.

## St. John's Main distribution work

The 2011 project removed five feeders from the Waterford River duct bank and reconfigured them as four aerial feeders. It included two manholes, 100 m of duct bank, twelve 100 m single-phase 750 MCM XLPE cables, and 0.5 km of double-circuit line. SJM-04 was removed. The filed capacities for SJM-02, SJM-06, SJM-09, and SJM-13 increased from 5.7 MVA to 10.0 MVA. The filed capacities for SJM-03, SJM-07, SJM-08, and SJM-14 increased from 5.7 MVA to 7.6 MVA. Total feeder capacity increased from 51.3 MVA to 70.4 MVA. Source: 2011 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 413 to 429.

## Northwest Brook feeder reliability

The selected NWB-02 work relocated 7.0 km of the 8.9 km Hillview to Hatchet Cove Tap section and 3.4 km from Hatchet Cove Tap to St. Jones Within to road right-of-way. The filing specified #2 ACSR and a total cost of $521,000. Source: 2011 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 396 to 400.

## Hydroelectric civil work

The Rattling Lake spillway was a 1958 stoplog structure. The filed replacement used a gravity overflow labyrinth design with no gates, external power, or operator intervention. Its design basis included a 416 m3/s inflow design flood, 1 in 10,000 annual exceedance probability, and 1.2 m freeboard. The project included 1.0 m crest raises at Rattling Lake Dam, Amy's Lake Dam, and the Amy's Lake freeboard dykes. The filed project cost was $2.600 million, including $1.800 million for the spillway. Source: 2011 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 204 to 210.

The third-quarter report states that the 105 m stoplog spillway had been replaced by a high-efficiency piano-key spillway and that the project was substantially complete. Source: CA-NP-217, physical PDF p. 1120.

The Sandy Lake project raised the spillway by 1.0 m to increase storage. The 2010 filing estimated 0.86 GWh of additional annual production and a cost of $612,000. A later RFI states that environmental approvals delayed the project and that it was completed in the 2011 capital program. Sources: [2010 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 30 and 254 to 264; [CA-NP-011](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/rfi/CA-NP-011.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.
