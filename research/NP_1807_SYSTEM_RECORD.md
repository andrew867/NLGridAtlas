# 1807 System Record

## Garrison public-works timber

A Commissary and Storekeeper's Office tender dated September 16 sought pine or spruce scantling for public works in the St. John's garrison. The notice specified the number, length and cross-section of each timber group. Delivery was required at the King's Wharf by May 20, 1808. Proposals were due October 10, 1807. The notice records procurement, not delivery or completed work.

## Barrack furniture

A November 5 notice sought eight deal tables for Merchants' Hall. Each table was to be 4.5 feet long and 3 feet wide, with a glued top and four braced legs. The same notice sought 16 stools made in the manner of barrack forms to suit the tables. Proposals were due November 9. The notice records a requested build, not completed furniture.

## Convoy flag signals

An order dated October 27 established two visual signals at Signal Hill or Fort Amherst. A red flag with a blue pendant below it meant that the convoy for England had sailed. A red flag with a blue pendant above it meant that the convoy for Portugal had sailed. The order was printed in the November 5 *Royal Gazette* and repeated later in the year.

## St. John's convoy departure

Governor John Holloway reported that HMS *Isis* reached Spithead on November 25 with a convoy of 31 sail. The convoy had left St. John's on October 30. The despatch gives the departure and arrival record, but not the individual vessels or route followed between the two ports.

## Harbour Grace and Fortune Bay court buildings

The Harbour Grace magistrates submitted an estimate of £789 for a Court House and Gaol. They reported that no more than £400 could be collected locally. Holloway considered the buildings necessary to support civil authority and recommended government assistance. He also reported that a comparable building was necessary in Fortune Bay. These were proposed works, not completed buildings.

## Harbour Grace church and parsonage repair

Holloway reported that the Harbour Grace church and parsonage house were being repaired. He had paid the authorized £50 from lease money. The despatch establishes an active repair and payment, but does not state the total project cost or completion date.

## St. John's school house

The school house for poor children in St. John's had been started on what Holloway considered too large a scale and had accumulated a debt of £300 and upwards. He reported that the year's donations would not clear the debt. He also proposed a £20 annual salary for a female assistant, payable from government lease revenue. The building and debt are reported conditions; the assistant's salary remained a proposal.

## Fogo worship house

Holloway reported that about 400 Protestant inhabitants at Fogo had built a house for divine worship. They asked for £50 to discharge its debt, which he recommended. With no missionary nearby, an unnamed older man conducted the service and spent his £15 annual allowance on books for the community. The building is reported complete; the requested debt grant and lease-money allowance remained recommendations.
