# 1874 System Record

## Direct Cable attempt in Conception Bay

The Times published the Supreme Court judgment in *Anglo American Telegraph Company v. Direct United States Cable Company* on August 29, 1874. The complaint states that the Direct United States Cable Company brought a telegraphic cable from Torbay, Nova Scotia, into Conception Bay on July 26. It states that the company laid the cable for several miles within the bay's headlands. The planned system would have connected the United States and the United Kingdom through Newfoundland.

The supporting affidavits describe a large steamer, believed to be the *Faraday*, entering Conception Bay with a line or cable astern. The vessel proceeded to a position about three miles east of Harbor Grace Island and about three miles from the north shore. It remained there before entering Harbor Grace, then departed for England the next morning.

## Limited injunction

The Chief Justice limited the interim order to work within Newfoundland jurisdiction. The injunction restrained the defendants from bringing a submarine cable to shore, landing a submarine cable, constructing land lines, and working or operating a telegraph line on the island. Judge Robinson concurred in the order.
