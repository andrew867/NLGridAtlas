# Newfoundland Power and Island system record, 1981

## Demand and planning

- Newfoundland Power peak demand was 716.2 MW. Island transmission peak demand was 978 MW and Hydro system peak demand was 834 MW. The table has no separate Hydro Rural value for 1981. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- The 1981 supply-planning case used a 1,234 MW coincident peak, 1,510 MW installed capability, 0.11 loss-of-load-probability index, 6,396 GWh energy requirement, 6,988 GWh firm capability, and a 592 GWh surplus. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)

The located comparative Newfoundland Power capital table starts in 1984. The located comparative reliability table starts in 1987. No supported 1981 Newfoundland Power capital, SAIFI, SAIDI, energy-sales, or customer-account total was located.

## Transmission

- TL232 is an 84 km, 230 kV radial line from Stony Brook to Buchans. It uses H-frame wood structures and two steel towers and was built as a parallel central-system link. [2008 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 116](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/application/NLH2008ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- TL234 is a 51.5 km, 230 kV radial line from Upper Salmon to Bay d'Espoir. It uses H-frame wood structures and steel towers and was constructed to connect Upper Salmon. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 54](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/applic/NLH2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- TL251 runs 48 km from Howley to Hampden and TL252 runs 53 km from Hampden to Jackson's Arm. Both are 69 kV, single wood-pole radial lines. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 54](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/applic/NLH2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Newfoundland Power's original 136L was an approximately 81 km, 138 kV link from Bishop's Falls to Cobb's Pond. Most structures were H-frame. The route followed the Trans-Canada Highway before a cross-country approach to Cobb's Pond. Later central-system conversion work changed the line endpoints. [CA-NP-121 Attachment A, physical PDF pp. 5 and 7](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/responses/CA-NP-121.PDF)
- Newfoundland Power's 147L is an approximately 58 km, 138 kV line between Cobb's Pond and Lewisporte. [147L Relocation Application, physical PDF p. 11](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Line147LNewFoundGold/app/From%20NP%20-%20Approval%20of%20Capital%20Expenditures%20to%20Relocate%20a%20Portion%20of%20Transmission%20Line%20147L%20and%20a%20CIAC%20from%20New%20Found%20Gold%20-%202026-07-31.PDF)
- The tap from 55L to Quartz Substation was constructed in 1981. [2023 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 440](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2023/NP2023Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202023%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202022-06-29.PDF)

## Stations and generation

- Newfoundland Power constructed Greenspond as a 66/12.5 kV distribution substation on a tap from the 115L/116L radial system. Its original 66 kV and 12.5 kV bus structures used wood poles. Before the connection, Greenspond Island was isolated from the grid and supplied by a Hydro diesel station. [2026 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 340 to 341](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202025-06-27.PDF); [CA-NP-034, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Capital/responses/CA-NP-034.PDF)
- Hardwoods breaker B7C1 was a Westinghouse 690SP2500 SF6 unit manufactured in 1981. It switched a 69 kV capacitor bank used for Eastern Avalon voltage support. The 2007 filing records an average of 250 operations per year since 1981. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 72 to 73](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/applic/NLH2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Bay d'Espoir Unit 5 received draft-tube concrete repairs for cavitation. The Unit 2 and Unit 4 greasing systems were modified so wicket-gate and bottom-bushing lubrication operated independently. [2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 319 and 326](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NLH2015Capital/files/application/NLH-2015-Capital-Budget-Application-Volume-I-2014-08-01.PDF)
- Hydro authorized Cat Arm construction early in 1981. Its permanent access-road contract was tendered in May and awarded in June for $11,977,710. [Muskrat Falls Project Exhibit 29 Revision 1, physical PDF p. 30](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit29-Rev1.pdf); [1983 Electric Power Control Act report, physical PDF p. 70](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/Consents/83PMARCH.pdf)
- Francois diesel Unit 2001 was a 136 kW Caterpillar D3306 installed in 1981. By the 2009 filing it had accumulated 81,343 operating hours and four overhauls. [2010 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 397 to 399](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NLH2010Capital/files/application/NLH2010ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf)

## Topsail penstock date conflict

The 2020 application states that the 1,910 m woodstave penstock was replaced in 1981. The 2004 plant history assigns the extended penstock and intake to the 1983 major refurbishment. The filings do not reconcile the dates, so neither year is treated as definitive for the complete penstock and intake scope. [2020 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 29](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2020/NP2020Capital/applications/NP%20-%202020%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202019-07-05.PDF); [2004 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 270](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicComplete.pdf)
