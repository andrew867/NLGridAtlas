# 1906 System Record

## Grand Falls hydroelectric development

A 2026 Government of Newfoundland and Labrador environmental filing says the Anglo Newfoundland Development Company first harnessed the hydroelectric potential at Grand Falls in 1906. The filing describes the present facility as an 80 MW development, but that value is its current installed capacity, not a 1906 rating.

The 2017 Exploits condition assessment separately dates the Grand Falls main dam and original forebay intake to 1907. The records support a sequence in which hydroelectric development began in 1906 and the named civil assets were completed in 1907.
