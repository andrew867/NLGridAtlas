### Heart's Content hydro plant

The 2014 filing describes one 2.4 MW unit with a 46.9 m rated net head, a 3,400 hp vertical Francis turbine, and a 3,000 kVA generator at 80% power factor. Normal annual production was 8.3 GWh. Source: physical PDF pp. 26 and 171.

The filed scope included a stator and exciter rewind, rotor re-insulation, generator ancillary equipment, PLC governor and plant controls, digital protection and metering, forebay fibre, arc-flash-rated switchgear, a vacuum breaker, battery and charger replacement, and new power cables to the yard. Source: physical PDF pp. 172 to 175.

The $5.735 million filing included $3.495 million for the 579 m penstock and $2.240 million for the plant work. Source: physical PDF pp. 26 and 175.

### La Manche Canal

The canal was 5,600 m long, approximately 10 m wide, and rated for 6 m3/s. The filing proposed increasing capacity to 9 m3/s and rehabilitating seven La Manche spillways plus the Cluneys diversion dam and spillway. Source: physical PDF pp. 217 to 219.

The proposed work was expected to recover 5.54 GWh per year and increase the combined annual production of Rocky Pond and Tors Cove from 39.0 GWh to 44.54 GWh. The filed cost was $1.665 million. Source: physical PDF pp. 24, 215, and 219.
