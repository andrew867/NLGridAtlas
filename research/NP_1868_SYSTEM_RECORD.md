# 1868 System Record

## Search result

The 1868 House of Assembly journal records a $126.45 contingent-expense payment to the Telegraph Company for messages. This is evidence of purchased telegraph service. It does not identify a new line, station, cable, operating change or construction project.

The journal appendix also publishes a Board of Works road report that identifies telegraph poles between Half-way House and Salmonier Bridge. The report is dated December 31, 1867. That infrastructure record belongs to the 1867 queue.
