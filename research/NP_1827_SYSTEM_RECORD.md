# 1827 System Record

## Conception Bay packet communication

The January 11 *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* prints two *Express* notices on physical PDF p. 1. The notices are dated September 21 and October 12, 1826. They establish the earlier Harbour Grace, Portugal Cove and St. John's operating arrangement but do not establish a new 1827 change.

The February 8 *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* prints a notice dated January 13, 1827, on physical PDF p. 3. The *Express* was laid up for the winter. A suitable boat with an experienced crew was to operate between Harbour Grace and Portugal Cove when conditions allowed. If water communication stopped, a letter carrier was to travel weekly from Harbour Grace to St. John's by land, weather permitting. P. Rogerson at Harbour Grace and James Clift at St. John's were the agents.

The May 4 *Public Ledger* prints an altered-service notice on physical PDF p. 1. The packet had undergone alterations for passenger comfort. It left Harbour Grace at 09:00 on Monday, Wednesday and Friday, and left Portugal Cove at noon on the next day. Cabin fare was 10 shillings, steerage fare was 5 shillings and a single letter cost 6 pence. Double letters and parcels were charged in proportion. James Clift and P. Rogerson were the agents.

The *Public Ledger* copy is dated April 17. The June 7 *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* prints the same timetable and fares on physical PDF p. 3 but dates the notice April 16. The one-day conflict is unresolved. The timeline uses the May 4 publication date and does not assign an alteration date.

The August 15 *Newfoundlander* prints a later copy dated July 25 on physical PDF p. 4. It repeats the route, timetable, fares and agents. It confirms continued advertising but does not identify another physical alteration.

## Carbonear lightning strike

The July 27 *Public Ledger* reports an event on July 16 on physical PDF p. 2. Lightning struck the business house of Slade, Elson & Co. at Carbonear. It entered through the chimney, damaged the grate and forced a door off its hinges. T. Newell, the agent during John Elson's absence, and Dr. Donnegan were in the room. The report says that they were briefly rendered speechless and recovered without injury.

The report does not identify a lightning conductor or other installed electrical apparatus.

## Newspaper search

The review covered 128 surviving files listed by the three MUN DAI collections: 15 issues of *The Newfoundlander*, 99 issues of the *Public Ledger* and 14 issues of the *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal*. Repeated packet advertisements were treated as continuity evidence, not separate operating events.

The MUN DAI *Royal Gazette* issue list starts in 1828. No 1827 *Royal Gazette* file was added to this review.

No named 1827 Newfoundland lighthouse project, electrical telegraph, installed electrical circuit or operating electrical apparatus was located in the reviewed files. Foreign reports and advertisements for loose equipment were excluded from the Newfoundland system chronology.
