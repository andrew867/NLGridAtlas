# Soldiers Pond converter station record

Updated: 2026-11-13
Work items: `HVDC-001`, `HVDC-003`

## Published record

The station diagram separates the 230 kV alternating-current yard, the two line-commutated converter poles, and the three synchronous condensers.

| Item | Dated evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioning | Hydro reported that the Labrador-Island Link was officially commissioned on 2023-04-14. Source: commissioning update, physical PDF p. 1. |
| Converter design basis | The 2014 integration study specifies a bipolar line-commutated converter system at +/-350 kV DC. The study gives 900 MW at Muskrat Falls and 830 MW at Soldiers Pond in bipolar operation. Source: 2014 corridor study, physical PDF p. 37. |
| Synchronous condensers | The 2014 design specifies three 175 MVAr machines. Hydro reported all three in service after 2023-04-13 except for planned maintenance. Sources: 2014 corridor study, physical PDF pp. 30 and 38; April 2023 monthly update, physical PDF pp. 2 to 3. |
| AC yard connections | The 2024 planning assessment lists TL201 and TL217 to Western Avalon, TL242 and TL266 to Hardwoods, and TL265 and TL268 to Holyrood. Source: 2024 Annual Planning Assessment, physical PDF pp. 13 to 14. |
| AC-yard construction | The independent engineer reported that the Soldiers Pond AC switchyard was energized and fully commissioned in November 2017. The same page records the converter transformers on their pads and oil-filled, and the three synchronous condensers assembled with their power and exciter transformers installed. Source: Independent Engineer Site Visit Report, physical PDF p. 8. |
| Converter-transformer count | Nalcor's September 2018 project presentation records seven converter transformers transported from Bay Bulls to Soldiers Pond in spring 2017. The CD0504 civil scope separately lists six delta-star converter-transformer foundations and one spare-transformer foundation for the converter-station works. Sources: CIMFP P-00136, physical PDF p. 82; CIMFP P-02995, physical PDF p. 7. |
| Converter-transformer spare | Hydro reported one spare converter transformer at Soldiers Pond. The response does not give its voltage or MVA rating. Source: PUB-NLH-022, physical PDF p. 1. |
| Preliminary transformer model | The April 2012 load-flow study modelled the Soldiers Pond converter transformers at 481 MVA per pole in normal operation and 613 MVA per pole for monopolar overload. The corresponding converter-side bus voltages were 265 kV and 225 kV. The table marks every value as preliminary and states that final values would be set during design. These are study-model inputs, not installed nameplate ratings. Source: NP-NLH-108 Attachment 4, physical PDF p. 293. |
| Electrode line | The 2020 emergency-response plan identifies EL3/4 between Soldiers Pond and Dowden's Point. Source: Near-Term Reliability Report, physical PDF pp. 38 to 39. |
| Filter banks and electrodes in planning model | The 2033/34 study case models all filter banks as available at Muskrat Falls and Soldiers Pond and models the electrode lines and sites in service. This is a study assumption, not an equipment inventory. Source: 2024 Annual Planning Assessment, physical PDF p. 8. |

## Evidence not located

- A current breaker-level Soldiers Pond AC-yard one-line.
- Converter-transformer voltage, winding, impedance, and MVA nameplate data.
- Filter-bank count, individual ratings, and bus assignments.
- A current station-service one-line.

The May 2008 three-terminal study modelled two 351 MVA transformer units per pole at Soldiers Pond for a different Gull Island, Soldiers Pond, and Salisbury scheme. The April 2012 point-to-point study later modelled 481 MVA per pole in normal operation and 613 MVA per pole for monopolar overload. Both studies identify their parameters as preliminary. Neither establishes the commissioned transformer nameplates.
