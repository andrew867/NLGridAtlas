# Soldiers Pond converter station record

Updated: 2026-11-11  
Work item: `HVDC-001`

## Published record

The station diagram separates the 230 kV alternating-current yard, the two line-commutated converter poles, and the three synchronous condensers.

| Item | Dated evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioning | Hydro reported that the Labrador-Island Link was officially commissioned on 2023-04-14. Source: commissioning update, physical PDF p. 1. |
| Converter design basis | The 2014 integration study specifies a bipolar line-commutated converter system at +/-350 kV DC. The study gives 900 MW at Muskrat Falls and 830 MW at Soldiers Pond in bipolar operation. Source: 2014 corridor study, physical PDF p. 37. |
| Synchronous condensers | The 2014 design specifies three 175 MVAr machines. Hydro reported all three in service after 2023-04-13 except for planned maintenance. Sources: 2014 corridor study, physical PDF pp. 30 and 38; April 2023 monthly update, physical PDF pp. 2 to 3. |
| AC yard connections | The 2024 planning assessment lists TL201 and TL217 to Western Avalon, TL242 and TL266 to Hardwoods, and TL265 and TL268 to Holyrood. Source: 2024 Annual Planning Assessment, physical PDF pp. 13 to 14. |
| Converter-transformer spare | Hydro reported one spare converter transformer at Soldiers Pond. The response does not give its voltage or MVA rating. Source: PUB-NLH-022, physical PDF p. 1. |
| Electrode line | The 2020 emergency-response plan identifies EL3/4 between Soldiers Pond and Dowden's Point. Source: Near-Term Reliability Report, physical PDF pp. 38 to 39. |
| Filter banks and electrodes in planning model | The 2033/34 study case models all filter banks as available at Muskrat Falls and Soldiers Pond and models the electrode lines and sites in service. This is a study assumption, not an equipment inventory. Source: 2024 Annual Planning Assessment, physical PDF p. 8. |
