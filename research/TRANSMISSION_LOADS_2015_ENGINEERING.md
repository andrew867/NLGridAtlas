# 2015 transmission-line rating and load record

PUB-NLH-324 reports the 0 degrees Celsius MVA rating, anticipated 2014/2015 winter peak, and anticipated 2015 average load for Hydro's 230 kV, 138 kV, and 66/69 kV transmission lines.

The atlas already carried selected 230 kV values in circuit-specific engineering records. The source-specific layer in `research/derived/transmission-loads-2015.json` adds all 16 rows from the 138 kV table and all 16 rows from the 66/69 kV table.

The table's percentage column compares average load with anticipated peak. It is not loading as a percentage of the 0 degrees Celsius rating.

The source labels its third table `66/69 kV`. That dated label is retained in the engineering record. It does not overwrite the 2018 circuit voltage in the separate assessment asset list.

## Operating notes retained from the filing

- TL222, TL223, TL224, and TL245 use a peak case with Hinds Lake and Cat Arm generation in service. Hinds Lake output affects flows between Deer Lake and Stony Brook.
- TL241 had two normally in-service 5 MVAR, 138 kV shunt reactors. TL244 had one. Hydro stated that these reactors affected line loading.
- TL243 is the Hinds Lake generation connection, and its average load depends on plant production.
- TL250's Bottom Brook average-load reading is influenced by line charging during lighter-load conditions.
- TL256 connects the St. Anthony and Roddickton area. Three 3 MVAR, 69 kV switched shunt capacitor banks at St. Anthony Airport affect its average loading.
- TL215's peak case assumes 5.7 MW from Rose Blanche Brook Hydro Plant.
- TL225 connects Deer Lake Power generation to Deer Lake Terminal Station and is part of the delivery path to Corner Brook Pulp and Paper through Massey Drive.
- TL251, TL252, and TL253 use a peak case with Rattle Brook generation in service.
- The TL264 record carries the filing's expectation that Duck Pond Mine would close during 2015.
- TL280 is the Star Lake generation connection, and its average load depends on plant production.

## Source

[PUB-NLH-324, Transmission Line Loads for 2015](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-324.pdf), physical PDF p. 1 for the field definitions, p. 4 for the 138 kV table and notes, and p. 5 for the 66/69 kV table and notes.
