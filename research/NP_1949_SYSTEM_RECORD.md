# 1949 System Record

## Mobile River watershed rights

Newfoundland Power held rights to use the Mobile River watershed for electricity generation under a lease dated November 23, 1946. An agreement dated October 21, 1949 amended that lease.

Source: [CA-NP-498](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-498.pdf), physical PDF p. 1. SHA-256 `ec26da9b27174191ca0d2bebf814b54a365df11ed31bcb9a1242d2b6316964fe`.

A 2009 Board order describes the October 21, 1949 instrument as granting Newfoundland Power rights within the Mobile River watershed. The order says the hydroelectric facilities later constructed in the watershed supplied approximately 49 GWh annually. That energy value describes the later facilities; it is not a 1949 production value.

## Utility property

The property schedule records six Mobile deeds in 1949: September 23, September 28, two parcels on September 30, and two parcels on December 13. The same schedule records a Southside Road property in St. John's acquired from the Government of Newfoundland on March 28, 1949. The schedule does not state the purpose of the Southside Road parcel.

The September 30 date was verified from the rendered page. The text extraction misread one occurrence as September 34.

## Public Utilities Board

The provincial government established the Board of Commissioners of Public Utilities in 1949. The Board received general authority to supervise public utilities and approve rates. The earlier Public Utilities Commission, created in 1929, had relied on government referrals and did not have the same general authority.
