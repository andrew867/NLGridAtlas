# 1847 System Record

## St. John's communications proposal

On May 22, the *Morning Courier* argued for regular steam communication between St. John's and Boston. The editorial said Newfoundland merchants needed prompt intelligence comparable with other North American colonies and the United States.

The article expected electric telegraph service between New Orleans and Boston and between New York and Halifax by way of Canada. Its Newfoundland proposal was a weekly steam service to Boston, not a telegraph line. The printed estimate covered a steamer, voyages, operating costs and possible public support.

No named 1847 Newfoundland telegraph line, company, station, instrument, construction project or operating circuit was located. No event is added to the atlas timeline for 1847.
