# Newfoundland Power 2013 capital and system records

## Primary filing

Source: [Newfoundland Power 2013 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013Capital/files/applic/NP2013ApplicationComplete.pdf)

### 110L transmission rebuild

The 110L project replaced the final original section between Lethbridge and Summerville. The section was 21.1 km long, used 227 structures, and carried 1/0 ACSR conductor. Corrosion had reduced its physical strength and electrical capacity, and the line section had been derated to approximately one-half of its original current capacity. The filed replacement used 477 ASC. Source: physical PDF pp. 285 to 292. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 291.

The third-quarter report states that 110L was completed in October. Source: [PUB-NP-163](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-163.pdf), physical PDF p. 767, attachment p. 56.

The fourth-quarter report states that all Bonavista Peninsula transmission-line rebuilds were complete after approximately $11.0 million of work since 2006. Source: [PUB-NP-163](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-163.pdf), physical PDF p. 787, attachment p. 76.

The 2012 filing uses 110L for the Lethbridge to Summerville section. Later circuit registers use different designations for parts of the Bonavista loop. The atlas keeps the filed 2012 designation in the dated construction record.

### Mobile radio system

The filing describes Newfoundland Power's early 1980s VHF system as nine operating areas with 24 repeater sites, 207 mobile radios, 85 portable radios, and 23 base stations. Mobile radios could use all nine areas and three simplex frequencies. The central switch was integrated with the System Control Centre telephone system and used Bell Aliant WAN facilities, Newfoundland Power IP routers, and VoIP equipment. Source: primary filing, physical PDF pp. 394 to 402.

The selected alternative moved the utility onto Bell's shared PassPort system. The filing requested $750,000 of 2013 capital and estimated $280,000 of annual operating cost. The 25-year net-present-value comparison was:

| Alternative | 25-year NPV |
| --- | ---: |
| Own and maintain | $6.457 million |
| Own, Bell maintains | $7.611 million |
| Bell shared PassPort | $4.560 million |

A later capital filing reports that the mobile radio replacement occurred in 2014 at approximately $838,000. Source: [Newfoundland Power 2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 141.

## Other completed 2013 work

The fourth-quarter report records these completed items:

- One new 25 MVA transformer at Glendale Substation.
- St. Catherines and Stephenville substation refurbishments.
- New Chelsea and Pitmans Pond turbine-runner replacements, with a combined estimated annual production increase of approximately 1.7 GWh.
- The Bonavista Peninsula transmission-line rebuild program.
