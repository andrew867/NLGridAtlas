# Great Northern Peninsula generation history

## Filed capacities

IC-270 records the generation in the St. Anthony and Roddickton system before the Great Northern Peninsula interconnection.

| Site | Filed capacity | Source |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| St. Anthony Diesel Plant | 8,850 kW, including one 850 kW mobile diesel generator | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |
| Roddickton Woodchip Plant | 5,000 kW | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |
| Woodchip-site black-start diesel | 450 kW | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |
| Woodchip-site mobile diesel | 850 kW | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |
| Roddickton Diesel Plant | 2,350 kW | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |
| Roddickton Mini-hydro Plant | 425 kW | IC-270, physical PDF p. 3 |

IC-270 also records TL257 as a 69 kV line built in 1989 between Roddickton Woodchip and St. Anthony Airport, and TL261 as a 69 kV line built in 1996 between St. Anthony Airport and St. Anthony Diesel Plant. These are dated line records, not current internal plant one-lines.

## Roddickton plant disposition

Order P.U. 34 (2004), physical PDF p. 1, records the earlier Board approvals to abandon the Roddickton woodchip and diesel plants. Physical PDF p. 11 consents to moving two 850 kW mobile diesel generators from Roddickton to St. Anthony.

## Current planning record

The 2024 Annual Planning Assessment, physical PDF p. 42, states that Hydro maintains a 9.7 MW diesel plant at St. Anthony for limited backup following contingencies affecting TL239, TL241, TL244, TL256, or TL261. This is the current filed plant capacity used by the atlas. It does not establish the internal generator lineup.
