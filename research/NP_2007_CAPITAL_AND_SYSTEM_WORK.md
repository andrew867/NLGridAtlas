## Rattling Brook

The application proposed a $18.242 million plant refurbishment for 2007, physical PDF pp. 18 to 19 and 375 to 385.

- The plant entered service in 1958 and contains two vertical Francis turbine-generators, each originally rated 7,500 kVA.
- The filed electrical work included switchgear, generator cables, excitation, protection, AC and DC distribution, station service, controls, instrumentation, heating, ventilation, forebay monitoring and fibre communications.
- The filed mechanical work included both main butterfly valves, the Unit 2 exciter and generator brake, cooling systems and condition-monitoring equipment.
- The station record identifies 101L to Grand Falls, 102L to Gander, a 20 MVA 66/4.16 kV generation transformer, and a 2.2 MVA 66/12.5 kV distribution transformer serving approximately 674 customers.
- The station work proposed a larger yard, corrected bus clearances, new station service, new line and bus protection, and changes for portable-substation access.

The later 2021 Capital Budget Application states that the 66 kV transmission, distribution and generation substation refurbishment was completed in 2007, physical PDF pp. 110 to 111. It does not itemize completion of every plant component in the original filing.

## Substation program

The strategic plan records 130 substations, with refurbishment or modernization work identified at 80 percent of them over a ten-year cycle, physical PDF p. 142. Nearly half were more than 40 years old and approximately one-third were more than 50 years old, physical PDF p. 144.

The 2007 station records are on physical PDF pp. 160 to 169.

- Blaketown: 42 MVA 138/66 kV T3, 20 MVA 138/25 kV T2, 64L, 48L, 55L, 94L, 80L and 86L, two 25 kV feeders and approximately 2,600 customers.
- Clarkes Pond: two 7.5 MVA 66/12.5 kV transformers, radial 55L, three feeders and approximately 2,500 customers.
- Garnish: 38-year-old deteriorated station proposed for replacement beside Highway 210. The rebuilt 301L arrangement avoided a new high-voltage bus and the existing transmission tap.
- Glovertown: 20 MVA 138/25 kV T1, a tap from 124L, two feeders and approximately 2,300 customers.
- Goulds: two distribution transformers with 33 MVA combined capacity, 4L, 17L, 18L, 25L and 72L on the 66 kV bus, and a 66/33 kV T1 supplying 3L to Petty Harbour.
- Ridge Road: 30L, 32L and 67L, eight 12.5 kV feeders and approximately 4,200 customers after the 4.16 kV section was converted and T1 retired.
- Stamp's Lane: four transformers with 50 MVA combined at 12.5 kV and 21 MVA combined at 4.16 kV, six 66 kV lines, eleven feeders and approximately 9,300 customers.

The system inventory records approximately 400 circuit breakers, 200 reclosers, 220 potential transformers, 360 voltage regulators, 2,500 protective relays, 115 battery banks and 2,500 high-voltage switches, physical PDF pp. 172 to 174. It also records 190 power transformers, including 68 with tap changers, physical PDF p. 148.

## Transmission

The transmission rebuild report is on physical PDF pp. 178 to 182.

- 43L: 66 kV, 25.1 km, Heart's Content to New Chelsea, H-frame wood construction, built in 1956. The filing records 8.0 km rebuilt in 2005, 12.0 km filed for 2006, and the final 5.1 km filed for 2007 at $570,000. PUB-NP-007 later lists 43L complete, physical PDF p. 2.
- 110L: 66 kV, 79 km, Clarenville to Lockston, single wood-pole construction, built in 1958. Corroded conductor had reduced the line to about one-half of its original load-carrying capacity. The filing records 17.4 km upgraded in 1966, 18.2 km from 1972 to 1974, 6.7 km filed for 2006, and 14.1 km between Lockston and Summerville filed for 2007 at $1.311 million. The fourth-quarter 2013 report states that the Bonavista Peninsula line rebuild program was complete, physical PDF p. 787.
- 20L: 66 kV, 20.1 km, Mobile to Cape Broyle, H-frame wood construction, built in 1951. The filing proposed a 7.5 km section for 2007 at $687,000. The fourth-quarter 2008 report later records the Mobile to Cape Broyle rebuild as complete, physical PDF p. 261.

The 43L and 110L route sheets are preserved at `public/reference-maps/43l-2007-route-181.png` and `public/reference-maps/110l-2007-route-182.png`.

## Hydro and thermal work

The facility-rehabilitation report proposed the following 2007 work, physical PDF pp. 133 to 134.

- Horse Chops West Dam upstream-slope work, $76,000.
- Bay Bulls Big Pond Dam upstream-riprap work, $76,000.
- Pittman's Pond West Dyke riprap work, $75,000.
- West Lake outlet concrete work, $69,000.
- Paddy's Pond outlet timber-crib work, $101,000.
- Petty Harbour Forebay Dam overtopping protection, $50,000.
- Topsail Pond Dam overtopping protection, $46,000.
- Lookout Brook spillway concrete work, $28,000.

The Wesleyville update records completed work from 2005, physical PDF p. 139. Newfoundland Power exchanged the Rolls-Royce AVON gas generator for a zero-hour-rated refurbished unit supplied by Siemens Canada. The unit was installed and commissioned at Wesleyville for $1.139 million, compared with the $1.124 million approved budget.
