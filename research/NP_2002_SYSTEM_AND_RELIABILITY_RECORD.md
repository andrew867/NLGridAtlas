# Newfoundland Power 2002 system and reliability record

## Source boundary

The public mirror has no standalone `np02cap` or `np2002cap` directory. The surviving direct record is split between the 2003 Capital Budget Application filed in August 2002, the 2003 General Rate Application, and the 2002 Annual Report filed as CA-213G. This note does not treat later references as a substitute 2002 capital application.

## Completed system work

Newfoundland Power reported approximately $60 million in 2002 capital investment. The named electrical work included:

- rebuilt distribution lines between Dunville and Fox Harbour, and between Grand Codroy Bridge and Cape Anguille;
- a distribution-line upgrade from Glovertown to the Eastport Peninsula;
- distribution reclosers in the Bonavista North area;
- pole, crossarm, insulator, and hardware replacement on the transmission line between Ochre Pit Cove and Salmon Cove;
- more than 2,000 insulators replaced in the Gander Bay area;
- a looped underground distribution cable system in downtown St. John's;
- the transmission-line rebuild between Grand Bank and Grand Beach;
- 11 km replaced and 10 km upgraded on the transmission line between Goulds and Mobile;
- the Gander wooden bus structure replaced with steel; and
- a power transformer and circuit breaker replaced at Rattling Brook.

Source: [2002 Annual Report, CA-213G](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-213G.pdf), physical PDF p. 18.

## Salt Pond and the Wesleyville move

The annual report states that a Salt Pond power transformer failed, followed by the portable replacement transformer. Newfoundland Power deferred the Salt Pond gas-turbine relocation to the Bonavista North area until 2003. The report does not identify the failed station-transformer designation.

Source: [2002 Annual Report, CA-213G](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-213G.pdf), physical PDF p. 17.

## August lightning event

The annual report records more than 10,000 lightning strikes, equipment damage across almost half the island, outages in nearly 300 communities, and approximately $1 million in repair cost.

Source: [2002 Annual Report, CA-213G](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-213G.pdf), physical PDF p. 17.

## Actual reliability

The first-revision Operations and Customer Service evidence replaced the original 2002 forecasts with year-end values:

| Measure | Including loss of supply | Excluding Hydro loss of supply |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| SAIFI | 4.76 interruptions per customer | 3.25 interruptions per customer |
| SAIDI | 4.54 hours per customer | 4.18 hours per customer |

The same revision records 17 underfrequency trips operating the load-shedding scheme in 2002 against a target of 8. It attributes the recent primary cause of loss-of-supply outages to equipment failure at Holyrood Generating Station.

Source: [2003 General Rate Application, Complete Application, first revision](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/Application/CompleteApplicationRev1.pdf), physical PDF pp. 83 to 86.

Source: [CA-279, first revision](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-279Rev.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.

CA-446 preserves the annual company-wide series excluding loss of supply from 1992 through 2002. Its 2002 values agree with the revised application, except the original CA-446 response prints SAIDI as 4.19 while the first revision and revised application print 4.18. The atlas uses the first-revision value.

Source: [CA-446, first revision](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-446Rev.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.

## Load-factor evidence

CA-241 gives actual annual system load factors of 0.519 in 1998, 0.540 in 1999, 0.549 in 2000, and 0.476 in 2001. It gives 0.492 for 2002 through 2005, but explicitly identifies every one of those values as a forecast. No actual 2002 load factor was found in the searched 2003 General Rate Application record.

Source: [CA-241](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-241.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.

## Filed system scale and controls

The October 2002 evidence describes approximately 8,000 km of distribution line, more than 250,000 poles, 50,000 transformers, 200,000 meters, and 137 substations. It describes real-time SCADA monitoring and control of individual distribution lines, electronic reclosers and relays, and the 2001 addition of address-to-feeder cross-referencing in the Problem Call Logging System.

Source: [2003 General Rate Application, prefiled application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/Application/NP_Application_Prefiled_2003.pdf), physical PDF pp. 49, 52, and 75 to 76.
