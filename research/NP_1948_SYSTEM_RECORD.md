# 1948 System Record

## King's Bridge Substation

Newfoundland Power dates King's Bridge Substation to 1948 and describes it as both a transmission and distribution substation. A 2026 filing repeats the same construction year and station role.

Sources: [2016 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016Capital/files/applic/NP-2016-Capital-Budget-Application-2015-06-23.pdf), physical PDF p. 263, SHA-256 `9032c57d9f433b245ebcf28fb72b206550bd1caa6002c3f02c4203f284b04489`; [2026 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202025-06-27.PDF), physical PDF p. 427, SHA-256 `5a7f83834afe356c05c3db5ae25f4c100fa1642a72ce67ff84999ec99f454340`.

## Lawn Forebay Dam

The Lawn Forebay Dam was originally rockfill. Concrete work in 1937 added a base and encased the rockfill compartments. In 1948, the downstream surface was refaced with concrete. Mesh-reinforced shotcrete was applied in 1983.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 185. SHA-256 `80f3ff5963cb77172f9b48c3a9ea97255c554c3291c0eb35d823652dbbb3190c`.

## Lower Churchill flow record

An Environment Canada hydrometric station began operating on the Churchill River at Upper Muskrat Falls in 1948. Continuous recording began in 1953. The project study states that the station recorded natural flow until the Churchill Falls hydroelectric development regulated the river.

Source: [Muskrat Falls Project Exhibit 50](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit50.pdf), physical PDF p. 32. SHA-256 `4856a2ab3fbc76162af70f5319d9b38b6b11b2f5b3c426a9319a2bda2166c02c`.

## Corporate record

A Moody's corporate history filed in Newfoundland Power's 2019 rate proceeding reports that Newfoundland Light and Power became a pure electricity company in 1948 and listed its shares in 1949. This is a filed secondary corporate-history source, not a contemporary 1948 utility record.

Source: [CA-NP-110 Attachment T](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2019/NP2019GRA/rfi/CA-NP-110.PDF), physical PDF p. 220. SHA-256 `75c05caee18610adfe32b2fbe140947f30b7e9bd2953841acc837f6f2d2a3857`.
