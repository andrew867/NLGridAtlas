# 1903 System Record

## Black River pulp mill

The *Newfoundland Quarterly* records that the Black River pulp mill closed in 1903 because of high operating costs and insufficient water power. It says construction began in May 1897 and included a dam, an artificial falls and water turbines intended to provide 12,000 hp for mill machinery.

This record describes direct mechanical water power. It does not identify an electric generator, generator voltage, transmission line or grid connection. The 12,000 hp value is therefore retained as the article states it and is not converted into an electrical megawatt rating.

Source: [Bob McNeily, "Black River Pulp Mill," *Newfoundland Quarterly*, Vol. XCII, No. 1](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/quarterly/TheNewfoundlandQuarterlyvolume92no1Summer1998.pdf), physical PDF p. 16, printed p. 14. SHA-256 `6b501adf29cbd85be795b84f686b1fdcce664af4d446d01b9da132c6c1342434`.

## Federal survey record

A 1966 Department of Fisheries stream survey independently records the 1903 end of production. It identifies Black River as the site of Newfoundland's first pulp mill, gives a 20 ton-per-day operating capacity, and records a 30 foot dam at the river mouth that supplied water for mill operations. The report says part of the dam was opened in 1946.

Source: [Lester G. Riche, *A Stream Survey Report of Black and North Harbour Rivers*](https://waves-vagues.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/library-bibliotheque/10260.pdf), physical PDF p. 12, report p. 7. SHA-256 `4a926bf30bbbdc60d70cd4217064ca61d29aa532cc051364893f9cc1754ed520`.
