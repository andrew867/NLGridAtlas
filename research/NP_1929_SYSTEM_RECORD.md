# 1929 System Search

## Lawn hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power places the original Lawn development in service in 1929. The plant originally had two Voith turbines. A 1983 refurbishment replaced them with one 600 kW Barber turbine and one Ideal Electric generator operating under about 24.3 m net head.

The woodstave penstock register also dates Lawn's original penstock to 1929. It records a 1981 replacement and 286 m of woodstave pipe.

## Public Utilities Commission

The provincial government created a Public Utilities Commission in 1929 to improve consistency in electrical rates and services. The commission could investigate matters referred by government. It did not have general authority to set rates or direct service. Rate-setting authority following a government referral was added in 1933. The Board of Commissioners of Public Utilities with general supervisory authority followed in 1949.

## Bay Bulls Big Pond property

Newfoundland Power's property schedule records a Bay Bulls Big Pond parcel acquired from William Frizzelle on September 25, 1929. The deed was registered in Volume 108, Folio 492.
