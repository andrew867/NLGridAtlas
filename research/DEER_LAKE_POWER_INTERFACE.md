# Deer Lake Power island interface

## Dated records

| Record | Filed fact | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2013 system one-line | Deer Lake Power is shown with seven 60 Hz generating units and an 81 MW plant assignment. The drawing shows the plant connected into the western island network. | 2013 General Rate Application, Volume II, physical PDF p. 80 |
| 2017 generation table | The Island Generation Supply Table lists DLPG1-7 at a combined 81.1 MW gross continuous unit rating. The frequency converter is listed separately at 18.0 MW. | Near-Term Generation Adequacy Report, physical PDF pp. 61 to 62 |
| 2018 connection record | L1 connects the Deer Lake Power generating station to Massey Drive. L16 and L17 connect Massey Drive to the Corner Brook frequency converter. L1, L16, and L17 connect to 66 kV bus B4 at Massey Drive. Hydro states that it wheels power on L1 to Newfoundland Power at Marble Mountain and Pasadena. | Industrial Customer Specifically Assigned Assets Review, physical PDF pp. 7 to 8 and 23 |
| 2015 TL225 record | TL225 is a 66 kV line between Deer Lake Power and Deer Lake Terminal Station. | PUB-NLH-309, physical PDF p. 4 |

## Atlas treatment

The Deer Lake Power station record shows the 60 Hz generating group and the two separately filed island connections, L1 to Massey Drive and TL225 to Deer Lake. The Corner Brook frequency converter remains a separate record because the 2017 generation table reports it separately from DLPG1-7.

The public records used here do not provide generator-by-generator ratings or a current plant switchgear one-line. The station diagram therefore stops at the filed plant bus and circuit connections.
