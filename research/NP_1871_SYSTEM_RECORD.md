# 1871 System Record

## Directory basis

The source title page identifies the work as *Lovell's Province of Newfoundland Directory, For 1871*. The telegraph entries are directory listings, not construction or commissioning records.

## New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company offices

The directory explicitly identifies company offices at six places:

- Black River, described as a telegraph station at the head of Placentia Bay, with A. Blackader listed as telegraph operator;
- Brigus, with Benjamin Gardner listed as operator;
- Carbonear, with George A. Nichole listed as operator and company agent;
- Chapel Arm, with J. P. Dray listed as telegraph operator;
- Harbor Grace, with W. H. Thompson listed as agent;
- St. John's, with A. M. Mackay listed as general agent and D. Stott as operator at Market House, Water Street.

The St. John's directory also lists F. J. Wyatt as agent for the Anglo-American Telegraph Company at 267 Water Street.

## Heart's Content

The Heart's Content entry describes the New York, Newfoundland and London Telegraph Company's line and lists a substantial group of telegraph operators. It also identifies a manager of the Atlantic Telegraph Company. This establishes Heart's Content as an operating telegraph centre in the directory, but the entry is not phrased as the same generic office notice used for the six places above.
