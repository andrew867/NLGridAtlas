# 1925 System Search

## Deer Lake Power generating station

The Statistics Canada generating-station table filed as IC-1 lists the Deer Lake plant on Grand Lake with nine units, commissioning years from 1925 to 1929, and 127,084 kW of plant capacity. The range establishes that the generating fleet began entering service in 1925. It does not assign a commissioning year or capacity to an individual unit.

Source: [IC-1, Electric Power Generating Stations 2004](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/IC-1.pdf), physical PDF p. 3, local file `mirror/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/IC-1.pdf`, SHA-256 `f886b2ec225a92093c9306ba1849340e006c0b95d44d4a1beee2f145311827e7`.

## Corner Brook mill electrical system

Hydro's 2017 Capital Budget Application says the Corner Brook Pulp and Paper mill was originally constructed in 1925 and contained predominantly 50 Hz power and electrical equipment. The same page explains that frequency converters were added in the early to mid 1960s to connect the 50 Hz paper mills to the new 60 Hz island grid.

This is a mill-construction and electrical-system record. It is not a 1925 date for the later Hydro frequency converter.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application, Volume II](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NLH2017Capital/files/applications/From%20NLH%20-%202017%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202016-07-28.PDF), physical PDF p. 592, local file `mirror/applications/2017/NLH2017Capital/files/applications/From NLH - 2017 Capital Budget Application - Volume II - 2016-07-28.PDF`, SHA-256 `172e69b10bd7109dd2b0712d55a7b898543be3bdf7005d594763231ead734079`.
