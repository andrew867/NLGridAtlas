# 1951 System Record

## Mobile hydro development

Newfoundland Power records the Mobile plant as commissioned in 1951 at 11.0 MVA under approximately 114.6 m net head. The generating unit used a 13,000 hp vertical Francis turbine and Canadian Westinghouse generator MOP-G1. The original powerhouse, crane and Westinghouse switchgear also date to 1951. The switchgear included original potential transformers, current transformers, emergency station-service transformers, protective relays, controls and meters. The generator retained its original stator windings and rotor when assessed in 2022.

The turbine record distinguishes the original machine from its later water-passage components. Dominion Engineering Works supplied the original 13,000 hp turbine in 1951. Voith Hydro supplied the replacement stainless-steel runner and wicket gates in 1990.

Original civil work included the diversion dam, canals, wood-stave and steel penstocks, surge tank and powerhouse. A later filing records a 1,568 m penstock. Its 454 m steel section remained from the 1951 installation; the 1,114 m wood-stave section was replaced with fibreglass in 1990. The original surge tank was replaced in 1999.

## Mobile Plant Substation and MOP-T1

The construction year is inconsistent inside the 2026 Capital Budget Application. The project summary on physical PDF p. 76 says MOP Substation was constructed in 1951. Appendix E on physical PDF p. 441 says 1950. Appendix E also says MOP-T1 was installed in 1951. The 2027 filing gives 1951 for the substation and transformer installation.

MOP-T1 is a 10 MVA, 66/6.9 kV Westinghouse transformer. It connects the 11 MVA generator to the 66 kV line to Mobile Substation.

## Transmission line 20L

20L was a 20.1 km radial 66 kV line between Mobile and Cape Broyle substations. The H-frame wood-pole line supplied more than 1,700 customers through Cape Broyle and Fermeuse. It also connected the Morris, Rocky Pond, Horse Chops and Cape Broyle hydro plants to the grid.

## Tors Cove G3

Tors Cove added a third unit in 1951. G3 used a 2,780 kVA English Electric generator and 3,550 hp Francis turbine. The filing gives a 2,500 kW load rating at 90% power factor. The addition brought the three-unit plant nameplate to 6.5 MW.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf), physical PDF p. 206. SHA-256 `80f3ff5963cb77172f9b48c3a9ea97255c554c3291c0eb35d823652dbbb3190c`.

## Horse Chops transformer

Reliance Electric manufactured asset 200165 in 1951. The 8.0 MVA transformer stepped the plant output from 6.9 kV to 66 kV. The record establishes the manufacturing year, not the installation date.

## Transmission-line date conflicts

The 18L construction year is unresolved between 1951 and 1952. The detailed 2014 report says the original Goulds to Stamp's Lane line was constructed in 1951; rebuild schedules give 1952. The later Glendale reroute dates to 1977.

The 69L construction year is unresolved between 1951 and 1959 within one filing. Its summary table gives 1951; the project text says the Kenmount to Stamp's Lane line was originally constructed in 1959. The rebuild scope covered 2.74 km and 48 single-pole structures.

## Utility property and operations buildings

Newfoundland Power's property schedule records land acquired in Corner Brook on June 29, 1951 and at King's Bridge Substation on July 6, 1951.

Three storage sheds later used at the Electrical Maintenance Centre were constructed by the United States Air Force at Red Cliff in 1951. United Towns Electric bought and moved them to Topsail Road in 1966.
