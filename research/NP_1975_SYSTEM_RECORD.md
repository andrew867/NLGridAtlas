# 1975 system record

## Demand

The historical demand table reports a Newfoundland Power peak of 488.2 MW and a Hydro system peak of 527 MW. The table does not print an Island transmission peak for 1975.

## Wiltondale Terminal Station

The 2013 Capital Budget Application dates Wiltondale Terminal Station to 1975. The station takes a 2.4 km, 66 kV tap from TL226. Breaker B1L29 supplies TL229 toward Glenburnie. Transformer T1 steps 66 kV down to 12.5 kV for WDL-L1 distribution service.

The same report records T1's forced replacement with a used Newfoundland Power transformer in 2008 and the 2009 fence and mobile-substation extension. Its 2007 to 2011 five-year transmission averages were 7.80 interruptions per delivery point and 179.80 hours.

Source: [2013 Capital Budget Application, Volume II](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013Capital/files/application/NLH2013ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf), physical PDF pp. 766 to 775. SHA-256 `68da195dc5a9bf05cb0756bc0989d59a14b7c297e0aaf0106b273c5b1643119b`.

## Holyrood Terminal Station

The terminal-support filing identifies three construction stages. Stage 1 was built in 1965, Stage 2 in 1975, and Stage 3 in 1982. Stages 1 and 2 used aluminum structures; Stage 3 used galvanized steel.

Source: [2017 Capital Budget Application, Volume I](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NLH2017Capital/files/applications/From%20NLH%20-%202017%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%20Volume%20I%20-%202016-07-28.PDF), physical PDF p. 334. ## Stephenville gas-turbine plant

The condition assessment says the Stephenville plant entered service in 1975. Two nominal 25 MW Rolls-Royce Olympus C gas generators drive one shared power turbine and 63.5 MVA generator through clutches. The plant provides standby generation and synchronous-condenser service.

Source: [Condition Assessment and Retirement Optimization Study](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/study/From%20NLH%20-%20Condition%20Assessment%20and%20Retirement%20Optimization%20Study%20-%202026-06-15.PDF), physical PDF pp. 5 to 10. ## Greenhill date conflicts

The 2027 filing says the Curtiss-Wright turbine was commissioned in 1975, originally rated 25 MW and derated to 20 MW in 1984. The 2002 engineering review says a 25 MW turbine was installed near Grand Bank in 1977.

The 2027 filing also contradicts itself on the substation. Physical page 14 says Greenhill Substation was constructed in 1975; physical page 45 says 1977. The atlas assigns no single construction or installation date.

## Transmission records

### 305L

The 2002 Burin Peninsula Engineering Review dates 305L to 1975 and identifies its Greenhill, Webbers Cove, and Laurentian connections. It records partial storm-damage rebuilds in 1996 and 1998.

Source: [2002 Burin Peninsula Engineering Review](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/rfi/PUB%20%2010-2A.pdf), physical PDF p. 5. SHA-256 `ad75cb82626e9a1f33b48a5d828d9d83d8ef64dc457638b91ff2b092257647ba`.

### 100L Clarenville section

The 2026 filing describes 100L as a 34.13 km, 138 kV H-frame line between Sunnyside and Clarenville using 397.5 ACSR conductor. Most of the line dates to 1964. A 2 km section connecting the line to Clarenville Substation was built in 1975.

Source: [2026 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202025-06-27.PDF), physical PDF p. 96. SHA-256 `5a7f83834afe356c05c3db5ae25f4c100fa1642a72ce67ff84999ec99f454340`.
