# 1907 System Record

## Exploits hydro civil works

The 2017 Exploits Generation condition assessment dates the Grand Falls main dam and original forebay intake to 1907. It describes the main dam as a 259 m concrete-gravity structure with wooden flashplanks and a maximum height of 6.1 m. The original intake was a concrete-gravity structure with 17 permanently open, non-operable gates, each 2.44 m by 3.66 m.

The same assessment dates the Bishop's Falls forebay intake to 1907. It identifies a concrete-gravity structure with 13 gates. Gate dimensions and sill elevation were unavailable because the assessment found no drawings.

The Grand Falls record distinguishes the original 1907 works from major 1997 changes, including the RCC dam, right embankment dam, power canal and replacement intake work.

## Petty Harbour Unit 2 date conflict

The 2022 Facility Rehabilitation report identifies Petty Harbour Unit 2 as installed in 1907. It says the turbine runner and generator were replaced in 1984, raising the unit rating to 1,350 kW, while original internal turbine components remained.

The detailed Petty Harbour development record instead dates the plant's three historical turbine-generator sets to 1900, 1908 and 1926 without mapping those dates to present unit identifiers. The atlas retains the 1907 Unit 2 statement and the 1908 historical plant-unit statement as conflicting records.

## Account 325 placement

The account 325 depreciation schedule records an $11,910 original-cost placement in 1907 under prime movers, generators and auxiliaries. The schedule does not identify a plant, unit or location. The amount is not assigned to Petty Harbour or an Exploits asset.
