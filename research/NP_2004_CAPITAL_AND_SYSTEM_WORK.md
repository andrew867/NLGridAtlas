# Newfoundland Power 2004 capital and system records

## Source set

The primary source is Newfoundland Power's revised 2004 Capital Budget Application, filed July 29, 2003. Source: [application PDF](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicCompleteRev1.pdf).

Projects in the application are proposed. A later record is required to classify a project as completed.

## Hydro generation

- Pierre's Brook: replace the original 1931 forebay head gate, guides, lifting equipment and stoplog guides. The filing records a failed September 2002 dewatering attempt and three days of plant unavailability. Source: physical PDF p. 263.
- Topsail: replace the 1983 governor and controls with a PLC-based unit-control panel, generator protection, digital AVR, synchronizer and metering. The filing records 115 unscheduled outages in the preceding five years, with 42 percent linked to speed-signal and bearing inputs and 17 percent to pressure-relief-valve control. Source: physical PDF pp. 263 to 264.
- Morris: replace corroded turbine stationary seals that had released rust and jammed wicket gates. Source: physical PDF p. 265.
- Rattling Brook: rewind the G1 stator after the identical G2 winding failed in 2002. Source: physical PDF pp. 265 to 266.
- Rocky Pond, Rattling Brook, Cape Broyle and Pierre's Brook: replace deteriorated cooling coils. The filing records seven failures since 1997. Source: physical PDF p. 266.

## New Chelsea

The station entered service in January 1957 with 3.7 MW installed capacity. The filing records 15.4 GWh normal annual production and the ability to supply local load after loss of 43L from Heart's Content. Source: physical PDF p. 285.

The proposed work included a generator breaker, digital generator protection, current and potential transformers, a unit-control PLC, synchronizer, AVR, metering, station service, governor replacement, generator rewind, main inlet valve, cooling and ventilation equipment, 66 kV bus and transformer protection, and replacement of 867 m of woodstave penstock and 240 m of steel pipeline. Filed cost was $3.973 million. Source: physical PDF pp. 285 to 293 and 329.

## Stations and feeders

- Port Blandford: replace PBD-124-A6 and PBD-124L-A7 138 kV switches and their structures. The underbuilt wood-pole switches had been tagged out of service, and 124L had to be de-energized to operate the affected equipment. Source: physical PDF pp. 336 to 337.
- Stamp's Lane: replace the oil-insulated cables supplying SLA-T1 and SLA-T2. The 1958 cables had leaking terminations and cracked bodies. Source: physical PDF p. 338.
- Pepperell and Summerford: replace one 66 kV breaker at each station. The Pepperell breaker was 51 years old and did not meet the critical-clearing criterion. The Summerford breaker was about 37 years old and had inconsistent controls. Source: physical PDF p. 340.
- Chamberlains: add a feeder from Fowler's Road to Dunn's Hill Road and transfer approximately 4.5 MVA from Kelligrews. Source: physical PDF p. 347.
- Pulpit Rock: add a feeder along Country Drive and Manning's Hill to Torbay Road for Torbay, Flatrock and Pouch Cove. Source: physical PDF pp. 347 to 348.
- Walbournes and Bayview: replace 15 MVA WAL-T2 with a 25 MVA unit and move the 15 MVA transformer to Bayview. The filing records 2002 peaks of 19.0 MVA on WAL-T1, 16.7 MVA on WAL-T2 and 19.9 MVA at Bayview. Source: physical PDF pp. 349 and 352 to 359.

## Transmission rebuilds

- 3L, Goulds to Petty Harbour: rebuild 4.7 km of the 1930 line. Source: physical PDF p. 388.
- 16L, Pepperell to King's Bridge: rebuild and increase the capacity of 2.0 km of the 1950 line. Source: physical PDF pp. 388 to 389.
- 38L, Seal Cove to Duffs: increase the capacity of 2.8 km of the 1961 line. Source: physical PDF p. 389.
- 116L, Hare Bay to Wesleyville: replace 25 poles and associated hardware. Source: physical PDF pp. 389 to 390.
- 123L, Clarenville to Catalina: replace approximately 1,200 defective insulators and associated hardware. Source: physical PDF p. 390.
- 124L, Clarenville to Gambo: replace 16 poles, structures and associated hardware. Source: physical PDF pp. 390 to 391.
- 132L, Grand Falls to Bishop's Falls: replace approximately 1,100 insulators on 52 structures. Source: physical PDF p. 391.
- 403L, St. George's to Lookout Brook: rebuild 5.1 km and complete selective work on a further 4.0 km. Source: physical PDF pp. 391 to 392.

## St. John's transmission review

The study rated conductors at 25 C summer and 0 C winter ambient temperatures, 0.61 m/s wind and 75 C conductor temperature. Source: physical PDF p. 396.

The 2004 forecast for Duffs, Seal Cove, Kelligrews, Chamberlains and Hardwoods totalled 53.8 MVA against the 40.3 MVA winter rating of 38L. The proposed upgrade table increased the Golden Eagle Tap to Seal Cove section to 96.0 MVA summer and 116.4 MVA winter. Source: physical PDF pp. 400 and 404.

The same table proposed work on 16L in 2004, 30L and 69L in 2007, and 12L in 2008. These are study recommendations, not completion records. Source: physical PDF p. 404.

The station table separates 2002 actual peaks from 2003 to 2008 forecasts. Oxen Pond T1 is listed at 13.3 MVA with an 8.5 MVA actual 2002 peak and a 9.3 MVA 2004 forecast. Source: physical PDF p. 407.
