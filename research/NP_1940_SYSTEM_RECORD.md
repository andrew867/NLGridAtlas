# 1940 System Record

## Tors Cove generation record

A later Newfoundland Power engineering assessment dates the Tors Cove powerhouse to 1940. It gives an installed capacity of 6,900 kW under 52.7 m net head and describes three horizontal Francis turbines and their generators as English Electric equipment commissioned in 1940.

The same page first says the powerhouse contains one generating unit, then describes three turbines. A separate generating-unit register lists TCV-G1 and TCV-G2 at 2.0 MW each with 1940 installation dates and TCV-G3 at 2.3 MW with a 1951 installation date. Later filings date plant service or commissioning to 1941 and 1942. These statements are retained as separate records.

## Governor record

A 2003 capital filing describes the G2 and G3 governor systems as original to the 1940 construction of the station and identifies English Electric as the governor, generator and turbine manufacturer. That statement conflicts with the generating-unit register, which dates G3 to 1951. The filing does not explain whether equipment was reused or whether the 1940 reference is an error.
