## Pulpit Rock feeder work

The filed project converted a 1.0 km section of PUL-01 along Marine Drive and Torquay Place from single phase to three-phase 1/0 AASC. The filing records approximately 1,300 customers and an actual section load of approximately 116 A against an 85 A planning criterion. It also converted a 1.2 km section of PUL-04 along Western Island Pond Drive, Forest River Road, and Captain Matthew Davis Drive from single phase to three-phase 1/0 AASC. PUL-04 also served approximately 1,300 customers, physical PDF pp. 41 to 42 and 361 to 370.

The Q3 report records PUL-04 complete and PUL-01 nearing completion after all poles were installed, physical PDF p. 13. The Q4 report records the two Torbay load-growth upgrades complete, physical PDF p. 12. The capital expenditure report records $732,000 actual expenditure against $670,000 approved, with no carryover to 2024, physical PDF p. 10.

## Corner Brook hospital supply

The filed hospital project identifies WAL-07 from Walbournes as the main supply and BVS-03 from Bayview as the requested redundant supply. The hospital demand was estimated at 7.8 MVA. The BVS-03 scope included a 2.5 km single-phase to three-phase conversion, 1.2 km of feeder relocation, 400 m of underground infrastructure, 1.0 km of right-of-way widening and pole work, and 4.0 km of new feeder along Confederation Drive and Crockers Road. The filing classifies the backup supply as a customer-funded special facility, physical PDF pp. 29 to 31.

The Q3 report records the redundant supply complete, physical PDF p. 13. The capital expenditure report records $2.467 million actual expenditure against $2.690 million approved, with no carryover to 2024, physical PDF p. 10.

## Other 2023 closeouts

The Q4 report records the Molloy's Lane and Walbournes modernization projects complete. It also records the Long Pond 25 MVA transformer installation complete, physical PDF p. 12. The expenditure report carries $180,000 for Molloy's Lane and $260,000 for Walbournes into 2024. Long Pond has no carryover in that table, physical PDF pp. 4 and 8. The carryover is financial closeout evidence. It does not reverse the equipment completion statements.

The same Q4 report records all poles and structure frames installed on an 11 km section of 55L between Blaketown and Placentia Junction. It records a 22 km section of 94L from Riverhead to St. Catherines complete, physical PDF p. 13. The 2023 expenditure report records $2.223 million carried into 2024 for 55L and reports $7.899 million total actual expenditure on the 2022 to 2024 94L project, physical PDF pp. 17, 29 and 34. Later filings revise both corridor records and remain the controlling sources for final scope and expenditure.

## Telecommunications

Newfoundland Power reported the St. John's teleprotection replacement installed and commissioned in 2023. It migrated 24 transmission lines in St. John's and the surrounding area to the new system, physical PDF p. 13.

The annual financial report repeats the 94L and telecommunications carryover amounts on physical PDF p. 52. It does not add equipment topology or commissioning detail beyond the project records above.
