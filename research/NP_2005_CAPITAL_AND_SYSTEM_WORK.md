# Newfoundland Power 2005 capital and system records

## Source set

The primary filing is Newfoundland Power's 2005 Capital Budget Application, filed August 31, 2004. Source: [application PDF](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2005cap/files/applic/np2005capApplicComplete.pdf).

The application describes proposed work. Later completion records are identified separately.

## Wesleyville gas turbine

The filing proposed a $1.124 million overhaul of the Rolls-Royce AVON gas generator that had been moved from Salt Pond to Wesleyville. Source: physical PDF pp. 170 to 174.

PUB-31.1-NP records three inspections: Trans Canada Turbine in 2000, then Rolls-Royce before and after the 2003 relocation. Both Rolls-Royce inspections recommended overhaul. Source: [PUB-31.1-NP](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2005cap/files/rfi/PUB-31-1NP.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.

The 2007 application later reports that the existing unit was exchanged for a zero-hour-rated refurbished gas generator supplied by Siemens Canada, installed, and commissioned at Wesleyville. Final cost was $1.139 million. Source: 2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 139.

## Rattling Brook

The plant entered service December 16, 1958. The filing describes two 8,500 hp vertical Francis turbines and two 7,500 kVA generators. The electrical assessment identifies original 1958 switchgear, generator cables, mechanical voltage regulators, and a vacuum-tube synchronizer. Source: physical PDF pp. 188 to 193 and 283 to 292.

The 2005 request was $350,000 for detailed assessment and design. PUB-32.1-NP states that it was not approval for the later construction program. Source: [PUB-32.1-NP](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2005cap/files/rfi/PUB-32-1NP.pdf), physical PDF p. 1.

The 2021 capital filing later records the 66 kV transmission, distribution, and generation substation refurbishment as completed in 2007. Source: 2021 Capital Budget Application Volume II, physical PDF pp. 108 to 112.

## Substation equipment

- Humber: the filing proposed replacing corroded cooling radiators on two transformers purchased in 1968 and 1974 with galvanized units. Filed cost was $174,000. Source: physical PDF pp. 34 to 35.
- St. John's Main: three 15 kV metalclad switchgear sections contained 17 air circuit breakers. The filing proposed two enclosing buildings at $251,000. Source: physical PDF pp. 312 to 313.
- Rocky Pond: the filing proposed replacing a 27-year-old 6.9 kV breaker for which replacement parts were obsolete. Source: physical PDF p. 315.
- Hardwoods: the HWD-04 25 kV feeder breaker had a 12.5 kA interrupting rating against an approximately 16 kA calculated station fault level. The filing proposed its replacement. Source: physical PDF p. 315.

These are filed scopes. No completion state is assigned without a later record.

## Transmission rebuilds

- 11L: 66 kV, Tors Cove to Mobile, built in 1942. The filing proposed rebuilding 5.0 km for $343,000. Source: physical PDF p. 319.
- 43L: 66 kV, Heart's Content to New Chelsea, built in 1956. The filing proposed rebuilding 8.0 km of the 25.1 km radial line for $707,000. Later filings identify a 12.0 km 2006 stage and the final 5.1 km 2007 stage. Source: physical PDF p. 320; 2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 178 and 181.
- 124L: 138 kV, Clarenville to Gambo, built in 1964. The filing proposed rebuilding 5.0 km for $500,000 and records earlier stages of 5.2 km in 2001 and 5.5 km in 2003. Source: physical PDF p. 321.

## St. John's east end

The planning study records the 2004 station arrangement:

- Virginia Waters: three 25 MVA transformers, seven existing 12.5 kV feeders, and approximately 5,800 customers. The third transformer had been added in 2003.
- Ridge Road: one 2.2 MVA bank on a 4.16 kV bus and two 20 MVA banks on a 12.5 kV bus, with approximately 4,200 customers.
- Broad Cove: one 25 MVA transformer, four 12.5 kV feeders, and approximately 4,100 customers.
- Pulpit Rock: one 25 MVA transformer, PUL-01 and PUL-02 in service, PUL-03 being added in 2004, and approximately 3,500 customers.

Source: physical PDF pp. 359 to 360 and 371.

The selected alternative proposed VIR-08 in 2005 and later feeder and transformer additions. Its filed net present value was $2.870 million. The other alternatives were $3.566 million and $3.655 million. These are planning values, not actual expenditures. Source: physical PDF pp. 361 to 365.
