# 1898 System Record

## Petty Harbour site and dam work

Brian Wadden's 1965 historical account says consulting engineer G. H. Massey arrived in St. John's on May 7, 1898. A survey team under Engineer Joyce began surveying Water Street. Massey went to Petty Harbour and selected a dam site whose stored water would supply the generators. The account says twelve men were soon working on dam construction.

This record establishes site selection and early dam work. Newfoundland Power's 1999 annual report separately says hydroelectric plant construction began at Petty Harbour in 1899. The atlas retains both statements instead of treating the 1898 civil work as proof that the generating plant or its electrical equipment was under construction.
