# 1894 System Record

## Grand Falls survey

A. P. Low's report places the Geological Survey party at Grand Falls during the 1893 to 1894 expedition. The party hauled its equipment and provisions up the Hamilton River from January 19 to the middle of May.

Low recorded the river surface at 1,660 ft above sea level before the descent and the valley at the mouth of the gorge near 900 ft. The report gives a total descent of 760 ft in 12 mi. It estimates discharge at Grand Falls at about 50,000 ft3/s and records a 302 ft main fall. The remaining descent was in rapids and the canyon below the main fall.

The report does not calculate electrical output or describe a generating plant. Later accounts that convert the site description into electrical power are not used for the 1894 values.
