# Newfoundland Power and Island system record, 1983

## Demand and planning

- Newfoundland Power peak demand was 742.6 MW. Hydro Rural peak demand was 49.2 MW. Island transmission peak demand was 1,251 MW and Hydro system peak demand was 1,040 MW. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- The 1981 supply-planning case forecast a 1,362 MW coincident peak, 1,594 MW installed capability, 0.15 loss-of-load-probability index, 7,059 GWh energy requirement, 7,455 GWh firm capability, and a 396 GWh surplus. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)

The located comparative capital table starts in 1984. The located comparative reliability table starts in 1987. No supported 1983 Newfoundland Power capital, SAIFI, SAIDI, energy-sales, or customer-account total was located.

## Upper Salmon and the Bay d'Espoir watershed

- Upper Salmon entered service in January 1983. [NP-44 Revised, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/filings/hydresp/NFpower/NP44Revised.pdf)
- The contemporary Board report describes Upper Salmon as an 86 MW facility. The later 2013 Island system one-line lists 84 MW. The filings do not explain the difference. [1983 Electric Power Control Act report, physical PDF p. 24](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/Consents/83PMARCH.pdf); [2013 Application Volume II, physical PDF p. 80](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013GRA/files/application/Application-VolumeII.pdf)
- A later engineering report describes the single 84 MW unit and its 463 m penstock. The penstock tapers from 7.0 m at the intake to 6.1 m near the spiral case. [PUB-NLH-005 Attachment 6, physical PDF p. 436](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/rfis/PUB-NLH-005.PDF)
- A severe January flood exceeded the probable maximum precipitation used for the original Bay d'Espoir design. Hydro commissioned flood studies for the reservoir system and the Salmon basin. [Muskrat Falls Project Exhibit 54, physical PDF pp. 27 to 28](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit54.pdf)

## Transmission

- TL241 was built from Hawke's Bay to Plum Point to 138 kV standard but operated at 66 kV. TL244 was built from Plum Point to Bear Cove to 66 kV standard. Both lines were upgraded for 138 kV operation in 1995. [IC-270, physical PDF p. 35](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/filings/Sept10/Hydro/pkg19.pdf)
- Hydro's 1983 annual-report passage describes 84 km of 138/69 kV line from Hawke's Bay to Flowers Cove. The work connected about 30 communities from Castors River to Eddies Cove that had previously used diesel generation. [CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 31](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/NLH2018CostofService/rfis/CA-NP-001.PDF)
- TL248 was built from Deer Lake to Massey Drive. With TL239 and the later TL247, it formed the Deer Lake 138 kV ring-bus arrangement established during 1983 and 1984. [PUB-NLH-318, physical PDF p. 4](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-318.pdf)

## Newfoundland Power hydroelectric work

- Morris entered service upstream of Mobile First Pond. The 1,135 kW plant used one Barber horizontal Francis turbine and one Ideal Electric induction generator under 30.0 m net head. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 137](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- Topsail received a major refurbishment that replaced the turbine, generator, switchgear, protection, and control systems. The Barber Voest-Alpine electronic governor also dates to this work. [2004 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 270](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicComplete.pdf)
- The Topsail development record dates the 1,915 m by 1,067 mm penstock and the extended intake to 1983. The new Barber turbine and Ideal Electric generator replaced the original 1.2 MW unit. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 198](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Lawn was substantially refurbished. One 600 kW Barber horizontal Francis turbine and one Ideal Electric generator replaced the two original Voith units. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 140](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)

## Station assets

- Morris received 66 kV and 2.4 kV wood structures. The station table records 1.5 MVA total transformer capacity. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 192](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- St. John's Main received a steel 12.47 kV low-voltage structure with switchgear. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 192](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- Long Lake received wood 66 kV and 25 kV structures. The table records 14.9 MVA total transformer capacity. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 201](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- Grand Bay's 66 kV steel high-voltage structure is dated to 1983. A later project report describes the station as built in 1984. The filings do not explain the one-year difference. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 201](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf); [2020 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 364](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2020/NP2020Capital/applications/NP%20-%202020%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202019-07-05.PDF)
- Hydro installed a 3 MVAR, 66 kV capacitor bank at Grand Bay for peak voltage support. [Island Transmission System Outlook, physical PDF p. 24](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit24-IslandTransmissionOutlook.pdf)

## VHF communications

Newfoundland Power bought Midland 70-341 VHF mobile radios and installed the Torbayman's Pond VHF tower. The later inventory does not give the number of Midland units bought in 1983. [PUB-69 NP, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/rfi/PUB-69NP.pdf)
