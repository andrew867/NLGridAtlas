# Bay d'Espoir Unit 8 design and early execution

Updated: 2026-11-13

## Filed electrical design

The June 2025 basis of design specifies a nominal 150 MW hydroelectric unit in the Powerhouse 2 extension. The turbine is a Francis machine coupled to the generator by a vertical shaft. The generator is a synchronous machine capable of synchronous-condenser operation, with static excitation.

The proposed generator step-up transformer is an oil-filled, three-phase, 129/172 MVA unit rated 13.8/230 kV. Its external dimensions are to be essentially the same as the Unit 7 transformer so the spare arrangement remains interchangeable. These requirements are on physical PDF pp. 5 and 8 to 9 of Attachment 12.

## Early execution record

Order P.U. 17(2025) approved $16,670,000 for Unit 8 early execution work. Order P.U. 21(2026), dated July 8, 2026, approved another $5,630,000. The two approvals total $22,300,000.

The October 2026 update records completed geotechnical field investigations. The 230 kV breaker request for proposals was under review and negotiation, but the Unit 8 breakers could not yet be awarded because that purchase was outside the approved early execution budget. Turbine and generator supplier engagement was paused, with no financial commitments made on that package. The commercial operation date was unknown.
