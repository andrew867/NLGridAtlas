# 1896 System Record

## St. John's electric street-railway proposal

An April 14, 1896 public notice said the petitioners would seek a charter for the Saint John's Street Railway Company. The proposed company would construct a single or double street railway and operate it by electricity or another authorized motive power.

Source: [The Evening Herald, April 14, 1896](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/eveherald/18960414vol07no87EveningHerald.pdf), physical PDF p. 1. SHA-256 `c0eadfc6f13f3a85d38181208497fb1d734342c1f3f9998bc0618acd5bd0b6a9`.

The legislature passed the incorporation bill later that year. The August 7 Daily News reports that the Governor declined assent and reserved the bill pending Her Majesty's pleasure. The 1896 record is therefore a proposal and legislative step, not proof of an incorporated company or operating electrical system.

Source: [The Daily News, August 7, 1896](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/dailynews/DailyNews18960807.pdf), physical PDF p. 4. SHA-256 `7df15216849ddb603e8674957fb9a08503c570e40e1f7eaf5d3d884c1eaf526a`.

## Black River company

The later Black River history says the Newfoundland Wood Pulp Company was formed in England in 1896. Physical construction began in May 1897. The company formation is not treated as commissioning of water-power or electrical equipment.

Source: [Black River Pulp Mill](https://dai.mun.ca/pdfs/quarterly/TheNewfoundlandQuarterlyvolume92no1Summer1998.pdf), Newfoundland Quarterly, Summer 1998, physical PDF p. 16, printed p. 14. SHA-256 `6b501adf29cbd85be795b84f686b1fdcce664af4d446d01b9da132c6c1342434`.
