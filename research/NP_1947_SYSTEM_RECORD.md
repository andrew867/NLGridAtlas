# 1947 System Record

## Muskrat Falls survey control

The 1999 Muskrat Falls feasibility study records a first-level traverse by Commander Desbarats in 1947. The survey established a benchmark at 72.04 ft above the Muskrat Falls site. The study says that benchmark was the reference for the 1965 site mapping.

Source: [Muskrat Falls Final Feasibility Study](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit19-MuskratFallsFeasibilityStudy1999.pdf), physical PDF p. 69. SHA-256 `0660aace240d7df9e987d51e81609508eaa5617264cabe5b2ec0c6ad0ae665d3`.

## Hamilton Falls benchmark

A development-area description filed with the 2009 Churchill Falls water-rights application identifies Benchmark J at Hamilton Falls as a 1947 G. H. Desbarats benchmark. The description uses it as the elevation reference for Area 1 of the Twin Falls development area.

Source: [Application Volume II Revised](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2009/Nalcor2009Water/files/applic/Application-VolumeII-Revised.pdf), physical PDF p. 135. SHA-256 `ca437445bf742f7111f0af39cc003693a0655eb575d2c2936220b3043f85a7cf`.

## Port aux Basques diesel electrical plant

Newfoundland Power's Account 332 depreciation schedule assigns $1,700 of Port aux Basques Diesel electrical-plant original cost to 1947. The row identifies the plant and account but not the equipment represented by that cost.

Source: [2003 General Rate Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/Application/CompleteApplicationRev1.pdf), physical PDF p. 1049. SHA-256 `f927aa2c20da8ba4275708b1c0afeec77b14b2d747917d4f09e15400783c4a52`.

## Search result
