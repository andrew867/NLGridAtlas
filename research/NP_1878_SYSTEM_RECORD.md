# 1878 System Record

## First recorded telephone line

The Encyclopedia of Newfoundland and Labrador reports that the first recorded telephones in Newfoundland entered service in St. John's on March 20, 1878. A private point-to-point line connected the homes of Postmaster General John Delaney and meteorological recorder John Higgins.

Delaney built the instruments from instructions and illustrations in Scientific American. The encyclopedia cites a Royal Gazette report that the apparatus worked and transmitted speech. The original 1878 Royal Gazette issue is not in the digitized collection, so the event remains reported rather than verified from the contemporary article.

## Northern Telegraph financing

The 1878 Supply Act appropriated $765 to pay interest on $17,000 advanced by the Newfoundland Savings' Bank for construction of the Northern Telegraph.

The act identifies the financed system but gives no route, line length, conductor, office equipment, construction stage or commissioning date.
