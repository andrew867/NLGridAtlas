# 1938 System Search

## Grand Falls Generator No. 4 date conflict

A 1992 Newfoundland Supreme Court judgment filed with the North Atlantic Refinery proceeding gives conflicting installation years for Grand Falls Generator No. 4. Physical PDF p. 132 says the unit was installed in 1938. Physical PDF p. 135 says it was installed in 1937. The atlas marks the installation year unresolved and presents the conflict with the 1937 record.

Source: [Abitibi-Price Inc. v. Voith Hydro Inc., included in NARL written submissions](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2013NARL/files/corresp/WrittenSubmissionsofNorthAtlantic.PDF), physical PDF pp. 131 to 135, SHA-256 `b7834bf0e6b77405d42ad46511a20f57b1c49b2a6955a0c4500e1276295e3831`.

## Remaining archive result

Exact-year searches covered generation, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, hydro civil works, property deeds and grants, transmission lines, circuits, feeders, telecommunications, system demand, reliability, storms and outages.

The returned records were:

- Aggregate accounting installation and retirement rows without named assets.
- Depreciation placement or experience bands beginning in 1938.
- Capital-market, legal and statistical material unrelated to the Newfoundland grid.
- Generating-station records from utilities outside Newfoundland and Labrador.
- Modern operating times or engineering values that contain the digits 1938.

Apart from the conflicting G4 installation year, none establishes a Newfoundland system event in 1938. The atlas therefore has no separate 1938 public card or timeline entry.

## Boundary with adjacent records

Fall Pond commissioning and turbine installation are explicitly dated to 1939. Tors Cove generation and property records begin in 1940. Those adjacent records remain assigned to their filed years. Grand Falls G4 remains unresolved between 1937 and 1938.

## Search rule

Decade-level statements, accounting totals and unnamed plant balances were not converted into asset histories. A later exact-year primary record can reopen this queue.
