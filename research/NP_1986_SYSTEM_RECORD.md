# Newfoundland Power 1986 system record

## Annual indicators

- Newfoundland Power reported actual capital expenditure of $32,184,000. [CA-NP-146 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-146,%20Attachment%20B.pdf)
- A later Island demand table reports a Newfoundland Power peak of 847.8 MW, a Hydro Rural peak of 43.9 MW, an Island transmission peak of 1,291 MW, and a Hydro system peak of 1,084 MW for 1986. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- A 1997 marginal-cost study instead prints 863 MW as Newfoundland Power's actual 1986 peak. The two filings differ by 15.2 MW and do not explain the difference. [IC-59, physical PDF pp. 336 and 338](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hydro2006gra/files/rfi/IC-59.pdf)
- A 1981 planning table projected a 1,548 MW coincident Island peak, 1,600 MW installed capability, 3.58 loss-of-load probability, 8,046 GWh required energy, 7,490 GWh firm energy, and a 556 GWh deficit for 1986. These are planning figures, not the later historical results. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)
- No supported 1986 Newfoundland Power SAIFI, SAIDI, CAIDI, annual energy, sales, or customer total was located. The comparative distribution-reliability table begins in 1987.

## Transmission and terminal stations

- Oxen Pond placed T2, rated 75/100/125 MVA at 230/66 kV, in service in 1986. The original Oxen Pond T2 was moved to Hardwoods and became T3, rated 40/53.3/66.6 MVA. [Oxen Pond application, physical PDF p. 17](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLHCBSUPP2013/oxenpond/application/2013-03-20AppOxenPond.PDF)
- Newfoundland Power built 7.2 km of 138 kV wood H-frame construction on 124L in 1986. [2022 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 343](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202022%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202021-05-18.PDF)
- A March 29 storm caused four structures to fail on TL220. Hydro reports restoration in 1 1/3 days. [PUB-NLH-299, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-299.pdf)
- Western Avalon installed a Staticon battery charger serving station protection and control power. [2006 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 73](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hydro2006cap/files/applic/NLH2006ApplicationComplete.pdf)

## Generation, controls, and civil work

- Holyrood reinstalled its gas turbine in a custom building with a new exhaust stack and silencer and a new inlet filter house. The power-turbine casings were replaced. The same study records a governor, synchronizer, and monitoring-system installation in 1986. [NP-NLH-022, physical PDF pp. 39 to 40, 60, 66 to 67 and 96](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLHCBSUPP2013/HolyroodDieselPU33/files/rfi/NP-NLH-022.PDF)
- Consecutive pages in that study disagree on the Gem 80/500 controller year. Physical PDF p. 66 says 1987; physical PDF p. 67 says 1986. The atlas leaves the controller year unresolved.
- Holyrood Unit 3 was converted for synchronous-condenser operation. The existing Unit 3 record retains the conversion and its source page.
- Petty Harbour overhauled Units 2 and 3. Unit 2 was rewound and received a brushless exciter; Voith EHR530 governors were installed on both units. [2006 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 178, 186 and 188](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2006cap/files/applic/NP2006ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- New Chelsea added remote supervisory control and data acquisition control. [2004 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 28](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicComplete.pdf)
- Rattling Brook replaced the Unit 2 runner with an Allis-Chalmers runner. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 393](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/applic/NP2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Port Union replaced two cylinder heads and four pistons on its Caterpillar D398A diesel unit. [PUB-NP-175, physical PDF p. 176](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-175.pdf)
- Cat Arm installed its station-service programmable logic controller. [2015 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 8 to 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2015/NLH2015Capital/files/application/NLH-2015-Capital-Budget-Application-Volume-II-2014-08-01.PDF)
- Stephenville installed an IRD vibration-monitoring system. Holyrood Unit 1 replaced its high-pressure feedwater heaters. [PUB-174, physical PDF pp. 74 and 229](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/rfi/PUB-174.pdf)
- Mobile added an electronic negative-sequence relay to its generating-unit protection. [2023 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 488](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2023/NP2023Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202023%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202022-06-29.PDF)
- Tors Cove installed a woodstave penstock with a 591 m by 2,743 mm upstream section and a 113 m by 2,438 mm downstream section. Sandy Brook replaced the original Island Pond rock-filled timber-crib dams with two earthfill dams, approximately 120 m and 60 m long. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF pp. 123 and 240](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Pitmans Pond installed a three-phase surge capacitor and metal-oxide-varistor arresters on its 2.4 kV bus. [2013 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 231 to 232](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013Capital/files/applic/NP2013ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Heart's Content installed a three-phase surge capacitor on its 2.4 kV bus and replaced surge protection. [2014 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 182 to 183](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/applic/NP2014ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Pierre's Brook installed three single-phase surge capacitors on its 6.9 kV bus. [2016 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 245](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016Capital/files/applic/NP-2016-Capital-Budget-Application-2015-06-23.pdf)
