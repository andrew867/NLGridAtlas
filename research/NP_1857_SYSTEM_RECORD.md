# 1857 System Record

## Company administration

A January 21 report identifies former Newfoundland Attorney General E. M. Archibald as secretary in New York to the New York, Newfoundland and London Electric Telegraph Company. It reports a salary of $2,400, or £600 in Newfoundland currency.

## Telegraph amendment

Chapter I received assent on March 3. The Act authorized the Newfoundland company to consent to the Atlantic Telegraph Company extending its submarine cable to the island and bringing it into a joint station. It authorized consolidation of the companies.

The Act protected each post, wire, insulator, cable, line, building, erection, apparatus, machine and other work against wilful and malicious damage or obstruction. A summary conviction could result in a fine not exceeding £5 or imprisonment not exceeding one calendar month. A company servant, officer or witness could apprehend an offender and take the person before a justice.

## Silver-thaw interruption

On March 3, the Legislative Council president said he had telegraphed absent Conception Bay members but had received no answer. He supposed that silver thaw on the wires caused the interruption. The report does not confirm the fault location or repair time.

## Atlantic cable attempt

A Halifax dispatch dates the accident to 3:45 a.m. on August 11. The report states that 335 nautical miles of cable had been paid out. Water was more than two miles deep for much of the run. The cable was running at somewhat over five knots while the *Niagara* was making four knots. The engineer directed that the brakes be applied more firmly, and the cable parted some distance from the stern.

## Cape Race news service

The November 18 newspaper reports that the Morning Post Office issued news obtained from a Havre steamer off Cape Race. The dispatch carried English dates to November 4. The article uses relative dates and does not state the calendar date of the ship contact.

## Gale outage

The December 16 newspaper states that the telegraph wire had been blown down during the previous week's gale, preventing receipt of later foreign intelligence. It does not identify the damaged section or the repair date.
