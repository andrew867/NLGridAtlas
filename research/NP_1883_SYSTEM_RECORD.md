# 1883 System Record

## Bell Telephone Company charter petition

On March 6, 1883, the Newfoundland House of Assembly received a petition from Lewis Brown McFarlane, attorney for the Bell Telephone Company. Bell requested Newfoundland powers corresponding to its Canadian charter. The petition sought authority to manufacture, sell or lease telephones and related apparatus, and to construct, acquire, maintain and operate telephone lines in Newfoundland.

On March 7, the House appointed a six-member select committee to consider the requested charter. The journal records the petition and committee, but not an enacted Bell charter or an operating Bell system.

## Telephone patent notices

Two notices dated June 18 stated that applications would be made to the Governor in Council for letters patent to the Bell Telephone Company of Canada. One covered improvements in electric telephony; the other covered improvements in speaking telephones. Whiteway and Johnson acted as Bell's solicitors.
