# 1954 System Record

## Transmission lines

Transmission line 25L was built in 1954 between Goulds and St. John's Main. The 2011 filing describes a 9.25 km, 66 kV line with 51 two-pole and three-pole H-frame structures, two steel towers, and non-standard 477 ACSR conductor.

Source: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 375. SHA-256 `7494209347c908d48dc7e2b4a458a46ce39d5395ef43b5737ed93119a9785061`.

Transmission line 24L is a 20 km, 66 kV radial line between Bay Bulls Big Pond and Mobile. The surviving record divides its original construction between 1954 and 1964. The 1954 H-frame section was completely rebuilt in 2002. A section of the 1964 single-pole construction was rebuilt in 2003; 7.7 km of original 1964 structures remained when the 2010 capital application was filed.

Source: [2010 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 331. SHA-256 `e3e004d1aadd10be1624cc33b402203eb60c744f24e4736d1219b94e41579cb2`.

## Substations

Mobile Substation was built in 1954 as a generation substation. The 2008 capital filing describes a later arrangement with three 66 kV circuits, one 33 kV circuit, a 66/12.5 kV distribution transformer, and a 66/33 kV transformer for Pierre's Brook generation.

Source: [2008 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008Capital/files/applic/NP2008ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 174. SHA-256 `0982f4e3c88c5967ccb014c2299758e56b2029abe316da0313098de946820bf2`.

Goulds Substation was built in 1954 as a major 66 kV transmission switching station and a 12.5 kV distribution station. The 2007 filing describes the later five-line 66 kV arrangement, three distribution transformers, three 12.5 kV feeders, and the 33 kV supply to Petty Harbour.

Source: [2007 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/applic/NP2007ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 166. SHA-256 `6cfd75d76b0f7cdb103216420b29a1130a57dc22999f47621951c8322b8c3402`.

Humber Substation was built in 1954 as a transmission and distribution station. Its control building was also built in 1954. The 2022 filing records later equipment and supply arrangements separately from the original construction.

Source: [2022 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2022/NP2022Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202022%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202021-05-18.PDF), physical PDF pp. 304 and 309. SHA-256 `00288c716dd9b077f00a6d5b30d2e876f4dcd0f6988aaafe656f6b0bc0da3f71`.

## Cape Broyle and Horse Chops

The commissioning year is unresolved. The 2013 development record says the Cape Broyle and Horse Chops development was commissioned in 1953. It also dates the 6,000 kW Cape Broyle turbine and 7,650 kW Horse Chops turbine to 1953. The 2025 capital filing dates both plants to 1954, with capacities of 7.0 MVA and 9.0 MVA respectively.

Both records describe one vertical Francis unit at each plant. Cape Broyle uses a Canadian Vickers turbine and Westinghouse generator. Horse Chops uses a Dominion Engineering turbine and Canadian General Electric generator. The plants operate in series, with Horse Chops upstream of Cape Broyle.

Sources: [CA-NP-036](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf), physical PDF pp. 258 to 259, SHA-256 `1b27d6b0a58416c06045f8e54618c33b2902f26dda83d53780a4b01b5e14b477`; [2025 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF), physical PDF pp. 544 to 545, SHA-256 `4d7af5a441cbb7cc82e98766e482afcf59b0ef7cc03497ffab852a5a90c71dd3`.

The Mount Carmel Pond spillway and outlet gate were installed in 1954 as part of the development. The outlet gate was operated manually because no electricity for control equipment was available at the site.

Source: [2025 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF), physical PDF p. 544. SHA-256 `4d7af5a441cbb7cc82e98766e482afcf59b0ef7cc03497ffab852a5a90c71dd3`.

## Petty Harbour penstock

The upper wood-stave section of the Petty Harbour penstock was installed in 1954. It was 250 m long, 2,286 mm in diameter, supported on timber cradles, and originally treated with creosote. The 2019 filing describes a 970 m complete penstock; the remaining 720 m was steel installed in 1999.

Source: [2020 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2020/NP2020Capital/applications/NP%20-%202020%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202019-07-05.PDF), physical PDF pp. 207 to 208. SHA-256 `eb12a773e3d81aa1cfc7b88aae339cfdb009a91fedd54088d9252d1f56366b47`.

## Newfoundland Power Commission

The Newfoundland Power Commission, Hydro's predecessor, was incorporated in 1954. Hydro's 2007 overview states that its original purpose was to electrify the province.

Source: [2007 Capital Budget Overview](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/hydro2007cap/files/applic/NLH2007ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 12. SHA-256 `0360de0ce61e145591bddc4df32656b7cb11890991895bdce5337e2c0e817b12`.
