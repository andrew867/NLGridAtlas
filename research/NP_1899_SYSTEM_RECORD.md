# 1899 System Record

## Petty Harbour construction

Newfoundland Power's 1999 annual report says the August 1999 opening of the Rose Blanche hydroelectric plant marked a century of hydroelectric plant construction that began at Petty Harbour in 1899.

The detailed Petty Harbour plant record separately dates construction of the original development and commissioning of the first turbine-generator set to 1900. The 1899 record is therefore limited to the start of construction.
