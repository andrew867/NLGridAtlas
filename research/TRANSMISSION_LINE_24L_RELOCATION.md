# Transmission Line 24L shoreline relocation

## Result

Newfoundland Power relocated a 1.6 km section of 24L from the Bay Bulls Big Pond shoreline to the roadside of the Southern Shore Highway. The Q2 2026 regulatory report states that the relocation was complete for the quarter ended June 30, 2026. The filing does not give an exact commissioning date.

## Filed engineering record

The 2024 Capital Budget Application identifies 24L as the radial 66 kV connection between Big Pond and Mobile. Its relocation project moved a 1.6 km shoreline section to the highway roadside. Increased water levels had placed the shoreline poles inside the high-water mark; water and ice pressure caused excessive leaning and put the line at risk of failure. The filed project budget was $701,000.

The same application proposed relocating 1.6 km of feeder BIG-02 to the new transmission poles. Its filed budget was $196,000.

NLH-NP-025 confirms that 24L operates at 66 kV. The relocated section lies inside the City of St. John's Bay Bulls Big Pond Protected Public Water Supply Area. Treated wood poles were not permitted, so the estimate used steel transmission poles. Limited access also required construction along the existing BIG-02 roadside right-of-way, with BIG-02 attached as an underbuild.

## Completion evidence

The Q2 2026 regulatory report says, "The relocation of a portion of transmission line 24L in St. John's is complete." This establishes completion by the reporting period ended June 30, 2026. It does not establish an exact in-service date.
