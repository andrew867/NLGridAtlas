The published MHI-Nalcor historical-demand table begins in 1967. No 1964 demand value was located in the reviewed archive.

## Sunnyside to Gander 138 kV corridor

The 2026 capital filing says the original transmission line directly connected Sunnyside and Gander. The corridor was later divided into 100L, 124L, 121L and 146L. The same filing dates all four sections to 1964.

### 100L

- Sunnyside to Clarenville.
- 138 kV radial H-frame line.
- 34.13 km total length.
- Approximately 148 H-frame structures.
- 397.5 ACSR conductor.
- The 2 km section connecting the line to Clarenville Substation was built in 1975.

### 124L and 121L

The 2022 filing describes 124L as the 138 kV H-frame line between Clarenville and Gambo before the 2022 reconfiguration:

- 86.1 km total length.
- Approximately 79 km of 1964 construction and 7.2 km built in 1986.
- Primarily 397.5 ACSR conductor.
- 22.3 km had been rebuilt since 2001.
- 30.0 km between Port Blandford and Terra Nova was rebuilt during 2021.
- The 2022 project addressed the remaining 26.7 km of the 1964 section and extended 124L into Glovertown.

Before that work, 121L was an approximately 5.3 km radial tap from Alexander Bay to Glovertown. The current line designations use 121L between Gambo and Glovertown and 124L between Glovertown and Clarenville. The 2026 construction table dates both original corridor sections to 1964.

## LET-01 submarine cable

The 2012 filing records:

- Approximately 3 km.
- Two-conductor single-phase cable.
- Laid directly on the sea floor.
- Installed in 1964.
- Ungrounded.
- Original LET-01 configuration was delta with no neutral.
- After LET-01 was converted to grounded wye, two back-to-back transformers near the Charlottetown landing kept the submarine conductors ungrounded.

## LEW-02

The 2006 feeder study identifies LEW-02 as a 25 kV feeder. It was originally constructed in 1964 with number 2 and number 4 ACSR. Sections at Loon Bay, Baytona and Birchy Bay still retained 1964 blackjack creosote poles and original insulators when the filing was prepared. Additional phases were extended beyond Michael's Harbour around 1980.

## Original Gambo to Glovertown feeder

The Glovertown feeder began in 1964 as a single-phase line from Gambo. Two additional phases were added when Glovertown Substation was built in 1976. The 2006 study identifies the later GLV-02 arrangement as a 25 kV feeder.

## Seal Cove and Tors Cove

- Seal Cove G1 received a replacement turbine runner in 1964. The original runner had been in service since 1924.
- One of the English Electric generators commissioned at Tors Cove in 1940 was refurbished by Westinghouse in 1964.

## Bay d'Espoir construction support

The Bear Brook water system at Camp Boggy was constructed in 1964 during the start of Bay d'Espoir development. It comprised a raw-water intake, a sump under the pump house, duplex pumps, single-point chlorination, a woodstave elevated reservoir and a distribution system. It supplied potable and fire-protection water to site facilities.

The line depot was also built in 1964 to support generating-station construction. The 2027 filing describes its later role as the operating base for transmission and distribution work on the Connaigre Peninsula.

## Wiltondale transformer

The power transformer later used at Wiltondale Terminal Station was manufactured in 1964. During the 2013 station rebuild it was moved to a new concrete pad. The filing says operating conditions and test results did not justify replacement.
