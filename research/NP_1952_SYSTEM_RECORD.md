# 1952 System Record

## Transmission line 21L

Newfoundland Power dates the original 21L construction to 1952. The 66 kV H-frame line connected the Horse Chops plant to the 20L tap near the Southern Shore Highway. It used 36 two-pole and three-pole structures and 266.8 ACSR conductor.

The filed length is inconsistent. The 2011 Capital Budget Application gives 5.73 km. The 2012 Capital Budget Application gives 5.3 km. Both figures are retained.

## Transmission line 18L

The construction year is unresolved. Newfoundland Power's rebuild-strategy tables list 18L between Goulds and Glendale as 1952. The detailed 2014 engineering report says the original 66 kV line from Goulds to Stamp's Lane was constructed in 1951. That report records 4.62 km and 64 original structures remaining between Brookfield Road and Goulds, plus a separate 1.19 km, 22-structure reroute built for Glendale in 1977.

Sources: [2008 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008Capital/files/applic/NP2008ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 194, SHA-256 `0982f4e3c88c5967ccb014c2299758e56b2029abe316da0313098de946820bf2`; [2014 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2014/NP2014Capital/files/applic/NP2014ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 308 and 310, SHA-256 `da133a107fc706c45e36d4e0f30b2156855a7b247f468aec02a23d1fad19566c`.

## Cape Broyle and Horse Chops

The generation dates conflict across three Newfoundland Power records. CA-NP-021 lists CAB-G at 5.6 MW and HCP-G1 at 7.7 MW, both with 1952 installation dates. The 2013 hydro-development record dates both developments and turbines to 1953. The 2025 capital filing dates both plants to 1954.

The Cape Broyle equipment record independently dates the 78 inch turbine inlet valve and its drain and bypass valves to 1952. By 2004, erosion prevented positive water shutoff and sometimes allowed the turbine to continue turning during shutdown.

Sources: [CA-NP-021](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-021.pdf), physical PDF pp. 1 to 2, SHA-256 `66c059cf61ee7a0230fcee4d07c1580c204829b43c3d6fa33ab17cb0c278c284`; [2005 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2005cap/files/applic/np2005capApplicComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 163, SHA-256 `49c9a494d639cef911d3f868a94d3584e0e3192b727710794da82c1cf9d807ae`.

## Stamp's Lane Substation

The construction year is unresolved. NLH-30 NP lists Stamp's Lane with a 1952 construction year. The 2007 and 2011 station reports say the 66 kV switching and 4.16/12.5 kV distribution station was built in 1963.

Sources: [NLH-30 NP](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/rfi/NLH-30NP.pdf), physical PDF p. 1, SHA-256 `fb0be1d468a43429eecdbb343ffa0a06a456179b3c0b01e665bd40b395e81bc8`; [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 297, SHA-256 `7494209347c908d48dc7e2b4a458a46ce39d5395ef43b5737ed93119a9785061`.
