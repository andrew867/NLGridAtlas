# 1888 System Record

## St. John's lighting expansion

The *Encyclopedia of Newfoundland and Labrador* reports four related 1888 developments:

- St. John's City Council awarded the St. John's Electric Light Company a street-lighting contract;
- the company purchased Edison Swan Company equipment from Manchester for smaller 16 candlepower incandescent lamps;
- the colonial government requested electric lighting for public buildings, including Government House and the Colonial Building; and
- the company began installing electric lights in private homes.

The source does not provide the contract date, lamp count, equipment ratings or completed installation count. The atlas records the developments as reported expansion, not as a fully specified as-built record.

## Council lighting recommendation

A later historical study reports that the new municipal council appointed a committee on October 11 to consider additional street lighting. On October 17, the committee recommended nine new gas lamps and nine new electric lamps. The study gives an annual supply price of $130 for each electric lamp. It does not state that all nine recommended electric lamps were installed during 1888.

## Proposed capital increase

The *Evening Mercury* advertised a December 7 special meeting of St. John's Electric Light Company shareholders. The meeting was to consider increasing capital stock to $43,000 for business expansion. The notice does not report the meeting result, an approved capital amount or physical work.
