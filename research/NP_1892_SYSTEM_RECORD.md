# 1892 System Record

## Flavin Lane replacement station

The Great Fire of July 8 destroyed the St. John's Electric Light Company station. Replacement work began on July 15. The September 28 report describes a completed one-storey building 70 ft square and 18 ft high, with a 60 ft brick chimney still under construction.

The replacement plant contained:

- two dynamos, each stated for 50 arc lights at 950 r/min;
- one alternating-current dynamo stated for 1,200 incandescent lights at 1,000 r/min;
- two 100 hp Leonard-Ball tandem-compound engines at 275 r/min;
- two 70 hp boilers;
- an exciter for the alternating-current dynamo;
- an arc switchboard with wall controllers, lightning arresters and ammeters;
- alternating-current instruments including a rheostat, potential indicator, potential transformer and current indicator; and
- two pole-mounted transformers described as receiving 1,000 V and supplying 52 V to the outgoing wires.

The two arc-light dynamos were driven by one engine. The alternating-current dynamo and exciter were driven by the other engine. The arc-light system was operating on September 28. Fifteen arc lights remained unavailable because poles and wires had not been replaced in the burnt district. Incandescent operation was expected the following week, so it is not recorded as operating on September 28.
