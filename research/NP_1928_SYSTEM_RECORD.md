# 1928 System Search

## Exploits River gauging record

Hydro's response to NP-308 in the 2001 General Rate Application says the Exploits River gauging record used to develop inflow records starts in 1928. The response lists it with the gauged streams used for the Bay d'Espoir, Hinds Lake and Cat Arm developments.

This is a hydrologic data-series date. It does not establish construction or commissioning of a plant, generating unit, dam, transmission line or station in 1928.

Source: [NP-308](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/filings/hydresp/NFpower/NP%20308.pdf), physical PDF p. 1, lines 5 to 24, SHA-256 `6a537be51ff1d6b9eebf6d73026a9c4d64a045c2a23cc34c3fe3940cc67e8561`.

The same response is also present in [Hydro filing package 25](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/filings/Oct3/Hydro/pkg25.pdf), physical PDF p. 10, SHA-256 `273ba8408b7c1f8aeac99d39c97aea5dec6946650972f29f3117c69badc0d8f7`.

## Remaining archive results

Exact-year searches covered plants, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, transmission, hydro civil works, property, telecommunications, load, reliability, storms and outages.

The other returned records were:

- Newfoundland Power depreciation placement bands and accounting rows without a named asset.
- Generating-station records from utilities outside Newfoundland and Labrador.
- Modern clock times, financial values, model results and spreadsheet serial dates containing the digits 1928.
- References to the Canadian Geodetic Vertical Datum of 1928.

None establishes a separate Newfoundland electrical-system construction or operating event in 1928. The atlas therefore has no separate 1928 public card or timeline entry.

## Search rule

The hydrologic series is retained in this research record because it affects the evidence behind later hydraulic-generation studies. It is not presented as an asset date. A later exact-year primary record can reopen this queue.
