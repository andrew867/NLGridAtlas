## Archive priority

1. Mirror Newfoundland Power regulatory PDFs. Its Regulation page currently exposes the 2024 to 2027 capital applications and the 2025 to 2026 general rate application. These are the best direct source for the remaining distribution-station records.
2. Mirror the NL Hydro uploads index. The inventory contains 2,521 PDFs and 9,402,399,683 listed bytes. Use it for Hydro transmission, generating-station, HVDC, telecommunications, and Muskrat Falls integration records.
3. Search Fortis annual reports selectively. They record corporate-level asset additions and transfers, but the current Fortis report library does not expose Newfoundland Power station drawings or capital-application appendices.

## Pulpit Rock voltage record

- [x] The 2025 application says PUL-T1 and the then-installed PUL-T2 supplied five 12.5 kV feeders, physical PDF pp. 458 and 463.
- [x] The then-installed PUL-T2 was a 15/20/25 MVA, 66/12.5/25 kV dual-secondary unit. The main project summary describes its service as 66/12.5 kV, physical PDF pp. 458 and 463.
- [x] The replacement specification was 15/20/25 MVA, 66/12.5/25 kV, with installation, testing, and commissioning planned for 2026, physical PDF pp. 88 and 470.
- [x] The 2026 and 2027 transformer listings show PUL-T1 and PUL-T2 at 66/25 kV, 25 MVA, physical PDF pp. 370 and 549.
- [x] The 2026 application scheduled the new PUL-T2 to arrive in the third quarter of 2026, physical PDF p. 197.
- [ ] The available record does not show the completed secondary connection or a feeder conversion from 12.5 kV to 25 kV. A commissioning report or as-built one-line is required before the atlas labels the Pulpit Rock feeder bus as 25 kV.

## Health Sciences Centre supply

- [x] The 2014 capital filing records a rebuild of 12L and identifies Memorial University, the Health Sciences Centre, and the Janeway among the facilities served by the two circuits, physical PDF p. 309.
- [x] The Memorial single-line identifies MUN-01 and MUN-08 as serving the Health Sciences Complex and Utilities Annex, CA-NP-158, physical PDF p. 3.
- [x] The Long Pond single-line shows HSC #2 and HSC #5 circuits on Memorial-owned 12.5 kV switchgear, CA-NP-158, physical PDF p. 5.
- [x] Newfoundland Power describes Memorial Substation as the University's primary supply point. It describes Long Pond as the second supply point and says some medical load can be restored through Memorial after a Long Pond outage, PUB-NP-013, physical PDF pp. 2 to 3; CA-NP-162, physical PDF p. 1.
- [x] Oxen Pond supplies Long Pond through 36L. No source found places the Health Sciences Complex on the OXP-01 distribution feeder.

## Remaining data classes

- [ ] Current circuit register. The 2005 handbook is a historical baseline, not a current network inventory.
- [ ] Current feeder register. Later capital filings prove additions such as PUL-04, PUL-05, KEN-01, and HWD-09 that are absent from the 2005 list.
- [ ] Feeder map sheets beyond the 34 extracted Hardwoods, Kenmount, Oxen Pond, Goulds, Glendale, St. John's Main, and Stamp's Lane sheets. HWD-08 is listed in the feeder register, but no corresponding extracted sheet is present.
- [ ] Station one-lines that assign feeders to transformer banks and bus sections.
- [ ] Commissioning and as-built records for replacement transformers.
- [ ] Protection drawings that identify normally open bus ties and alternate supply paths.
- [ ] Geographic coordinates for stations and line routes. Existing system maps are schematic or low-resolution.
