# 1931 System Search

## Pierre's Brook hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power places Pierre's Brook Plant in service in 1931. The 2014 capital application gives one generating unit with a 3.4 MW nameplate rating, 80 m rated net head and approximately 24.4 GWh normal annual production. It says the development was built to supply the Bell Island iron-ore mines.

The embedded AMEC penstock report describes the development as a single-unit 4.3 MW plant operating under about 278 ft head. The same inspection found that the riveted-steel penstock, foundations, cradles and anchor block were consistent with original 1931 construction. Although 1959 drawings showed replacement structures, the observed works did not match those drawings. AMEC concluded that the drawn replacement was not constructed.

A 2015 electrical report identifies a 4,000 kVA generator at 80 percent power factor, equal to a 3,200 kW load rating. It reports 4,100 kW peak output after the 1995 runner and 1996 stator-winding replacements. These values use different labels and dates. The atlas retains each label instead of treating them as one rating.

## Property record

Newfoundland Power's property schedule lists three Pierre's Brook acquisitions in 1931:

- August 3 from Henry Lash.
- August 28 from Thomas Cotton.
- October 7 from Michael Vickers.
