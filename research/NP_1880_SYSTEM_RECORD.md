# 1880 System Record

## Telegraph appropriations

The 1880 Supply Act appropriated $4,603 to pay interest on telegraph lines from the Fishery Award. It also appropriated $7,000 for the extension and maintenance of a new telegraph line.

The 1880 Indemnity Act separately covered $1,945.83 already spent on telegraph interest and maintenance. This was an indemnity for expenditure, not an additional statement of completed line construction.

The statutes do not identify a route, endpoint, line length, conductor, office equipment or completion date. They do not connect the appropriations to the Coon River to Harbor Breton branch reported as completed in 1882.
