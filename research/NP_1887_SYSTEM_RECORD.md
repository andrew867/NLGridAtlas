# 1887 System Record

## Newfoundland Graving Dock electric-light rate

The official 1887 Year Book lists the Newfoundland Graving Dock at River Head, St. John's. Its dockage schedule states that electric lights were furnished for night work at $3 per hour per light.

The tariff establishes that electric lighting was available for dock work. It does not identify the lamp owner, generating source, supply circuit, equipment rating, installation date or a new 1887 system change.
