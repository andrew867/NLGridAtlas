# 1900 System Record

## Petty Harbour hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power's detailed plant record says the original Petty Harbour development was constructed in 1900 and the first horizontal Francis turbine-generator set was commissioned that year. The other historical sets were commissioned in 1908 and 1926.

The 2010 filing gives about 5,300 kW total installed capacity for the three sets under 57.9 m net head and approximately 15.87 GWh normal annual production. Those figures describe the three-unit plant in 2010; they are not assigned to the single 1900 machine.

Source: [2010 Capital Budget Application, Petty Harbour Hydro Plant Refurbishment](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 207.
