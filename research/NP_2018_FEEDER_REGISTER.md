# Newfoundland Power distribution feeder register

Source: PUB-NP-081 Attachment B  
Source pages: physical PDF pp. 10 to 20  
Planning period: 2024 to 2025 winter

## Result

Attachment B contains 300 unique feeder records: 108 in the St. John's region, 87 in the Eastern region, and 105 in the Western region.

Each record gives operating voltage, planning capacity, winter capacity, forecast winter peak, and forecast loading against planning capacity. The published percentage equals forecast winter peak divided by planning capacity for all 300 rows within 0.02 percentage point, allowing for displayed rounding.

These are planning values, not measured feeder peaks. The atlas keeps the register separate from the June 2005 feeder list and from actual-load records.

The highest published ratio is VIR-07 at 99.21 percent, using a 12.6 MVA forecast and 12.7 MVA planning capacity on physical PDF p. 13.
