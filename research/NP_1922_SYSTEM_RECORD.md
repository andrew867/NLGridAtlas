# 1922 System Search

## Seal Cove unit-year conflict

A Statistics Canada generating-station table filed by Hydro lists Newfoundland Light & Power's Seal Cove plant with two units, years 1922 to 1927 and 3,150 kW plant capacity. The range does not assign a commissioning year to either unit.

Newfoundland Power's detailed development record instead says the two-unit Seal Cove development was commissioned in 1924. Its 2013 equipment response also lists both SCV-G1 and SCV-G2 with 1924 installation dates.

The evidence therefore does not establish a 1922 Seal Cove commissioning event. The atlas retains 1924 for the development and keeps the 1922 to 1927 range as a source conflict.
