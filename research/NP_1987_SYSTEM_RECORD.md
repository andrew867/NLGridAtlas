# Newfoundland Power 1987 system record

## Annual indicators

- The comparative reliability table reports SAIFI of 4.05 interruptions per customer, SAIDI of 6.32 hours per customer, and CAIDI of 1.56 hours per interruption. [Information-17, physical PDF p. 11](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008GRA/files/information/Information-17.pdf)
- The historical capital table reports actual capital expenditure of $38,881,000. [CA-NP-146 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 9](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2016/NP2016GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-146,%20Attachment%20B.pdf)
- The accessible 1998 ten-year operating summary begins with 1989. It does not establish 1987 generation, purchases, sales, peak demand, or customer totals.

## Transmission and telecommunications

- TL250 is a 123.231 km, 138 kV H-frame wood line between Bottom Brook and Grandy Brook. TL255 is a 33.014 km line of the same voltage and construction between Grandy Brook and Hope Brook. Both were built in 1987. Each line is listed at 89.1 MVA at 30 C, 104.9 MVA at 25 C, 130.8 MVA at 15 C, 145.2 MVA at 7 C, and 161.7 MVA at 0 C. [PUB-NLH-316, physical PDF p. 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NLH-316.pdf)
- The 2003 telecommunications inventory dates the TL250 and TL255 powerline-carrier links to 1987. [2003 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 41](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd2003B/files/applic/AppSectionH.pdf)
- A later TL217 history records a major 1987 failure in the central section of the 84 km, 230 kV Buchans to Massey Drive line. Seven structures failed in cascade and a conductor broke. The outage lasted 63 days and cost approximately $500,000. [Exhibit 88, physical PDF p. 3](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/exhibits/Exhibit88.pdf)

## Stations and generation

- Newfoundland Power extended the Glendale 66 kV high-voltage bus in 1987. The register dates the original 66 kV structure and bus to 1976. [CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 191](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-088.pdf)
- West Brook completed major intake rehabilitation in 1987. The work repaired the intake foundation concrete and replaced the intake gate. [PUB-NP-001, physical PDF p. 1](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/rfi/PUB-NP-001.pdf)
- West Brook also replaced its 1,219 mm woodstave penstock with a 536 m long, 1,372 mm diameter buried fibre-reinforced plastic penstock in 1987. [CA-NP-036, physical PDF p. 282](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf)
- Rattling Brook replaced the Unit 1 turbine runner with a new Allis-Chalmers runner in 1987. The work repaired shaft pitting and the stainless-steel gland sleeve. Cavitated scroll-case head-cover and discharge-ring surfaces were sandblasted and rebuilt with Mazel molecular metal. The plant also installed water-level probes and transducers at the forebay and Amy's Gate. [2007 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 383 and 392](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2007cap/files/applic/NP2007ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Port Union replaced the diesel unit's off-engine controls, instrumentation, and unit breaker in 1987 as part of plant switchgear replacement. [PUB-NP-175, physical PDF p. 176](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-175.pdf)
- The drawing register identifies 791D-17082 as an as-built record for 1987 emergency repairs to the north side of Goodyear's Dam. The register does not describe the repair scope. [PUB-NLH-005, physical PDF p. 719](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/rfis/PUB-NLH-005.PDF)

## Conflicting Holyrood dates

- A 2011 condition study states that Units 1 and 2 were both modified in 1987 from 150 MW to 175 MW. Unit-specific capital histories separately date the Unit 1 uprate to 1988 and the Unit 2 uprate to 1989. The atlas retains all three records and marks the combined 1987 date unresolved. [Holyrood Condition Assessment and Life Extension Study, physical PDF p. 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2012Capital/files/reports/AMEC-Report-Jan28-11.pdf)
- Two consecutive pages in one gas-turbine condition report disagree on when the Gem 80/500 programmable logic controller replaced the relay logic. Physical PDF p. 66 states 1987. Physical PDF p. 67 states 1986. The atlas marks the year unresolved. [NP-NLH-022 Attachment 1, physical PDF pp. 66 to 67](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLHCBSUPP2013/HolyroodDieselPU33/files/rfi/NP-NLH-022.PDF)
