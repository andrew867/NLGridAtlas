# 1876 System Record

## Northern communications gap

The Postmaster General's report for 1875, dated February 21, 1876, states that northern service remained constrained by the absence of telegraph communication. It recommended more frequent mail between the north side of Trinity Bay and the south side of Bonavista Bay. It also proposed a steamship mail connection for Twillingate and Fogo through the Tiger service.

The report records an operating communications gap and proposed mail measures. It does not establish that a telegraph line entered construction in 1876.

## Cape St. John and northern telegraph proposals

A March 8 petition proposed the Cape St. John Telegraph Company. Its main electric telegraph would run from the Anglo-American station at Conn River through Hall's Bay to Cape St. John. The petition linked the route to the northern and Labrador trade, fisheries, and the Tilt Cove and Bett's Cove mining districts. It requested a charter and a £500 sterling annual guarantee on company shares.

The joint committee recommended a charter under the terms of a December 23, 1875 letter. It proposed a £500 annual interest guarantee while the line operated, secured by a first charge on the Tilt Cove and Bett's Cove mining properties. The report also discussed a possible Heart's Content to Bonavista connection through intermediate settlements, at a probable cost of about $25,000.

On April 25, the House asked the Governor to use the colony's available means to connect Twillingate and Fogo, Bonavista Bay and Trinity Bay with the capital. The Legislative Council concurred on April 26. These records establish petitions, committee recommendations and legislative addresses, not construction or commissioning.
