# 1946 System Record

## Lookout Brook Hydro Plant

Newfoundland Power dates the original Lookout Brook plant, generating units G1 and G2, and the original powerhouse building to 1946. G3 was added in 1958. G1 and G2 were replaced by G4 in 1984.

Source: [2024 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2024/NP2024Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202024%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202023-06-22.PDF), physical PDF pp. 112, 438 and 440. SHA-256 `9eb1f103128578345b75b1cc058149494a73b37310d8a03838ddba53ed9a8056`.

## Port aux Basques

Newfoundland Power dates Port aux Basques Substation construction to 1946. The station later served both distribution and local diesel generation. A depreciation schedule assigns $9,670 of Port aux Basques Diesel electrical-plant original cost to 1946.

Sources: [2011 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2011/NP2011Capital/files/applic/NP2011ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 292, SHA-256 `7494209347c908d48dc7e2b4a458a46ce39d5395ef43b5737ed93119a9785061`; [2003 General Rate Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/Application/CompleteApplicationRev1.pdf), physical PDF p. 1049, SHA-256 `f927aa2c20da8ba4275708b1c0afeec77b14b2d747917d4f09e15400783c4a52`.

## Heart's Content development

The development record says the 1918 Heart's Content hydroelectric development was extensively renovated in 1946. It describes a 558 m long, 1,829 mm diameter woodstave penstock section constructed in 1946. The 2013 capital filing also dates the reinforced-concrete intake to 1946 and describes its wingwalls, timber gates, stop logs, trashracks, controls and gatehouse.

A separate asset-ledger record dates the existing penstock to 1965. The atlas retains both dates because the 1946 civil description and the 1965 ledger record may represent different accounting or physical scopes.

Sources: [CA-NP-036](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf), physical PDF p. 160, SHA-256 `1b27d6b0a58416c06045f8e54618c33b2902f26dda83d53780a4b01b5e14b477`; [2013 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013Capital/files/applic/NP2013ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF pp. 180 to 181, SHA-256 `4e304493e29bfa99d35eb0dfb01673691c0f6ae10b0b19b6d5c152bf0f130984`.

## Fenelons Pond Dam

The Board's 2004 order dates the Fenelons Pond Dam in the Seal Cove hydroelectric system to 1946. The order identifies an earthfill embankment, spillway and flow-control structures.

Source: [Order P.U. 43(2004)](http://www.pub.nl.ca/PU/orders/2004/pu43-2004.pdf), physical PDF p. 7. SHA-256 `00900dcc8dad86eb655f123eacb8eb03bd7654ef1ae413c8e38b0171245e6a80`.

## Mobile River watershed rights

Newfoundland Power held rights to use the Mobile River watershed for electricity generation under a lease dated November 23, 1946. The lease was amended on October 21, 1949.

Source: [CA-NP-498](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-498.pdf), physical PDF p. 1. SHA-256 `ec26da9b27174191ca0d2bebf814b54a365df11ed31bcb9a1242d2b6316964fe`.
