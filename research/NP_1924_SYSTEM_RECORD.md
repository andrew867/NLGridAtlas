# 1924 System Search

## Seal Cove hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power's detailed development record dates Seal Cove to 1924. It describes two generating units in a concrete powerhouse, a 1,220 m long by 2,134 mm diameter above-ground treated wood-stave penstock, a concrete intake, and storage at White Hill Pond forebay, Fenelons Pond and Soldiers Pond. That record gives 3,400 kW combined turbine output.

A 2015 capital filing says G1 was manufactured by Allis Chalmers in 1924. It says the stator was rewound in 1988 while the rotor windings remained original before the filed 2015 rewind.

Later sources do not publish one stable capacity for the plant. Examples include 3,180 kW in CA-NP-088, 3,300 kW in the 2015 Capital Budget Application and 3,580 kW maximum rated demand capacity in CA-NP-022. The commissioning year and two-unit arrangement are consistent; each capacity remains attached to its dated source.

## Petty Harbour water rights and unit table

The 2020 Capital Budget Application cites a February 18, 1924 deed from Reid Newfoundland Company Limited and others to Newfoundland Light and Power Company Limited. The deed granted the Petty Harbour water rights perpetually.

The 2013 selected-generating-unit table lists PHR-G3 at 1.8 MW with a 1924 installation date. A separate historical plant record says one of the three original turbine-generator sets was commissioned in 1926. The filings do not establish that the 1926 historical machine became present unit PHR-G3, so the records remain separate.
