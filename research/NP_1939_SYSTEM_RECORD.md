# 1939 System Record

## Fall Pond generation

Newfoundland Power's development assessment dates Fall Pond commissioning to 1939. It gives a development capacity of 400 kW under about 15.2 m net head and identifies one 380 kW horizontal Francis turbine manufactured by Voith and installed in 1939, with a Westinghouse generator.

The capacity is not consistent across the filings. The 2010 depreciation study and the generating-unit register give 350 kW, while the development assessment gives 400 kW for the development and 380 kW for the turbine. The atlas retains all three values with their source dates.

## Waterworks

The assessment records a 13 m long, 1,219 mm diameter woodstave penstock supported above ground on concrete cradles. The forebay structure is a concrete buttress dam approximately 129 m long and 11 m high. Its 33 m overflow spillway, plant intake and woodstave unwatering conduit are integrated into the dam.

The assessment also describes abandoned storage structures at Beaver Pond, Lundrigan's Pond and Rocky Pond. It does not assign their construction to 1939, so they are not presented as 1939 events.

Source: [CA-NP-036](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf), physical PDF pp. 275 to 276, SHA-256 `1b27d6b0a58416c06045f8e54618c33b2902f26dda83d53780a4b01b5e14b477`.

## Station and connection

The development assessment describes a transformer station stepping generation voltage from 2,400 V to 12.5 kV. It was tied to a distribution feeder from Laurentian Substation and had no separate transmission line.

This is a historical arrangement. The current transformer register lists three 12.5/4.2 kV, 0.25 MVA generation transformers, so the 2.4 kV record is not applied to the current one-line.
