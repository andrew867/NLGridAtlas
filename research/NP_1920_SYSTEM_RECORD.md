# 1920 System Search

## Unidentified utility plant placements

Newfoundland Power's filed depreciation schedules retain three original-cost placements dated 1920:

- $2,400 in account 323.00, Canals, Penstocks, Surge Tanks and Tailraces.
- $13,548 in account 324.00, Dams and Reservoirs.
- $885 in account 341.00, Substation Buildings and Structures.

The schedules do not identify the plants, dams, substations or locations. The values therefore remain accounting evidence and are not assigned to named atlas assets.

Source: [CA-NP-017](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-017.pdf), physical PDF pp. 555, 557 and 576, local file `mirror/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-017.pdf`, SHA-256 `2fc6689b19530846bda9db06bf41f239c4734492bbca09068fd92aba392e58f4`.
