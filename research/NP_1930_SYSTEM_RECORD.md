# 1930 System Search

## Transmission line 3L

Newfoundland Power dates 3L between Goulds and Petty Harbour to 1930. The 2004 capital filing describes it as a radial line serving 400 Petty Harbour-area customers and connecting the hydro plant to the main grid. It records several unplanned outages during the previous four or five years from deteriorated line components. The filed project covered 4.7 km at a cost of $364,000.

Source: [2004 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np2004cap/files/applic/ApplicCompleteRev1.pdf), physical PDF p. 388, SHA-256 `0de7e09f300f69a48fee555483bd94860b62901fc04094272e5ff3799fc74f4b`.

## Lawn Forebay Dam and intake

Newfoundland Power dates the original Lawn Forebay Dam and timber-crib intake to 1930. The intake was completely rebuilt in concrete in the 1950s. The dam received concrete repairs in 1983 and spillway and downstream-rockfill work in 1995. The intake gate and its concrete support structure were repaired in 1995.

Source: [PUB-NP-001](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/rfi/PUB-NP-001.pdf), physical PDF p. 2, SHA-256 `c9a6a807983ac65d2435aad16ce45441395f4cbcd486b929a2d712538c0729c4`.

## Pierre's Brook equipment and property

The 2015 electrical assessment dates the original Pierre's Brook generator exciter to 1930. It says the exciter was refurbished in 1994, its brushgear was replaced in 2004, and it was in good condition at the time of the assessment.

The property schedule records two Pierre's Brook acquisitions in 1930, one from J. William Morris on July 15 and one from Thomas Cotton in August. The scan does not make the August day legible. It also records the Broad Cove Substation parcel acquired from Samuel Tucker on October 14, 1930.

## Grand Falls hydro development

A 2017 engineering report says the Grand Falls hydro development added a generating unit in 1930. It does not identify the unit number. The same report says original Units 1 to 3 were removed in 2002 and lists the remaining units separately, so the atlas does not assign the 1930 date to a surviving unit.

Source: [PUB-NLH-005 Attachment 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/NLH2018ReliabilityAdequacy/rfis/PUB-NLH-005.PDF), physical PDF pp. 606 and 610, SHA-256 `7295b73cf565c32382d53881a6e3e6e655d42a1c7c7d9d74665fc8d096097f02`.
