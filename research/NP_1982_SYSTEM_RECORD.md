# Newfoundland Power and Island system record, 1982

## Demand and planning

- Newfoundland Power peak demand was 731.8 MW. Island transmission peak demand was 1,083 MW and Hydro system peak demand was 880 MW. The table has no separate Hydro Rural value for 1982. [MHI-Nalcor-92, physical PDF p. 2](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/MuskratFalls2011/files/rfi/MHI-Nalcor-92.pdf)
- The 1981 supply-planning case forecast a 1,309 MW coincident peak, 1,594 MW installed capability, 0.06 loss-of-load-probability index, 6,764 GWh energy requirement, 6,911 GWh firm capability, and a 147 GWh surplus. [CA-295, physical PDF p. 8](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/nfpower03/rfi/CA-295.pdf)

The located comparative Newfoundland Power capital table starts in 1984. The located comparative reliability table starts in 1987. No supported 1982 Newfoundland Power capital, SAIFI, SAIDI, energy-sales, or customer-account total was located.

## Transmission and terminal expansion

- The contemporary Board report records $6.6 million in 1982 transmission and terminal expansion expenditures. It separately identifies the completed 27 km, 230 kV Holyrood to Hardwoods route at a project cost of $3.8 million, 74.6 km of 138 kV facilities from Deer Lake to Rocky Harbour at a project cost of $7.6 million, and $2.4 million in terminal-plant expansion expenditure. [1983 Electric Power Control Act report, physical PDF pp. 24 to 25](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/Consents/83PMARCH.pdf)
- Later filings identify the Holyrood to Hardwoods circuit as TL242. The 1982 asset register dates the associated Holyrood breaker B13B15, buswork and steel supports, control and protection equipment, current transformers, foundations, grounding, and lighting to October 19, 1982. [IC-NLH-097, physical PDF pp. 3 to 5](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NLH2013GRA/files/rfi/IC-NLH-097.PDF)
- TL239 is a 138 kV line commissioned in 1982. It has 610 wood-pole structures and shares an approximately 71 km right-of-way with TL226 between Deer Lake and Berry Hill through Gros Morne National Park. [TL226 and TL239 Re-Routing Application, physical PDF p. 10](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/NLH2018Capital/NLH2018Capital_SUPP_TL226TL239/applications/From%20NLH%20-%20Application%20-%20Approval%20to%20Re-Route%20Transmission%20Lines%20TL%20226%20and%20TL%20239%20-%202018-10-12.PDF)
- TL253 is a 12 km, 69 kV radial wood-pole line from Jackson's Arm to Coney Arm. It was built in 1982 to supply distribution systems in the area. [2008 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 116](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2008Capital/files/application/NLH2008ApplicationComplete.pdf)
- Newfoundland Power rebuilt 300L from Linton Lake to Marystown and 308L from Marystown to Salt Pond. [PUB 10-2A, physical PDF p. 4](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/np03cap/files/rfi/PUB%20%2010-2A.pdf)

## Hinds Lake

The power-canal dyke breached on January 30, 1982. Hinds Lake was shut down for three months and the repairs cost $2.0 million. [1983 Electric Power Control Act report, physical PDF p. 25](http://www.pub.nl.ca/ARCHIVE/hyd01gra/Consents/83PMARCH.pdf)

## Station protection and DC equipment

- Bayview's control building and 18 electromechanical protection relays dated from 1982. Four panels protected BVS-T1, the 66 kV bus, and transmission lines 357L, 358L, and 359L. The 2018 filing proposed replacing them with four digital relays and a new control building. [2018 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 233 to 234](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2018/NP2018Capital/applications/From%20NP%20-%202018%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202017-07-07.PDF)
- Salt Pond's transformer-protection relays were installed in 1982. The 2017 filing proposed replacing ten bus and transformer electromechanical relays with two digital relays and constructing a new control building. [2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 231 to 232](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2017/NP2017Capital/files/applications/FromNP-2017CapitalBudgetApplication-2016-07-19.pdf)
- Berry Hill's 48 V, 35 A Saft Nife station charger was installed in 1982. [2012 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 725](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NLH2012Capital/files/application/NLH2012ApplicationComplete-VolumeII.pdf)
