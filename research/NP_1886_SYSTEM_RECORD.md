# 1886 System Record

## Government street-light contract

A historical study reports that the St. John's Electric Light Company obtained a government contract for 25 electric street lamps in 1886. The annual price was $200 for each lamp. The electric lamps replaced 35 gas lamps. The arrangement continued until municipal government began in 1888.

The study cites two 1886 letters in the Provincial Archives, Herbert Burchell to Maurice Fenelon on August 28 and November 6. Digitized copies of those letters were not located.

## Expansion and operating condition

The *Colonist* reported on October 5 that additional electric lights were to be erected throughout the city. The notice gives no count, locations or completion date.

On November 20, the same newspaper reported that Mr. Dunn and his staff were examining the electric lights after poor light during the previous few nights. The report establishes an operating problem and an inspection, but gives no cause, equipment identity or repair result.
