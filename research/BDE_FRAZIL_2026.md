# January 2026 Bay d'Espoir frazil-ice event

The April 30, 2026 Hydro report documents the first complete frazil-ice shutdown in Bay d'Espoir's 60-year operating history.

## Plant sequence

Frazil ice accumulated on the intake trash racks for all four penstocks, which serve the seven generating units. Physical PDF p. 13 records the following January 21 sequence.

- Units 5 and 6 were reduced to 20 MW at 11:30, then shut down at 12:25 and 12:30.
- Unit 7 was reduced to 50 MW at about 13:30.
- Units 1 to 4 were reduced to 20 MW as trash-rack differential values increased.
- Unit 7 was placed in synchronous-condenser mode before its final removal.
- By 18:00, all seven units were out of service and reserve was approximately 250 MW.

Physical PDF p. 14 reports approximately 360 MW of reserve when the Power Warning was issued on January 24. The system response included standby generation, Maritime Link imports, increased Labrador-Island Link loading, industrial curtailment and capacity assistance.

## Return to service

Physical PDF p. 15 states that Units 1 to 6 were initially loaded to 20 MW on January 25 to flush ice safely from the tailrace and preserve upstream ice cover. Appendix A, physical PDF p. 32, gives the later release times and capacities.

| Time | Record |
| --- | --- |
| January 25, 15:42 | Units 1 to 6 released for full service, 6 x 76.5 MW |
| January 31, 10:33 | Unit 7 released, available at 154.4 MW |

The report says Units 1 to 6 returned after intake ice removal. Unit 7 remained unavailable while the damaged Intake 4 gate-hoist mechanism was repaired and ice was removed.
