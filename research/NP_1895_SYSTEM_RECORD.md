# 1895 System Record

## Reid electrical tramway notice

Brian Wadden's 1965 street-railway history says the Reid Newfoundland Company published a notice daily for a full month beginning in September 1895. The notice sought permission to construct and maintain a single or double railway in St. John's and adjacent areas, operated by electricity or another authorized motive power.

The notice establishes a proposal. It does not establish an incorporated company, an approved charter, construction or operation. The legislature passed an incorporation bill in 1896, but the Governor withheld assent pending Her Majesty's pleasure.
