# 1908 System Record

## Petty Harbour generating-unit addition

Newfoundland Power's detailed Petty Harbour development record says the original plant was built in 1900 and contained three horizontal Francis turbines and generators. It dates the first unit to 1900 and the other units to 1908 and 1926. The three units had about 5,300 kW aggregate installed capacity under 57.9 m net head.

The source does not identify the 1908 machine with a present unit number. A later selected-unit table lists PHR-G1 as 1910, PHR-G2 as 1986 and PHR-G3 as 1924. The atlas therefore records the 1908 addition as a historical plant unit without assigning it to PHR-G1, PHR-G2 or PHR-G3.

Source: [2010 Capital Budget Application](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2010/NP2010Capital/files/applic/NP2010ApplicationComplete.pdf), physical PDF p. 207.
