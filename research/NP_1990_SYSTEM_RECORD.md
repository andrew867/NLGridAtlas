# Newfoundland Power 1990 system record

## Reliability and capital

CA-NP-004 Attachment A, physical PDF p. 4, reports 1990 SAIFI of 2.46 interruptions per customer, SAIDI of 4.55 hours per customer, and capital expenditure of $67.074 million. The five-year rolling averages were 2.47 interruptions and 3.78 hours per customer.

## Energy, demand, and customers

The 1998 annual report's ten-year summary, physical PDF p. 29, records 4,008 GWh purchased, 411 GWh generated, 4,419 GWh total supply, 4,174 GWh in sales, a 1,074 MW annual peak one-hour demand, and 192,365 customer accounts.

## Transmission and terminal stations

- Peter's Barren Terminal Station was added in 1990. TL221 was terminated there, and its Daniel's Harbour to Peter's Barren section was renumbered TL262, physical PDF pp. 3 to 4.
- The island transmission register identifies TL259 as an 86.625 km, 138 kV H-frame wood line from Berry Hill to Peter's Barren, built in 1990. It identifies TL260 as a 36 km, 138 kV H-frame wood line from Seal Cove to Bottom Waters, built in 1990, physical PDF p. 5.
- A storm on April 25 caused one TL217 conductor span to fail between Western Avalon and Holyrood. The line returned to service within 24 hours, physical PDF p. 3.

## Generation

- Bay d'Espoir Unit 7 received partial-discharge monitoring, physical PDF p. 1.
- Holyrood Unit 1 received replacement breeching. Its boiler was modified to increase capability to 175 MW, physical PDF p. 222.
- The Mobile gas turbine's gas generator received an overhaul or repair, physical PDF p. 37.
- The Mobile hydro turbine runner was replaced. The generating unit was disassembled for this work, physical PDF pp. 484 and 493 to 494.

## Bell Island supply

The BCV-02 supply used four operating submarine cables, two installed in 1988 and two in 1990. One 1990 cable replaced a 1988 cable abandoned because of a factory-splice problem. A three-phase cable installed in 1955 was abandoned after a 1990 fault, physical PDF p. 80.

## Telecommunications

The Great Northern Peninsula inventory records two Deer Lake to Berry Hill powerline-carrier links, TL239A and TL239B, purchased in 1990, physical PDF p. 42.
