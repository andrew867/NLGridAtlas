# Newfoundland Power 1992 system record

## Reliability

CA-446, first revision, physical PDF p. 1, reports 1992 SAIFI of 3.46 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 4.75 hours per customer. Both figures exclude Hydro loss-of-supply outages.

## Capital expenditure

The 2003 rate-hearing report, physical PDF p. 33, records $43.080 million actual capital expenditure against a $47.739 million budget, a 9.76 percent underspend.

## Energy and customers

The 2001 annual report's ten-year summary, physical PDF p. 40, records 4,066 GWh purchased, 417 GWh generated, 4,483 GWh total supply and 4,244 GWh in sales for 1992. The year-end customer count was 198,936.

No standalone 1992 annual return was located in the public mirror. The annual system totals survive in the later annual report.

## Losses

CA-NP-004 Attachment B, physical PDF p. 49, reports 1.87 percent transmission, 3.00 percent distribution and 4.78 percent total energy losses for 1992. Peak losses were 3.18 percent on transmission, 5.18 percent on distribution and 8.10 percent in total.

## Hardwoods

- Hydro's 2001 system-addition history, physical PDF p. 224, records installation of a 125 MVA, 230/66 kV transformer at Hardwoods in 1992.
- The 2012 Oxen Pond transformer report, physical PDF p. 16, identifies this transformer as T4, rated 75/100/125 MVA, and states that a 230 kV bus-tie breaker was added to reduce complete-station outage risk.
- The dated HWD-1 operating drawing on physical PDF p. 189 of the 1991 record already shows breaker B1B2. The public sources do not resolve the difference between the drawing date and the later project history.
- The 2010 Hardwoods life-extension report, physical PDF p. 33, records replacement of both exhaust stacks in 1992. The existing interior silencer panel was reused.

## Bay d'Espoir

- Hydro's system-addition history, physical PDF p. 224, lists completion of Bay d'Espoir 230 kV bus modifications in 1992.
- The Powerhouse 1 generator condition assessment, physical PDF p. 52, records a wedge inspection and stator rewedging on Unit 4. The unit later tripped on a phase A ground fault on a main lead, after which the bus was reinsulated and the unit returned to service.

## 80L

CA-NP-088, physical PDF p. 279, identifies 80L as a 66 kV H-frame circuit between Blaketown and Heart's Content. A 5 km section near Heart's Content was rebuilt in 1992.
