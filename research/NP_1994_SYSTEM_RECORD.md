# Newfoundland Power 1994 system record

## Reliability

CA-446, first revision, physical PDF p. 1, reports 1994 SAIFI of 4.38 interruptions per customer and SAIDI of 19.53 hours per customer. Both figures exclude Hydro loss-of-supply outages.

## Capital expenditure

CA-2, physical PDF p. 3, records $34.773 million actual capital expenditure against $39.724 million approved. Actual expenditure includes $2.378 million for energy supply, $1.372 million for substations, $1.907 million for transmission, $22.527 million for distribution and $0.792 million for telecommunications.

## Energy and customers

The 2002 annual report's ten-year summary, physical PDF p. 51, records 4,178 GWh purchased, 420 GWh generated, 4,598 GWh total supply and 4,371 GWh in sales for 1994. The year-end customer count was 204,900.

## December 8 to 11 storm

The embedded Power System Outage Report in PR-PUB-NLH-003 provides the operating chronology and engineering findings.

- Physical PDF p. 22 records wind gusts to 126 km/h and wet-snow accumulation up to four inches in diameter on some transmission conductors. TL201 and TL217 were the two 230 kV connections between the Holyrood and Avalon grid and the other island generating stations.
- Physical PDF p. 23 records TL201 tripping at 23:04 on December 8 and TL217 tripping at 01:33 on December 9. The TL217 trip separated the Avalon load and Holyrood from the other island generating stations.
- Physical PDF pp. 24 to 25 record two complete Avalon blackouts on December 10. The first followed a Holyrood 230 kV line-breaker flashover at 13:25. The second followed a TL217 conductor break near Western Avalon at 18:09.
- Physical PDF p. 25 records Hardwoods gas turbine synchronized with Newfoundland Power hydro generation at 21:28 on December 10. TL217 returned at 09:19 on December 11, Holyrood Unit 2 synchronized at 12:46 and Unit 3 at 13:35.
- Physical PDF pp. 26 to 27 record seven failed TL201 wooden structures near Western Avalon, a broken TL217 conductor, and repair designs upgraded for the conditions observed during the storm.
- Physical PDF p. 34 gives an estimated $750,000 repair cost for transmission-line and terminal equipment.
- Physical PDF pp. 35 to 38 identify reviews of welded eyebolts, long spans, conductor vibration, 230 kV breakers, Holyrood black-start facilities, station-service transfer, generator governors, Avalon transfer limits and salt contamination.

## Newfoundland Power outage operations

PUB 24.0 NP, physical PDF pp. 31 to 33, reports that the storm caused widespread transmission and distribution damage on the Avalon, Burin and Bonavista peninsulas. The 1994 restoration process used paper trouble-call records, manual sorting and crew dispatch based on the outage team's system knowledge. Kenmount Road's standby generator supplied the whole building; Duffy Place had enough standby capacity for a call centre, some offices and operations areas.

## Follow-up work

The January 1996 joint remedial-action update in PR-PUB-NLH-003 records the following completed or active work.

- Physical PDF p. 53 lists ring-down telephone, dial-up telephone and VHF links in operation between the utility control centres. The power-line-carrier telephone path was in progress.
- Physical PDF p. 56 records new Holyrood station-service control panels, revised black-start procedures and a successful Unit 3 black start during 1995.
- Physical PDF pp. 60 to 62 record the Avalon voltage study, TL217 vibration-damper replacement, salt-contamination controls and a planned Holyrood breaker coating trial.
- Physical PDF p. 63 records November 1995 sectionalizing work on two Northwest Brook feeders and one Sunnyside feeder, a seven-pole reroute at Salvage and completion of the South West Arm crossing in December 1995.
