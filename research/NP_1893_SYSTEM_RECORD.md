# 1893 System Record

## Search result

The 1893 Year Book lists the Electric Light Company among incorporated bodies and gives 1885 as its incorporation year. The Evening Herald of June 2, 1893 offered fifteen shares in the Electric Light Company for sale. These records establish corporate presence, not a change to the electrical system.
