# 1945 System Record

## Lookout Brook Hydro Plant

Several filed Newfoundland Power records date Lookout Brook commissioning or initial construction to 1945. CA-NP-088 describes a 5,800 kW plant with two generating units under 154.6 m net head. The 2017 capital filing describes a 6.15 MW plant, a reinforced-concrete powerhouse, two units, a 1,365 m penstock, and controlled storage at Joe Dennis Pond and Cross Pond.

The 2027 capital filing dates the 13.1 km Flat Bay Brook Road to the plant's initial construction in 1945. The road connects Route 1 to the plant.

A newer 2024 capital filing dates the original plant, G1, G2 and powerhouse to 1946. The commissioning year remains unresolved.

## Port Union standby diesel

Union Electric installed a diesel generator at Port Union in 1945 to back up the hydroelectric units during low reservoir levels. The record says a Caterpillar D398A engine and General Electric 675 kVA generator replaced the original unit in 1965.

Later plant records print 1962 and 1972 dates for the later Port Union diesel asset. The atlas retains the three dates because the records do not reconcile the equipment identity.

Source: [PUB-NP-175 Attachment I](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/IslandInterconnectedSystem/files/rfi/PUB-NP-175.pdf), physical PDF pp. 162 and 176. SHA-256 `8df8884e25756f10d616f7bd3822745adf634ccf5777e210489a7e7f16f08d5a`.
