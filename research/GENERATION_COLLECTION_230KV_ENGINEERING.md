# Generation collection 230 kV engineering record

## TL234

The 2012 generating-station table identifies TL234 as a wood-pole line. The Granite Canal row gives ratings of 248.6 MVA at 30 °C, 376.9 MVA at 15 °C, and 469.6 MVA at 0 °C. The Upper Salmon row prints 469.5 MVA at 0 °C. Both filed values are retained.

The 2013 Wood Pole Line Management report records 1981 as the year in service and identifies 122 poles for the 2012 inspection plan.

PUB-NLH-324 gives a 469.6 MVA rating at 0 °C, a 125.5 MVA anticipated 2014/2015 winter peak, and 79.7 MVA anticipated average loading for 2015. The response identifies TL234 as the connection for Upper Salmon and Granite Canal generation.

The 2016 reliability assessment models TL234 from Upper Salmon to Bay d'Espoir. It gives a 51.538 km route length and a 469.6 MVA winter rating.

## TL263

The 2012 generating-station table identifies TL263 as a hybrid line. Its tangent structures use wood poles with steel crossarms. Heavy-angle and dead-end structures use steel towers. The table gives ratings of 199.3 MVA at 30 °C, 297.7 MVA at 15 °C, and 369.5 MVA at 0 °C.

PUB-NLH-324 gives a 369.5 MVA rating at 0 °C, a 41.9 MVA anticipated 2014/2015 winter peak, and 30.8 MVA anticipated average loading for 2015.

The 2016 reliability assessment predates the Granite Canal Tap split. It models TL263 from Granite Canal to Upper Salmon, with a 74.761 km route length and a 369.5 MVA winter rating. Those values describe the pre-split circuit. They are not assigned to TL270.

An April 2017 operational study states that all alternating-current work for the Maritime Link had been completed. It states that TL263 had been reterminated at Granite Canal Tap Terminal Station and that TL269 from Granite Canal Tap to Bottom Brook Terminal Station 2 was in service.

## TL270

The 2017 General Rate Application describes reconfiguring TL263 at Granite Canal Tap. It assigns TL270 to the remaining section between Granite Canal Tap and Granite Canal Generating Station. The filing footnote states that the Granite Canal Tap assets and reconfiguration were not yet in service in that filing.

PUB-NP-084 Attachment A later lists TL270 as a 230 kV circuit from Granite Canal Tap to Granite Canal. It does not publish a separate TL270 length or rating. The atlas does not transfer the older full-length TL263 values to TL270.
