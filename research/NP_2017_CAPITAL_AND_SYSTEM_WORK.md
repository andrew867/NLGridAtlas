## RBK-01 load-growth work

Rattling Brook had one 12.5 kV distribution feeder serving approximately 775 customers in Norris Arm South. The filed 2017 peak was 174 A per phase on a 6.6 km section of #2 ACSR rated at 168 A for planning. The filing attributes the overload to expansion of the Central Waste Management Facility. It selected replacement with 4/0 AASC, rated at 356 A for planning, because the combined phase load precluded balancing and no adjacent feeder tie was available. Filed cost was $637,000. Source: 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 360 to 364. The route drawing is on physical PDF p. 366.

## Feeder automation

The substation feeder automation program proposed remote control for 17 distribution feeder breakers or station reclosers. Six were part of the Catalina and Salt Pond refurbishments, three at each station. The other 11 were at Islington, Summerford, Northwest Brook, Gillams, Grand Bay and Seal Cove Road. The filing does not identify those 11 feeder designations. Source: 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 239.

## St. John's Main underground system

The 2017 filing describes a Waterford River ductbank carrying the main trunks of nine distribution feeders from St. John's Main toward the downtown core. The ductbank and related underground system were approximately 40 years old. The filed 2017 project cost was $2.440 million. Source: 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF p. 78.

CA-NP-001 reports $4.185 million of actual expenditure for the multi-year project, including $1.546 million carried from 2017 into 2018. Source: CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 10.

## Operational communications

The 2017 program covered mobile, portable, base-station and console radio equipment for field operations. It also covered data links between line devices, substations, hydro plants and the System Control Centre using land-line, fibre-optic and wireless services. CA-NP-001 reports $112,000 of actual expenditure against a $98,000 budget. Sources: 2017 Capital Budget Application, physical PDF pp. 97 to 98; CA-NP-001, physical PDF p. 11.
