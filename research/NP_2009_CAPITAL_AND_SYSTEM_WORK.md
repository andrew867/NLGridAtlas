## 110L and 111L

The filing identifies 110L as a 66 kV, 79 km, single wood-pole line from Clarenville to Lockston. The 2009 work covered 4.9 km near Clarenville at a filed cost of $627,000. Corrosion had reduced the conductor to approximately one-half of its original current-carrying capacity. Source: physical PDF pp. 312 and 318.

The filing identifies 111L as a 66 kV, 31 km, single wood-pole line from Lockston to Catalina through Port Union. The 2009 work covered the remaining 17.7 km after a 13 km section approved for 2008, at a filed cost of $2.285 million. Source: physical PDF pp. 312 to 313 and 319.

The Q3 2009 report states that the 110L and 111L rebuilds were complete. Source: CA-NP-217, physical PDF p. 505.

## NWB-02

NWB-02 was a 25 kV feeder serving approximately 1,050 customers around Southwest Arm. The filing records 190 interruption reports and 2,151,561 customer-minutes from 2002 to 2006. Conductor, insulator, or fuse events accounted for 57% of outages. Source: physical PDF pp. 343 to 348.

The three-year filed program totalled $1.463 million, with $496,000 in 2009, $447,000 in 2010, and $520,000 in 2011. The work included roadside relocation, replacement of deteriorated poles and insulators, severe-weather construction, cutout and arrester work, and feeder coordination. The filed route map is on physical PDF p. 350.

## Rose Blanche

The filed project used a 1.16 m ogee concrete extension on the existing 40 m spillway. The estimated cost was $464,800. The filing estimated a 0.9 GWh annual increase, from 20.7 GWh to 21.6 GWh. Source: physical PDF pp. 220 to 222.

The Q3 2009 report states that the spillway had been raised. The 2009 Annual Report states that the work increased plant energy output. Sources: CA-NP-217, physical PDF pp. 505 and 611.

## Rocky Pond

The filed work included replacement of the 1942 woodstave penstock, intake gate, gate guides, main inlet valve, and drain valve; addition of a bypass valve; generator rewind; digital governor controls; and replacement of the forebay distribution and copper communications line. The filed 2009 cost was $6.517 million, plus $75,000 of 2008 engineering work. Source: physical PDF pp. 161 to 170.

The Q4 2009 report states that the governor, generator, penstock, and substation refurbishment was complete. Source: CA-NP-217, physical PDF p. 553.

## Broad Cove load transfer

The filing proposed converting a Broad Cove feeder section from 12.5 kV to 25 kV and transferring load to a Hardwoods feeder. The $244,000 project was intended to keep BCV-T1 within its loading limits and defer transformer replacement. Source: physical PDF pp. 60 to 61.

No project-specific completion statement was located in the reviewed quarterly reports or PUB-NP-005. The atlas keeps this item as proposed.
