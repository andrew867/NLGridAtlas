## Completion boundary

The 2015 capital application describes the proposed Colliers and Springfield breaker, protection, control-building, SCADA, transformer-controller, incoming-breaker, and grounding work at physical PDF pp. 435 to 439. The later PUB-NP-005 response explicitly lists Springfield among the substations whose planned refurbishment was completed. No equivalent direct Colliers completion statement was located in this research pass. The current circuit register establishes the line endpoints, but does not establish the present breaker positions inside Colliers.
