## Holyrood Stage I date conflict

The published records do not give one consistent commissioning sequence:

- The 2022 Unit 1 root-cause investigation dates Unit 1 commissioning to 1969. It gives the original output as 150 MW and the 1988 uprated output as 175 MW.
- IC-NLH-150 dates Unit 1 to late 1969 and Unit 2 to early 1970. It gives an original rating of 150 MW for each unit.
- A 2014 capital record dates Unit 1 to 1970 and Unit 2 to 1971.
- A 2011 capital record states that the complete Stage I, Units 1 and 2, came on line in 1971.

The atlas therefore marks the commissioning sequence unresolved and retains each filed date.

## Holyrood 1969 equipment and facilities

- UST-1 and UST-2, 10 MVA each, star-delta, resistance grounded, ONAN, 16 kV to 4160/2400 V, primary tap changer +2/-2 at 2.5%.
- SST-12, 10.5/14 MVA, star-delta, resistance grounded, ONAN/ONAF, 69 kV to 4160/2400 V, primary tap changer +2/-2 at 2.5%.

## Stations and transmission

The 2025 Summerville filing states that the distribution substation was constructed in 1969. It is supplied by 66 kV circuit 113L between Princeton Pond and Lethbridge.

- Source: `http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2025/NP2025Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202025%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202024-06-28.PDF`
- Physical PDF page: 390

The 2026 capital filing describes 94L as the 66 kV H-frame radial supply from Blaketown to Riverhead and dates it to 1969. The same filing dates 95L from Riverhead to Trepassey to 1969.

- Source: `http://www.pub.nf.ca/applications/2026/NP2026Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202026%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202025-06-27.PDF`
- Physical PDF pages: 192 and 477

IC-NLH-097 Revision 1 records 1969 as TL215's year built. It also assigns 1969 dates to Riverhead and Trepassey transformers and terminal equipment, and estimates Stony Brook in service in 1969. The same register explicitly leaves the Riverhead and Trepassey station in-service dates and TL213 year built unknown. Asset dates are not substituted for those unknown dates.
