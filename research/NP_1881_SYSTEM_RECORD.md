# 1881 System Record

## Railway communications and electric-light equipment

The Newfoundland Railway Act passed on May 9, 1881. Section 18 admitted construction and maintenance materials for railway telegraph and telephone lines free of duty. The listed materials included wire, instruments, fixtures and supplies. The same list included dynamo machines and fixtures for electric lights.

The act establishes an authorized procurement category. It does not identify a route, line voltage, dynamo rating, station installation or 1881 commissioning date for any telegraph, telephone or electric-light equipment.
