## Unidentified utility plant placements

- $37,500 in account 323.00, Canals, Penstocks, Surge Tanks and Tailraces. The ledger records a $4,000 retirement in 1966. The 2010 survivor table retains $33,500 of original cost for the 1913 vintage.
