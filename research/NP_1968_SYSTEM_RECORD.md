## Newfoundland Power transmission

The 2023 Capital Budget Application states that the 66 kV 55L corridor was constructed in 1971 except for a 1.0 km section built in 1968.

- Source: `http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2023/NP2023Capital/app/From%20NP%20-%202023%20Capital%20Budget%20Application%20-%202022-06-29.PDF`
- Physical PDF pages: 440 to 441
- SHA-256: `be0143cf9814e8573dcb5cdcdfa7fcd213190a5274902d0c5d60becbbf65dd73`

The filed 404L record is inconsistent:

- The 2015 filing says 404L was constructed in 1967, was 1.2 km long, and had 14 single-pole structures.
- The 2016 filing says 404L was constructed in 1968, was 2.0 km long, and had 14 single-pole structures.
- Both filings place it between Wheelers and the tap on 401L.
