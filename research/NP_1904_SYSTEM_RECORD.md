# 1904 System Record

## Victoria hydroelectric development

Newfoundland Power's detailed development record dates the Victoria hydroelectric development to 1904. It describes a rock-masonry powerhouse, one generating unit, Blue Hill Pond forebay dam and intake, Rocky Pond storage reservoir, and a 494 m mixed woodstave and steel penstock.

The record gives 500 kW total development capacity under 64.3 m net head. It separately identifies the present machine as a 450 kW Voith turbine installed in 1914 with a Westinghouse generator. The source says the original turbine-generators are retained as museum exhibits, but it does not give their ratings. The present unit must not be treated as the original 1904 machine.

Source: [CA-NP-036, Victoria Hydroelectric Development Record](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/2013/NP2013GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-036.pdf), physical PDF p. 180. SHA-256 `1b27d6b0a58416c06045f8e54618c33b2902f26dda83d53780a4b01b5e14b477`.

## Capacity and penstock records

A 2008 Newfoundland Power operating summary also dates Victoria to 1904. It lists one unit, 0.55 MW capacity and 3.0 GWh average output. The 0.55 MW value differs from the 500 kW total in the detailed development sheet. Both dated values are retained.

Source: [CA-NP-263, Newfoundland Power Description of Operations](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/ARCHIVE/NP2008GRA/files/rfi/CA-NP-263.pdf), physical PDF p. 95. SHA-256 `c1bb6fbdaa468ff1e28393c485f9e31deae14adfe782aa4df41f45dea62fd3db`.

The 2020 woodstave register independently gives 1904 as the original construction year for the Victoria penstock. It records 412 m of woodstave pipe, with 238 m replaced in 1959 and 174 m in 1963, plus 82 m of steel installed in 1986.

Source: [NLH-NP-017, Woodstave Penstock Register](http://www.pub.nl.ca/applications/2020/NP2020Capital/rfi/NLH-NP-017.PDF), physical PDF p. 1. SHA-256 `4adb017b3e4308d57d981686482f7169494fd4b69a1be02d3b1624a75c24ad39`.

## Depreciation search

The depreciation studies use placement bands beginning in 1904 for accounts 322, Buildings and Structures, and 324, Dams and Reservoirs. Those life tables do not identify a named 1904 asset placement. No additional asset was assigned from the placement-band boundary.

## Search closeout

No separate named 1904 Newfoundland transmission line, substation, transformer, telecommunications, load, reliability, storm or outage event was located. Other exact-year results were out-of-province generation records, legal citations, numerical values, page numbers and clock times.
