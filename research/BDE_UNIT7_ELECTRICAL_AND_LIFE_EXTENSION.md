# Bay d'Espoir Unit 7 electrical record

Updated: 2026-11-13

## Unit electrical equipment

The August 2023 inspection checklist records the Unit 7 generator as a GE unit installed in 1977. Its filed values are 172 MVA, 13.8 kV, 7,196 A, 60 Hz, 0.9 power factor, 225 rpm rated speed and 380 rpm runaway speed. The checklist is reproduced at physical PDF p. 211 of the life-extension application.

The ABB static excitation system was commissioned in 2004. The checklist records 230 V and 1,457 A output, AVR and VAR control modes, digital controls, a power-system stabilizer, and DC field breaking. Its oil-filled ABB excitation transformer is rated 1,175 kVA, 13.8/7.97 kV to 559 V, with 5.64% impedance. The transformer was replaced in 2014. These values are on physical PDF pp. 218 to 219.

The GE neutral-grounding cubicle was commissioned in 1992. It is metal enclosed and uses transformer and resistor grounding. The filed voltage values are 18 kV rated and 24.94 kV maximum. The checklist is on physical PDF p. 221.

## Approved work

Order P.U. 12(2026) approved capital expenditures of up to $66,265,000 on April 21, 2026. The original $85,346,227 request was not approved. The order requires Hydro to report at critical project milestones. The approved amount and date are on physical PDF pp. 3 to 4.

## Evidence boundary

The inspection checklists establish Unit 7 equipment data. They do not provide a current Bay d'Espoir station-bus one-line or breaker arrangement.
