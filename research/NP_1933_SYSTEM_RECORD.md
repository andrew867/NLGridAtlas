# 1933 System Search

## Unassigned property vintages

Newfoundland Power's depreciation records contain two exact-year property vintages from 1933:

- Account 323.00, Canals, Penstocks, Surge Tanks, and Tailraces, retains $1,270 of original cost.
- The combined wire, duplex, and services account begins with a $6,049 addition and balance.

The filed schedules do not identify a generating plant, water-control structure, circuit, community or work order for either vintage. Later depreciation testimony also confirms that early placement bands include plant additions that no longer existed on Newfoundland Power's system by the study date.

## Archive result

Exact-year searches covered generation, turbines, generators, stations, transformers, hydro civil works, property records, transmission lines, circuits, feeders, telecommunications, system demand, reliability, storms and outages.

The other returned records were United States securities-law references, depreciation placement bands, market series, aggregate accounting transactions, hydrology series, modern operating times, engineering calculations containing the number 1933, and external utility records. No named Newfoundland or Labrador grid event can be assigned specifically to 1933.

The atlas therefore has no 1933 public card or timeline entry.

## Search rule

Neither vintage can be attached to an asset without a plant ledger, work order or other source that identifies its location. A later primary record can reopen this queue if it supplies that link.
