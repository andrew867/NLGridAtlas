# 1879 System Record

## Telegraph appropriation

The 1879 Supply Act appropriated $7,000 to pay interest and aid maintenance of new telegraph lines.

The act does not divide the appropriation between interest and maintenance. It gives no route, endpoint, line length, conductor, office equipment or construction date.
