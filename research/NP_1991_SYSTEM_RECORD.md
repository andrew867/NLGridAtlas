# Newfoundland Power 1991 system record

## Reliability and capital

CA-NP-004 Attachment A, physical PDF p. 4, reports 1991 SAIFI of 2.09 interruptions per customer, SAIDI of 3.26 hours per customer, and capital expenditure of $43.322 million. The five-year rolling averages were 2.29 interruptions and 3.56 hours per customer.

## Energy, demand, and customers

CA-213C, physical PDF p. 29, records 4,008 GWh purchased, 417 GWh generated, 4,425 GWh total supply, 4,196 GWh in sales, a 1,101 MW annual peak one-hour demand, and 195,730 customer accounts.

## Total Island forecast and actual

Hydro's historical load table, physical PDF p. 235, records a 1991 filed Total Island Interconnected System peak of 1,480 MW and an actual peak of 1,488 MW. Filed energy was 7,547 GWh and actual energy was 7,464 GWh.

## Transmission and terminal stations

- Exhibit 85, physical PDF p. 54, identifies TL217 as the 76 km, 795 kcmil ACSR 26/7 line between Western Avalon and Holyrood. In 1991, the portion over Hawke Hill and near Brigus Junction was upgraded for 63 mm glaze ice by adding mid-span structures and dead-end towers.
- Drawing HWD-1 revision 17, physical PDF p. 189, is dated July 7, 1991. It shows the Hardwoods B1 and B2 230 kV sections, B7 and B8 66 kV sections, T1 through T5, G1, C1, and the B1B2 and B7B8 ties. Later project histories date the 230 kV bus-tie work to 1992; both records remain separately identified.

## Generation

- Bay d'Espoir Unit 3 was re-wedged with piggyback stator wedges, physical PDF p. 52.
- Bay d'Espoir Unit 7's synchronous-condenser blower was replaced, physical PDF p. 1.
- Pierre's Brook received a welded-steel differential surge tank. The former foundations were reused and a new anchor block and tee were installed at the base, physical PDF p. 298.
- West Brook received a battery bank and charger, physical PDF p. 182.
- Holyrood Unit 2 received a replacement breeching and a boiler modification that increased capability to 175 MW, physical PDF p. 245.
- Holyrood Units 1 and 2 received replacement forced-draft motors, physical PDF p. 735.
- The Holyrood 15 MW gas turbine received a major overhaul, physical PDF p. 19.

## Telecommunications

NP-117, physical PDF p. 1, states that Hydro purchased its VHF system in 1989 and that manufacturer support ended in 1991. The response gave an expected system life of 10 to 12 years.
