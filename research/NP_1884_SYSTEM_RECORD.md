# 1884 System Record

## St. John's private telephone line

The *Encyclopedia of Newfoundland and Labrador* records a telephone line between Archibald's Furniture Store, at Duckworth Street and St. John's Lane, and the manager's residence on Devon Row. It describes the 1885 Anglo-American Telegraph exchange as Newfoundland's first public telephone system, so the 1884 installation was a private point-to-point line.

## Store attribution conflict

*When Was That?* also records two telephones installed in 1884 between a furniture store and its manager's Devon Row residence. It identifies the store as Archibald's Furniture Factory, then locates it by the later T. and M. Winter premises. The encyclopedia instead gives the Duckworth Street and St. John's Lane junction. The two references may describe the same installation, but they do not establish the same business description.
