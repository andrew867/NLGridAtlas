# Health Sciences Centre construction photographs

Memorial University's Faculty of Medicine Founders' Archive preserves construction photographs taken by John O'Reilly. The catalogue states that reproduction requires written permission. The atlas cites the records and does not copy the image files.

The photographs do not establish which contemporary utility feeder supplied each internal load. The 2024 Memorial single-line separately identifies MUN-01 and MUN-08 as Health Sciences Complex feeders.
