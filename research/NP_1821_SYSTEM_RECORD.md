# 1821 System Record

## Lively packet communication

The November 29, 1821 *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* advertises the new packet boat *Lively* between Portugal Cove and Harbour Grace on physical PDF p. 3. The boat was to sail twice a week. Its new craft was arranged for six oars, and six men could be provided when required.

Fares were eight shillings for ladies and gentlemen, five shillings for tradesmen and four shillings for labourers. Hire of the boat cost 30 shillings, and letters cost nine pence each. Letters and packages could be left at Mr. Lee's printing office in St. John's or with proprietor Francis Neary at Portugal Cove.

The later notice dated November 13, 1822 reduced the listed fare for ladies and gentlemen to five shillings and named James Neary as proprietor. The sources do not explain either change.

## Electric-eel report

The same issue prints an article titled "Electric Eel" on physical PDF p. 4. It describes a live electric eel brought from Surinam to the Jardin des Plantes. The article calls the animal a "living electrical machine" and reports that a touch produced a shock. It describes repeated shocks causing contraction of the hand and arm, the shocks stopping when the animal was exhausted, and their return after rest.

This is electrical context printed in Newfoundland. The reported observation did not occur in Newfoundland and does not document installed electrical apparatus.

## Dated and geographic boundaries

An October article titled "Effects of Lightning" concerns an event near Biberach in Prussia. A January fire-engine report concerns Saint John, New Brunswick. The reviewed lighthouse references describe Genoa and Harwich. None is a Newfoundland event or installation.

The reviewed files did not provide a supported 1821 Newfoundland lighthouse project, electric telegraph, installed electrical circuit, operating electrical apparatus, pilotage system or local lightning-damage event.

## Newspaper search

The review covered all 43 surviving 1821 files listed in the MUN DAI *Newfoundland Mercantile Journal* collection, comprising 172 physical PDF pages. The files run from January 4 to December 27. The online collection has no issue for April 26, May 3, May 10, September 6, September 13, September 20, September 27, October 25 or November 15.

The MUN DAI *Royal Gazette* collection has no digitized 1821 issue. Its online run ends in April 1818 and resumes in January 1828. The MUN DAI main newspaper list and limited-run newspaper list identify no other digitized collection covering 1821. These collection gaps do not establish that no other 1821 newspaper existed or survives elsewhere.
