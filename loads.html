Transmission damage and two blackouts

December 8, 23:04
TL201 tripped; seven structures failed near Western Avalon
December 9, 01:33
TL217 tripped; Avalon and Holyrood separated from the island grid
December 10, 13:25
Holyrood 230 kV breaker flashover; first complete Avalon blackout
December 10, 18:09
TL217 conductor break; second complete Avalon blackout
December 10, 21:28
Hardwoods gas turbine synchronized with Newfoundland Power hydro generation
December 11, 13:35
Holyrood Units 2 and 3 were both back on the system

Winds gusted to 126 km/h and wet snow accumulated to four inches in diameter on some transmission conductors. TL201 repairs took 14 days. The damaged line sections were rebuilt for the loading observed during the storm.
