FILED IN 2004

Keep the 170 MHz band

Hydro proposed a standards-based trunked VHF system. The budget included six additional repeaters. The filing said 170 MHz gave the required coverage with the fewest sites.

Physical PDF pp. 200 to 203
REVISION / SEPTEMBER 24, 2004

Ten additional sites

The revised summary specified 39 repeater sites, ten more than the existing 29. The added coverage included Granite Canal, the Bay d'Espoir Highway, southern Labrador, Happy Valley-Goose Bay, and the Burin Peninsula.

Revised summary / PDF p. 1, report p. 11
2024 TO 2026 PROJECT

The replacement plan changed

Hydro cancelled the proposed satellite-phone and cellular scope. It selected radios on the provincial P25 system and Starlink with Cradlepoint routers for selected fleet vehicles.
